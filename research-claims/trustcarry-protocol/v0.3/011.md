**Sealed record identifier.** `kaal:research-claim:persona-protocol-v0.3:011`

**Author-affirmed research claim.** A reputation history can substitute for a bond only if it attaches to a subject that cannot costlessly shed it, is interpretable in the present domain, and is contestable and inspectable.

**Status.** Public research record. Not yet eligible for the published-source scholarly claim layer.

**Protocol.** TrustCarry Protocol v0.3, historically titled and aliased as Persona Protocol v0.3.

**Supporting passage from Persona Protocol v0.3.**

> A reputation history is the missing safeguard. It substitutes for the bond by making adverse conduct costly to the actor rather than only to the counterparty. But a reputation history performs that function only under three conditions. The history must attach to an accountable subject that cannot costlessly shed it. The history must be interpretable in the domain of the present transaction. The history must be contestable by the subject and inspectable by the relying party.

Graph adjacency does not prove competence transfer, local stages do not create universal rank, and cross-system comparison requires a declared, fallible crosswalk.

Horizontal and vertical development are graph views, not automatic promotions. Adjacency between fields does not prove transfer. A stage inside one admission system does not become a universal rank. Cross-system comparison requires a declared crosswalk, and a crosswalk is a claim that can be wrong.

Persona Protocol therefore represents reputation as a contextual projection over provenance-bearing evidence rather than as a transferable scalar. It preserves adverse, withheld, challenged, revoked, and superseded events. It permits selective principal disclosure. It treats authority as an explicit, scoped, revocable grant that fails closed.

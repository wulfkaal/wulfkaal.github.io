**Sealed record identifier.** `kaal:research-claim:persona-protocol-v0.3:010`

**Author-affirmed research claim.** Cross-organizational autonomous-agent transactions face screening, verification, and credible-commitment costs that can force counterparties to absorb risk, reject external agents, or require human guarantors.

**Status.** Public research record. Not yet eligible for the published-source scholarly claim layer.

**Protocol.** TrustCarry Protocol v0.3, historically titled and aliased as Persona Protocol v0.3.

**Supporting passage from Persona Protocol v0.3.**

> Applied to autonomous systems, the framework yields a specific diagnosis. An autonomous agent transacting across an organizational boundary faces high ex ante screening costs, high ex post verification costs, and almost no credible commitment technology. It cannot post a bond. It cannot be sued in its own name. It cannot be reliably excluded from re-entry. Its counterparty therefore either absorbs the risk, collapses the relationship into a hierarchy by refusing all external agents, or demands a human guarantor and thereby forfeits the coordination gain the agent was supposed to deliver.

Reputation governance must be revisable on the timescale of exploitation and able to ingest evidence of its own gaming.

Supporting passage
Governance must also be revisable on the timescale of the exploit rather than the timescale of the standards cycle. The Folk Theorem result forecloses a static rule set. The author's dynamic-regulation framework supplies the alternative: rules are adaptable only where "the institutions and rulemaking processes that produce them integrate dynamic elements that generate timely, relevant, and decentralized information for rulemaking" (Kaal 2013, claim 2267560-012). A reputation protocol whose governance cannot ingest evidence of its own gaming is a reputation protocol with a fixed expiry date.

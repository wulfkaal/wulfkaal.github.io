**Author-affirmed research claim.** Authority grants must identify issuer, subject, action, resource, constraints, time, delegation depth, revocation, policy, and proof; child grants may preserve or narrow but never enlarge scope.

**Status.** Public research record. Not yet eligible for the published-source scholarly claim layer.

**Supporting passage from Persona Protocol v0.3.**

> An authority grant identifies the issuer, the subject Persona, the action, the resource, the constraints, the validity period, the delegation depth, the revocation status, the governing policy, and the proof. A child grant may narrow or preserve scope. It may not enlarge amount, duration, action, resource, disclosure rights, or delegation depth.

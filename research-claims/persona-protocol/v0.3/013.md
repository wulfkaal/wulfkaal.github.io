# kaal:research-claim:persona-protocol-v0.3:013

**Author-affirmed research claim.** Eliminating psychological guile does not eliminate objective misalignment: a faithfully optimizing agent can impose counterparty losses without deception, and verifiability alone does not determine whether to transact.

**Status.** Public research record. Not yet eligible for the published-source scholarly claim layer.

**Protocol.** TrustCarry Protocol v0.3, historically titled and aliased as Persona Protocol v0.3.

**Supporting passage from Persona Protocol v0.3.**

> Second, the elimination of psychological guile does not eliminate misalignment. An agent with a faithfully executed objective function can still impose losses on a counterparty whose objective differs. Opportunism in the Williamsonian sense is self-interest seeking with guile; an agent optimizing a specified objective against a counterparty's interest produces the same institutional harm without the guile. Verifiability tells a relying party what the agent did. It does not tell the relying party whether to transact.

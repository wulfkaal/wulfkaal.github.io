An authority grant identifies the issuer, the subject Persona, the action, the resource, the constraints, the validity period, the delegation depth, the revocation status, the governing policy, and the proof. A child grant may narrow or preserve scope. It may not enlarge amount, duration, action, resource, disclosure rights, or delegation depth.

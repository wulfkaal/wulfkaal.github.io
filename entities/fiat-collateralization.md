- [3402701-002](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/3402701-002) [mechanism/argued] *(failure mode)* -- Fiat currency collateralization is expensive and inefficient because the entire backing value must be held liquid; anything less opens arbitrage attacks of the kind Soros used against the Bank of England.

  Craig Calcaterra, Wulf A. Kaal, Vadhindran K. Rao, Stable Cryptocurrencies - First Order Principles (2019). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=3402701

  > Therefore, the price tag of fiat-backed tokens is, at a minimum, the interest rate of the pegged fiat currency
  Craig Calcaterra, Wulf A. Kaal, Vadhindran K. Rao, Stable Cryptocurrencies - First Order Principles (2019). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=3402701

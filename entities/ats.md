  > Hence, capital-raising opportunities for private companies likely would not be materially increased despite the validation of blockchain technology as a means of cost-effectively supporting securities offerings.
  Wulf A. Kaal, Samuel Evans, Blockchain-Based Securities Offerings (2019). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=3411110

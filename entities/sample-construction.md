  Wulf A. Kaal, The Impact of Dodd-Frank Act Compliance Cost on the Hedge Fund Industry (2014). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=2389423

**2015**

**2016**

  > of purely unconstrained mutual funds (i.e., those which were the focus of this study) (N=84) included only those UMFs that scored a "9" or better out of a total of 114 funds identified in the Morningstar Nontraditional Bond index.
  Wulf A. Kaal, Unconstrained Mutual Funds and Retail Investor Protection (2016). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=2811729

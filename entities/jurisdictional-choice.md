- [2831040-037](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/2831040-037) [mechanism/argued] -- Consumer choice adds a dynamic element to rulemaking because once consumers opt out of a suboptimal regulatory regime, public rulemakers in that jurisdiction can adjust their rulemaking in response, creating a feedback effect for the public rulemaker.

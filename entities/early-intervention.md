- [2831040-022](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/2831040-022) [failure/argued] *(failure mode)* -- Despite the consensus favoring it, early regulatory intervention in innovation is rare in practice, and regulation mostly fails to keep pace as the innovation evolves and as understanding and use of the technology spreads.

  Wulf A. Kaal, Dynamic Regulation for Innovation (2016). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=2831040
- [2831040-024](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/2831040-024) [failure/argued] *(failure mode)* -- In the later stages of more matured innovation it is often not possible to alter the regulatory status quo, which closes the window that early intervention proposals depend on.

  Wulf A. Kaal, Dynamic Regulation for Innovation (2016). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=2831040
- [2831040-025](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/2831040-025) [mechanism/argued] -- Early regulatory intervention becomes unnecessary in a dynamic regulatory framework, because the regulatory challenges associated with innovation would become transparent in real time through improved, decentralized information and feedback effects.
  > Because in a dynamic regulatory framework, the regulatory challenges associated with innovation would become transparent in real- time, early regulatory intervention would not be necessary.
  Wulf A. Kaal, Dynamic Regulation for Innovation (2016). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=2831040

  Wulf A. Kaal, Quantum Economy and the Future of Work (2024). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=4900880
- [4900880-031](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/4900880-031) [predictive/argued] -- Quantum computing will displace job roles built on repetitive and manual tasks, but that displacement is offset by new roles in quantum programming, algorithm design, and quantum cryptography that did not previously exist.

- [4900880-032](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/4900880-032) [mechanism/argued] -- As technological progress accelerates, the half life of skills continues to shorten, which makes continuous adaptation and learning a condition for career longevity rather than an optional professional habit.

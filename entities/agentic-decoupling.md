**2026**

- [6421319-003](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/6421319-003) [definitional/asserted] -- Agentic Decoupling, defined as the progressive severance of value creation from human labor and consumption, is what distinguishes the AI transition from all prior technological transitions.
  > What distinguishes this moment from all prior technological transitions is what I have termed Agentic Decoupling: the progressive severance of value creation from human labor and consumption.
  Wulf A. Kaal, The Collapse of Scarcity Economics (2026). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=6421319
- [6421319-005](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/6421319-005) [condition/argued] *(failure mode)* -- Humans remain the terminal consumers of AI generated value but cease to be its proximate driver, and this distinction is why traditional demand side economics loses descriptive accuracy in the agentic layer.
  > This distinction between terminal and proximate consumption is essential for understanding why traditional demand-side economics, which assumes that human consumption decisions directly drive production decisions at each margin, loses its descriptive accuracy in the agentic layer.
  Wulf A. Kaal, The Collapse of Scarcity Economics (2026). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=6421319
- [6607458-008](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/6607458-008) [definitional/asserted] -- Agentic decoupling is the structural severance of value creation from human labor and consumption at the production-function level; the claim is not that human consumption disappears but that it ceases to be the proximate driver of the dominant mode of production in affected domains.
  > The claim is not that human consumption disappears. It is that human consumption ceases to be the proximate driver of the dominant mode of production in the affected domain.
  Wulf A. Kaal, Computative Economics A Framework for Economic Analysis under Computational Abundance (2026). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=6607458

- [3067615-003](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/3067615-003) [definitional/argued] -- ICOs cannot be qualified as donations and are therefore distinguishable from crowdfunding, because ICO participants acquire a financial stake in the company and, as the case may be, a right to vote on future decisions.

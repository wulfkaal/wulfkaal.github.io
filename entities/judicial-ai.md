  > For example, a study of an AI system for traffic violation appeals in a Dutch court found that while AI improved consistency, it altered legal experts' decisions, highlighting the need for human oversight.
  Wulf A. Kaal, Morgan A. Gray, The Evolving Role of Artificial Intelligence in Law (2025). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=5541658

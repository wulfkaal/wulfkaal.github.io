  > The physical location of assets becomes less important when more content is digitized and online and virtual products emerge. As a result, markets are becoming increasingly decentralized.
  Wulf A. Kaal, Decentralization and Feedback Effects (2021). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=3808852
- [3808852-023](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/3808852-023) [definitional/argued] -- Decentralized cryptocurrency exchanges epitomize market decentralization because they let users interact and trade anonymously in a secure environment without third party intermediation.
  > DEX epitomize market decentralization because they allow users to interact and trade anonymously in a secure environment without the need for third party intermediation.
  Wulf A. Kaal, Decentralization and Feedback Effects (2021). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=3808852

- [3017612-011](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/3017612-011) [condition/evidenced] -- The Model Act conditions the arrangement on notice to the market for shares by requiring a legend on the stock certificates that flags the agreement to purchasers of covered shares.
  > Finally, the Model Act language requires that a legend appear on the stock certificates flagging the agreement for purchasers who buy shares covered by such an agreement.
  Wulf A. Kaal, Shareholder Agreements - National Report of the United States of America (2017). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=3017612
- [3017612-018](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/3017612-018) [condition/evidenced] -- Under the MBCA a purchaser of shares who bought without knowledge of an existing shareholder control agreement may obtain rescission of the purchase.
  > Under the MBCA, rescission may also be available to a purchaser of shares who did not have knowledge of the existence of a shareholder control agreement at the time of purchase.
  Wulf A. Kaal, Shareholder Agreements - National Report of the United States of America (2017). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=3017612

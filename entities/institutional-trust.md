  Wulf A. Kaal, The Future of Law - Dynamic Web3 Governance (2024). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=4957318
- [4957318-029](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/4957318-029) [design/argued] -- Web3 systems answer the web2 pathology at the architectural level rather than the content level: decentralized, transparent, community governed designs eliminate the centralized control that drives misinformation and echo chambers, and substitute transparency, decentralization, and user control.
  > Unlike Web2 platforms, Web3 systems aim to eliminate the centralized control that exacerbates the spread of misinformation and the creation of echo chambers. Instead, they prioritize transparency, decentralization, and user control,
  Wulf A. Kaal, The Future of Law - Dynamic Web3 Governance (2024). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=4957318

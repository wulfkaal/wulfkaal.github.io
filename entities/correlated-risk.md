- [1998455-003](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/1998455-003) [mechanism/argued] *(failure mode)* -- Because governments prioritize the rescue of systemically important financial institutions over other entities, those institutions are incentivized to adopt similar risk profiles and to correlate their risks.

  Wulf A. Kaal, Initial Reflections on the Possible Application of Contingent Capital in Corporate Governance (2012). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=1998455
- [1998455-037](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/1998455-037) [failure/argued] *(failure mode)* -- Combining the existing prioritization of bailouts for systemically important institutions with central bank purchases of their contingent capital in a given jurisdiction would further incentivize those institutions to adopt similar risk profiles and correlate their risks.

**2019**

- [3405660-006](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/3405660-006) [failure/evidenced] *(failure mode)* -- LTCM was diversified across markets but not across strategy, so its positions failed together; market level diversification does not imply strategy level diversification.
  > It became obvious that while the fund was diversified in terms of markets, its overall strategy was not diversified.
  Kaal, Indirect Regulation of Hedge Funds (2019). SSRN: https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3405660

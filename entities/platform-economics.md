  > Removing such centralized fees also allows for the removal of downward pressure on the platforms' worker compensation as costs are less likely to increase and be passed on to workers and consumers.

**2026**

- [6607458-038](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/6607458-038) [definitional/argued] -- Computative Economics is not a generalization of digital platform economics, because platform economics extends Neoclassical Economics while remaining within its primitives, whereas the computative primitives differ and the equilibrium concept is recursive rather than competitive.

  Wulf A. Kaal, Computative Economics A Framework for Economic Analysis under Computational Abundance (2026). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=6607458

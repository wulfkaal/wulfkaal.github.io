## Every claim under this term

6 claims across 4 works, 2019 to 2021.

**2019**

- [3406323-011](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/3406323-011) [mechanism/argued] -- Personal data, preferences, and opinions can only be limitedly commercialized inside centralized structures, whereas in decentralized structures the same assets can be tokenized, valued, and mobilized, including in real time.

  Wulf A. Kaal, Decentralization - A Primer on the New Economy (2019). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=3406323

**2020**

- [3709041-012](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/3709041-012) [design/argued] -- Redistribution in decentralism is grounded in ownership rights in each person's individuality derived data that creates economic value, rather than in taxation or market determined income.

  Kaal, Blockchain Technology for Good (2020). SSRN: https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3709041

**2021**

- [3782198-012](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/3782198-012) [mechanism/argued] -- Web 2.0 companies solved the hosting problem through a Faustian bargain: free hosting is exchanged for access to shared content, ownership of viewers' personal information, control over what may be shared, and access to user attention.

  Wulf A. Kaal, How Decentralized Systems Can Upgrade AI (2021). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=3808867
- [3808867-011](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/3808867-011) [mechanism/argued] -- Personal assets such as one's data, preferences, and opinions can only be commercialized to a limited degree in existing centralized structures, whereas decentralized structures allow those same assets to be tokenized, valued, and mobilized.

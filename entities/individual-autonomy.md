- [3782201-004](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/3782201-004) [mechanism/argued] -- Self policing is more effective when the members themselves hold the power, because a centralized hierarchy in which each member holds distinct powers and responsibilities is more prone to structural corruption.
  > The power of decentralization is individual autonomy. Self-policing their own members is more effective when the members have the power, unlike a centralized hierarchy which is more prone to structural corruption when each member holds distinct powers and responsibilities.
  Craig Calcaterra, Wulf A. Kaal, Future of Decentralization (2021). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=3782201
- [3782201-019](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/3782201-019) [design/argued] -- The correct goal is not to eliminate the middlemen but to automate them, because unlike human middlemen automated middlemen can be designed so that they do not contribute to the corruption they were built to fight.
  > Automating these middlemen functions makes them more efficient, like the chemical catalysts in a cell. Unlike human middlemen, automated middlemen can be designed so that they don't contribute to the corruption they were designed to fight.
  Craig Calcaterra, Wulf A. Kaal, Future of Decentralization (2021). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=3782201

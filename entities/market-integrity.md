**2012**

- [1998455-035](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/1998455-035) [empirical/argued] -- Treating the price of contingent capital securities as an indicator of how much people care about market integrity and moral hazard would require altruistic motives on the part of purchasers, and the author doubts this: the nascent market appears to have been built on investors' expectation of above average returns.
  > This would imply, however, altruistic motives of CCS purchasers. It is unclear if altruism in CCS purchases would be realistic. The nascent market in CCS seems to have been built on investors' expectation of above- average returns.

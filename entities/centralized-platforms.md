  Wulf A. Kaal, Decentralized Mechanical Turk Through Verified Reputation (2018). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=3128900

  Wulf A. Kaal, Decentralized Mechanical Turk Through Verified Reputation (2018). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=3128900
- [3128900-011](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/3128900-011) [empirical/evidenced] -- Roughly 38 percent of the labor pool is unbanked but skilled, and this group has no access to centralized micro task marketplaces.

  Wulf A. Kaal, Decentralized Mechanical Turk Through Verified Reputation (2018). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=3128900
- [3128900-012](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/3128900-012) [condition/argued] *(failure mode)* -- A bank account is a necessary condition for participation in the existing centralized micro task marketplace: without one, a worker can neither contribute nor profit.

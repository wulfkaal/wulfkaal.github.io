  Kaal and Oesterle, The History of Hedge Fund Regulation in the United States (2016). SSRN: https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2714974

  > Hedge funds' emergence as short sellers can be viewed as positive, eliminating some of the market overpricing due to the high costs of short selling.
  Kaal and Oesterle, The History of Hedge Fund Regulation in the United States (2016). SSRN: https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2714974

**2019**

- [3411110-016](https://wulfkaal.github.io/claims/3411110-016) [failure/argued] *(failure mode)* -- Instant settlement destroys an existing business practice: without the T+3 grace period, institutional investors can no longer lend idle shares to investors covering short positions.

  Wulf A. Kaal, Samuel Evans, Blockchain-Based Securities Offerings (2019). SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=3411110

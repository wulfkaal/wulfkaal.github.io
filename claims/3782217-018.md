# kaal:claim:3782217-018

**Claim.** Institutional corruption follows a repeating cycle: people surrender their power of information transmission to institutions that are initially more effective and efficient, those institutions centralize and ossify into corruption, and new technology then allows people to bypass them.

**Holds when.**

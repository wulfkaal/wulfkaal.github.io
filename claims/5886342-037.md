# kaal:claim:5886342-037

**Claim.** Every DAO member must have a real possibility to exit the DAO under the rules of the DAO Agreement without incurring unreasonable costs, which makes exit a mandatory feature of a compliant DAO rather than a matter of design choice.

**Type.** design  **Support.** asserted

**Holds when.**

- exit follows the rules of the DAO Agreement

**Source quote.**

> (1) A DAO Member must have the possibility to exit

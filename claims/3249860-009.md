# kaal:claim:3249860-009

**Claim.** To avoid a token price crash, escrow accounts holding unissued tokens should carry usage and access controls assuring investors that escrowed tokens will not be issued at a discount, with lockups or phased releases minimizing crash risk.

**Type.** design  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- issuers that retain unissued tokens in escrow after an ICO

**Source quote.**

> To avoid a token price crash, token escrow accounts should provide usage and access controls that assure investors that escrowed tokens will not be issued at a discount. Lockups of escrowed tokens for a specified time period or phased releases can also help minimize the risks of token price crashes.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Crypto Economics - The Top 100 Token Models Compared* (2018), II.4 Monetary Policy, page 9

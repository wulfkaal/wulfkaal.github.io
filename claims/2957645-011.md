# kaal:claim:2957645-011

**Claim.** Feedback effects change the timing of regulatory information: instead of acquiring necessary information only after rules have already proven suboptimal, they increase the availability of relevant information ex ante and support anticipation of necessary revisions.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- an informational exchange process between regulated entities and rulemakers is in place

**Source quote.**

> Rather than acquiring necessary information after rules have emerged as suboptimal, feedback effects help increase the availability of relevant information for rulemaking ex ante and anticipate necessary revisions

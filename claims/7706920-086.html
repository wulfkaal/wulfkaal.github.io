kaal:claim:7706920-086

Claim. In Study 1 the registered prediction for the cell with obligation 230 and entry cost 60 failed because the benchmark equated full answerability with staying, while the agents stayed and paid what they could as the corrected rule predicts.

Type. empirical · Support. reported

Holds when

  • Study 1: 240 episodes, none excluded, prompted language-model agent run through a command-line interface.
  • Not a test of agents holding earnings.

Source quote

H3, the prediction for the cell (D,c) = (230, 60), failed. The benchmark equated full answerability with staying, and the agents stayed and paid what they could, which the corrected rule of Result 1 predicts.

From. Wulf A. Kaal, Verification as Identity: Accountability, Personhood and the Scarcity of Verification in the Agent Economy (2026), page 17. Full text.

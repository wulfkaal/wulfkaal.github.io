# kaal:claim:3409548-022

**Claim.** Managers of funds that exist entirely in cyberspace cannot assume they are judgment proof; the practical consequence of operating across a global node network is exposure to more regulation, not less.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- funds operating through digital currencies and smart contracts without a domicile

**Source quote.**

> However, managers cannot assume that they are judgment-proof.

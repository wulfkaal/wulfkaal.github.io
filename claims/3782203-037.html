kaal:claim:3782203-037

Automating exclusion of cheaters through smart contracts makes punishment credible and removes the infinite regress of traditional enforcement, where members would have to police those who failed to police those who failed to police cheaters.

Source quote, verbatim
Algorithms can be written which exclude members who cheat from hav- ing access to their market. Punishment for cheating becomes automated and there- fore credible. Free riding in policing can at least partly be eliminated by automation.

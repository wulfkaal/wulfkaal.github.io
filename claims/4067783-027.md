# kaal:claim:4067783-027

**Claim.** Anonymity lets information flow from the edges of the system, from individuals who would have no agency in centralized structures, and such inputs are much less likely to materialize if DAO participants know each other.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- anonymity is preserved among DAO participants

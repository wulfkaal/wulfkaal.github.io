Claim. Where every level of a sponsorship chain has costly entry, recovery degrades gracefully and the chain recovers the lesser of the harm and the sum of the levels' capacities, so capacities add.

Type. mechanism · Support. argued

Holds when

  • Conditional on the single-agent, deterministic, stationary model of Part V.A, with an agent whose only assets are its earnings and its record.
  • Each level is an agent as in Part V.A that decides by Result 1 whether to stay when the obligation reaches it.

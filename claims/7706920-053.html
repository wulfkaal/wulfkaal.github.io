kaal:claim:7706920-053

Claim. Adjudication that does not depend on the claimant's recovery, such as a verifier-initiated slash of the record, is the remedy the model suggests for the claimant-participation problem.

Type. design · Support. argued

Holds when

  • Conditional on the single-agent, deterministic, stationary model of Part V.A, with an agent whose only assets are its earnings and its record.

Source quote

The remedy suggested by the model is adjudication that does not depend on the claimant’s recovery, such as a verifier-initiated slash of the record.

From. Wulf A. Kaal, Verification as Identity: Accountability, Personhood and the Scarcity of Verification in the Agent Economy (2026), page 11. Full text.

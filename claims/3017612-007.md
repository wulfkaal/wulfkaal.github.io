# kaal:claim:3017612-007

**Claim.** Because public and close corporation shareholders differ materially in bargaining power, close corporation shareholders should be granted greater flexibility to order their affairs by agreement.

**Type.** normative  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- closely held corporations

**Source quote.**

> Courts and academics have acknowledged there is a significant difference between shareholders of public and close corporations, specifically in regards to bargaining power, such that close corporation shareholders should have greater flexibility to align their affairs as they see fit.

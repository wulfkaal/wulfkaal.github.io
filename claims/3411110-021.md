# kaal:claim:3411110-021

**Claim.** Regulators' relative unsophistication about the technology is itself a risk driver, because a poorly informed regulator is likely to over-react and precipitate new or expanded regulation.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- regulators lacking technical understanding of blockchain

**Source quote.**

> The relative unsophistication of the regulators and the lack of regulators' understanding of the technology could result in an over-reaction which in turn can precipitate creating new / expanding existing regulations.

# kaal:claim:3808859-018

**Claim.** Open source contribution is governed by a cost-benefit calculation rather than pure altruism: the volunteer's opportunity costs of forgone paid work must be offset by satisfaction, autonomy, peer recognition, skill acquisition, or downstream commercial opportunity.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Source quote.**

> Software developers perform a cost-benefit analysis when they decide to make an open source project contribution and throughout the respective project.

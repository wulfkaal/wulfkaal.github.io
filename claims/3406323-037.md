# kaal:claim:3406323-037

**Claim.** Increasing counterparty trust in decentralized commerce requires that the full history of transactions be openly viewable and verifiable, so that counterparties' actions endure as part of a public record and a meaningful precedence system.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- decentralized smart contract based commerce between anonymous parties

**Source quote.**

> The history of transactions has to be fully openly viewable and verifiable, so that business parties can see that their counterparties will perform as expected.

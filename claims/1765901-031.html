kaal:claim:1765901-031

Extending private rights of action extraterritorially would expose non-U.S. companies to Section 10(b) liability based on any U.S. conduct, including conduct inside U.S. business operations alleged to have produced securities fraud abroad, and much of global securities litigation would migrate to the United States.

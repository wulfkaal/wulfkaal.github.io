# kaal:claim:4685567-014

**Claim.** Impact 1.0 impact markets fail on the demand side: they have more sellers than buyers, and many potential buyers would have funded those projects anyway, which removes the need for a secondary market and significantly reduces resale value.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- Impact 1.0 secondary markets for social impact credits

**Source quote.**

> Impact 1.0 markets typically have more sellers than buyers and many potential buyers would have done those 47 projects anyway thus removing the need for a secondary market. This imbalance of high supply and low demand significantly reduces the "resale" value

# kaal:claim:5095633-031

**Claim.** The reputation systems of SingularityNET, Fetch.ai, Ocean Protocol, Numeraire, and DcentAI are structurally insufficient for a fully decentralized Mechanical Turk model of large-scale AI dataset creation, offering only incremental innovation.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- highly contextual, iterative, and ethically sensitive dataset tasks
- continuous compliance with evolving legal standards

**Source quote.**

> While these platforms offer incremental innovations in how contributors are evaluated and rewarded, they remain structurally insufficient for a fully decentralized "Mechanical Turk" model of large-scale AI dataset creation.

# kaal:claim:3128900-006

**Claim.** The cost structure of centralized micro task systems, which require intermediation, produces significant overpricing that does not benefit the workers themselves.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- centralized systems that necessitate intermediation

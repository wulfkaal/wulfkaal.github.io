A burned entry fee cannot enter the incentive constraint as a sanction: it does not pad a later walk and affects honesty only through the participation channel of raising the premium.

Source quote, verbatim
What fees cannot do in this layer is enter the incentive constraint as a sanction: the fee is burned at entry, does not pad a later walk, and affects honesty only insofar as ( 12) raises the premium. That is a participation channel, and it follows from the fee’s placement in this model, not from Theorem 1 alone.

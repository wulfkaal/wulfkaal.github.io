# kaal:claim:3808873-039

**Claim.** One token one vote on chain governance produces plutocracy: it allocates more power to holders of a significant share of supply so that majority token holders have more power than all other holders combined, reintroducing the defects of one share one vote legacy designs.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- on chain voting mechanisms of the early 2020s

**Source quote.**

> A one- token-one-vote design allocates more power to token holders who have a significant share of the total supply of a given token. Majority token holders have more power than the rest of the token holders combined.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Decentralization Neutralizers* (2021), XI. Lack of Decentralized Governance Systems, page 31

kaal:claim:3782210-036

A meaningful reputational system with the potential for retrospective review would let a network rely on fewer nodes, since randomly selected nodes staking their reputation can do the polling work and be reviewed and punished later, which is one reason proof of stake is more efficient than proof of work.

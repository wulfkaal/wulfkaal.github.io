kaal:claim:2097160-012

Contingent convertible bonds issued to executives are typically too small in volume to dilute investors' equity holdings or to supply a meaningful equity infusion during a crisis, so copying investor CoCo designs for executive pay produces suboptimal outcomes.

Source quote, verbatim
contingent convertible bonds may be issued to executives in volumes that may not suffice to dilute investors' equity holdings. The lower volume of contingent convertible bonds issued to executives may not provide a sufficiently strong equity infusion during a crisis.

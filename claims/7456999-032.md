# kaal:claim:7456999-032

**Claim.** RLRF permits validators to have different objective functions and domain models, treating honest disagreement on ambiguous cases as epistemic signal and systematic deviation as a possible adversarial signal.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- the proposed reputation-weighted validation pool

**Source quote.**

> The mechanism does not require agents to share objective functions or domain models. Diversity of across the pool is a feature rather than a bug: under reputation-weighted aggregation, disagreement among honest agents on genuinely ambiguous cases produces measurable epistemic signal, while systematic deviation from consensus identiﬁes adversarial behavior.

# kaal:claim:2150377-011

**Claim.** Quarterly rather than annual Form PF updating for large hedge fund advisers is designed for timeliness: its purpose is to give the Financial Stability Oversight Council data current enough to identify emerging trends in systemic risk.

**Type.** design  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- advisers with at least $1.5 billion RAUM attributable to hedge funds

**Source quote.**

> Mandatory quarterly reporting for large hedge fund advisers in the United States aligns with international trends and is intended "to provide the FSOC with timely data to identify emerging trends in systemic risk."151

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Hedge Fund Manager Registration Under the Dodd-Frank Act* (2012), 2. Disclosure

# kaal:claim:2097160-036

**Claim.** Against the critique that long-term debt in pay does not deter short-run risky bets because expected short-term gains exceed the discounted value of the debt, adding early-trigger contingent convertible bonds changes managers' incentives by forcing them to weigh the effects of triggering events rather than only the debt to equity mix of their portfolio.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- early trigger attached to a substantial portion of the executive's debt holdings

**Source quote.**

> portion of executives' compensation packages could change managers' incentives. Executives would no longer simply focus on the debt versus equity portion of their portfolio; they would also consider the effects of triggering events.237

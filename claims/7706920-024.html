Claim. The requirements of continuity under change and of consequence, that an adverse finding attaches and stays, are where identity design is needed and they conflict.

Type. condition · Support. argued

Holds when

  • Five requirements are stated: binding, persistence, non-transferability, continuity under change, consequence.
  • The corpus assumes the first three.

Source quote

The corpus assumes the first three. The last two are where design is needed, and they conflict.

From. Wulf A. Kaal, Verification as Identity: Accountability, Personhood and the Scarcity of Verification in the Agent Economy (2026), page 6. Full text.

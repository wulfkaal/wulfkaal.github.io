# kaal:claim:1558614-025

**Claim.** The U.S. requirement that directors act on an informed basis is watered down because many states permit charter provisions exculpating directors from liability for breach of the duty of care, including the duty to act on an informed basis.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

> This requirement, however, is watered down in the United States by many states that allow corporations to adopt charter provisions that exculpate the directors from liability to the corporation for breach of a duty of care

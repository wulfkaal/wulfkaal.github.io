# kaal:claim:3402701-021

**Claim.** Contesting the common assumption that a 100 percent reserve is required to guarantee a peg, the authors argue full reserves are not always necessary for any currency that has intrinsic worth.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- currency possesses intrinsic worth, for example genuine transactional use

**Source quote.**

> On the contrary, we argue that a 100% reserve is not always necessary, as is the case with any currency which has an intrinsic worth

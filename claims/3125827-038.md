# kaal:claim:3125827-038

**Claim.** The sem token economy is inflationary at equilibrium, and the authors argue this is a feature: inflation improves security and discourages rent seeking by penalizing holders who do not use their tokens to evaluate posts.

**Type.** empirical  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- new tokens minted with every fee and never destroyed

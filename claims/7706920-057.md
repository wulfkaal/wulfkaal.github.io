# kaal:claim:7706920-057

**Claim.** Raising the entry cost above the threshold buys no capacity, and where the highest entry cost the registry can set is below the threshold, capacity must come from earnings, that is from the garnishable share, the standing premium and the slash share.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- Conditional on the single-agent, deterministic, stationary model of Part V.A, with an agent whose only assets are its earnings and its record.
- Assumes an entrant participates only if the value of entry exceeds an outside option.

**Source quote.**

> Raising c above ¯c buys no capacity, and where cmax < ¯c, entry cost cannot be raised far enough to buy it. In that case capacity must come from earnings, which means from φ, κ and the slash share s.

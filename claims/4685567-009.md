# kaal:claim:4685567-009

**Claim.** The absence of an efficient marketplace lets big dollar donors hijack the priorities of impact intermediaries such as foundations, donor advised funds, NGOs and fiscal sponsors, producing a feast or famine pattern for fundable projects.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- intermediated philanthropy without a central marketplace

**Source quote.**

> Because of the lack of an efficient marketplace, impact intermediaries, such as foundations, DAFs, NGOs, and fiscal sponsors, among others, often experience their priorities being hijacked by big-dollar donors. This often results in a feast or famine situation for fundable projects in Impact 1.0.

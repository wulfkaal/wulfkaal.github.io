kaal:claim:7598518-021

Claim. The channel the RLRF program claims reputation can resist is the one this study does not open, because its validators are stationary, its stake has no external value, and its weights are fixed before generation 1.

Type. condition · Support. stated

Holds when

  • The limit is structural, set by the design, rather than an observed null.
  • Goodhart resistance is the property the parent program claims, so this study leaves that claim untested.

Source quote

This is the channel the RLRF program claims reputation can resist. This study does not open it: its validators are stationary, its stake has no external value, and its weights are fixed before generation 1.

From. Wulf A. Kaal, Can Reputation Prevent Model Collapse? A Preregistered Test of Reputation-Weighted Data Filtering (2026). Full text.

# kaal:claim:3652481-030

**Claim.** The one token one vote designs popular in the early 2020s produced suboptimal voting outcomes because they allocate more power to holders of a large share of total token supply, leaving majority holders more powerful than all remaining holders combined.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- one token one vote voting mechanisms

**Source quote.**

> Suboptimal voting outcomes in existing decentralized protocols were associated with the then popular one-token-one-vote voting mechanisms. A one- token-one-vote design allocates more power to token holders who have a significant share of the total supply of a given token.

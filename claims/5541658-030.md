# kaal:claim:5541658-030

**Claim.** Because AI systems are predominantly developed in the West and trained mostly on Western data, their outputs are liable to carry cultural biases that inadequately represent non-Western cultures and the values inherent in them.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

- training corpora dominated by Western sources

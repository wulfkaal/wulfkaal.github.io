# kaal:claim:3227967-009

**Claim.** The major cause of lawyers obstructing transactions is the tendency to standardize or proceduralize legal solutions and to use standard form templates, because fixed standardized solutions imposed on complex, dynamic transactions produce frustration and difficulties.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

**Source quote.**

> This can happen for many reasons but the tendency to standardize – or "proceduralize" - legal solutions and to employ standard form "templates" is one major factor. Fixed and standardized solutions are imposed on complex, dynamic transactions resulting in frustration and difficulties.

# kaal:claim:2337268-013

**Claim.** Advisers with more than $150 million in regulatory assets under management are defined as large private fund advisers and must register with the SEC.

**Type.** definitional  **Support.** asserted

**Holds when.**

- advisers to one or more private funds
- RAUM above $150 million

**Source quote.**

> Investment advisers having more than $150 million regulatory assets under management (RAUM) are defined as large private fund advisers and are required to register with the SEC.23

# kaal:claim:1558614-024

**Claim.** Under the German business judgment rule's benefit of the corporation element, management cannot be acting for the corporation's benefit when its actions threaten the corporation's existence and economic survival.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** asserted

**Holds when.**

- German business judgment rule, second element

**Source quote.**

> management does not act for the benefit of the corporation if its actions threaten the existence and economic survival of the corporation

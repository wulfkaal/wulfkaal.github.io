# kaal:claim:3782217-020

**Claim.** Truth discovery in the marketplace of ideas is more effective and efficient when news aggregators are more decentralized: ten newspapers reporting on a story produce a more accurate historical picture than one newspaper reporting on the story ten times.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

**Source quote.**

> Truth discovery in any market, including the marketplace of ideas, is more effective and efficient when news aggregators are more decentralized. When there are J` newspapers reporting on a story, a more accurate historical picture emerges than when one newspaper reports on the story J` times.

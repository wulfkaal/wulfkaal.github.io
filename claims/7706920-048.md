# kaal:claim:7706920-048

**Claim.** The sign of the effect of collateral on answerability depends on whether the claimant can reach it, so a bond forfeited on an adjudicated harm adds to capacity while a refundable bond lowers it.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- Conditional on the single-agent, deterministic, stationary model of Part V.A, with an agent whose only assets are its earnings and its record.

**Source quote.**

> A bond that is forfeited on an adjudicated harm has the opposite effect: it adds B to the earnings-side capacity, which becomes E + B. The sign of the effect of collateral therefore depends on whether the claimant can reach it, and the conclusion is limited to collateral that the claimant cannot reach.

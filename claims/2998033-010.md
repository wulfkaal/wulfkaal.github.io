# kaal:claim:2998033-010

**Claim.** Fears that technologically untrained judges will misunderstand blockchain are overstated, because blockchain is no different from other software that courts have already evaluated, and courts assisted by well trained attorneys should be able to appreciate its significance.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Source quote.**

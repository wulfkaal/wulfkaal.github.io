Over-approval is moral hazard in the monitoring layer itself: the validation pool is the substrate’s monitor, and a pool that approves work failing ground truth is a monitor whose verdicts have decoupled from the quantity it exists to verify, the adjudicator’s failure mode that the incomplete-contracts tradition predicts wherever quality is adjudicated ex post.

Source quote, verbatim
Over-approval is moral hazard in the monitoring layer itself: the validation pool is the substrate’s monitor, and a pool that approves work failing ground truth is a monitor whose verdicts have decoupled from the quantity it exists to verify, the adjudicator’s failure mode that the incomplete-contracts tradition predicts wherever quality is adjudicated ex post.

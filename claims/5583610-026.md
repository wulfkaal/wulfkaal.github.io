# kaal:claim:5583610-026

**Claim.** Funding LER from marketing budgets and expensing it immediately under ASC 606 and IFRS 15 avoids the balance sheet liabilities that traditional loyalty programs incur through deferred revenue.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- issuers reporting under U.S. GAAP or IFRS
- rewards classified as marketing expense rather than a material right

**Source quote.**

> LER allows issuers to fund LER voucher rewards from marketing budgets, treating them as immediate expenses under U.S. GAAP (ASC 606) and IFRS 15. This avoids balance sheet liabilities

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Liquid Equity Rewards in Corporate America* (2025), 5.2. Economic Incentives for Shareholder Retention, page 22

Claim. Capacity rises with slope one in the entry cost up to the entry-cost threshold and is constant beyond it.

Type. mechanism · Support. argued

Holds when

  • Conditional on the single-agent, deterministic, stationary model of Part V.A, with an agent whose only assets are its earnings and its record.

Source quote

Capacity as a function of c is Γ(c) = min{E, V + − V0 + c}. It rises with slope one up to ¯c and is constant beyond it.

From. Wulf A. Kaal, Verification as Identity: Accountability, Personhood and the Scarcity of Verification in the Agent Economy (2026), page 12. Full text.

# kaal:claim:3402701-030

**Claim.** For any long-lived currency, a major shift in perceived value is eventually inevitable, and such a shift is not covered by reserves defending against hot money or by bonds defending against temporary economic instability.

**Type.** predictive  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- long-lived currencies
- black swan events

**Source quote.**

> For any long-lived currency a major shift in the perception of the value of a currency is at some point inevitable; a shift that is not accounted for by a reserve defending against hot money fluctuations or bonds defending against temporary instability in the larger economy

# kaal:claim:1428387-006

**Claim.** Because realization events for private equity investments occur infrequently, hedge fund managers have an incentive to avoid side pockets and to use estimated valuations for those investments instead.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- where the hedge fund holds private equity style investments
- where performance fees cannot be charged on side-pocketed assets

**Source quote.**

> The infrequency of realization events of private equity investments creates incentives for hedge fund managers to avoid side pockets and instead use estimates for the valuation of private equity investments.

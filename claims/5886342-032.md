# kaal:claim:5886342-032

**Claim.** A DAO is formed on the basis of a DAO Agreement setting out its terms and rules, and that agreement may consist of digital contracts, of written documents, or of a combination of the two.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** asserted

**Source quote.**

> (1) A DAO shall be formed on the basis of a DAO Agreement, which sets out the relevant terms and rules of the DAO in question. This agreement may consist of Digital Contracts, written documents, or a combination of the two.

**From.** Furrer Andreas, Wulf A. Kaal, Stephan D. Meyer, *Universal Digital Law Codex (UDLC)* (2025), Part B, Book V, Section 2, Article 55 DAO Agreement, page 12

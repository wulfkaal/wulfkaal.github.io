kaal:claim:7598518-024

Claim. No validator, filter, or score in the design is asked about rarity, novelty, frequency, diversity, or coverage, and the outcome is computed by an evaluator alone from a sealed reference that no filter reads.

Type. design · Support. stated

Holds when

  • The oracle alone receives one bit per candidate from the sealed reference, the candidate's true validity verdict.
  • Validators do see a tag, whose association with rare membership is bounded by design.

Source quote

Validators judge validity only. No validator, filter, or score is asked about rarity, novelty, frequency, diversity, or coverage. The outcome is computed by an evaluator alone, from a sealed reference holding rare-set membership and component identity, which no filter reads.

From. Wulf A. Kaal, Can Reputation Prevent Model Collapse? A Preregistered Test of Reputation-Weighted Data Filtering (2026). Full text.

# kaal:claim:2816408-012

**Claim.** Title IV exempts private fund advisers with less than $150 million assets under management from registration, and requires the SEC to weigh investment strategy, size, and governance in determining the systemic risk of private funds.

**Type.** definitional  **Support.** asserted

**Holds when.**

- Dodd-Frank Act section 408

> Title IV of the Dodd-Frank Act exempts private fund advisers with less than $150 million AUM from registration (Dodd-Frank Act § 408) and requires the SEC to examine factors including the investment strategy, size, and governance of an investment adviser

# kaal:claim:3441904-034

**Claim.** Feedback effects in decentralized systems raise the availability of relevant information for governance rules ex ante and allow necessary revisions to be anticipated before rules emerge as suboptimal, rather than acquiring that information ex post.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- dynamic decentralized governance frameworks with peer to peer information transfer

**Source quote.**

> Rather than acquiring the necessary information ex-post after rules have emerged as suboptimal, feedback effects help increase the availability of relevant information for governance rules ex-ante and anticipate necessary revisions before rules emerge as suboptimal.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Blockchain-Based Corporate Governance* (2019), IV.2 Benefits of Dynamic Decentralized Governance, page 29

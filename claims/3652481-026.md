# kaal:claim:3652481-026

**Claim.** Stable and presumptively optimal static or constitutional rules for DAO governance typically enable gaming and arbitrage opportunities, because rational opportunistic parties will circumvent complex static rules to increase their share of power and profit.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- the system is open and democratic
- the rule set is static and complex

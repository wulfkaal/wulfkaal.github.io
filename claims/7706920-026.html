kaal:claim:7706920-026

Claim. An identity rule that wrongly splits honest agents from their records five percent of the time has the same effect on the exit side as lowering the discount factor from 0.95 to 0.9025, and nearly doubles the standing required on the exit side.

Type. mechanism · Support. argued

Holds when

  • Worked example at delta = 0.95 and epsilon = 0.05 using the Part V.C parameters.
  • Stated for the model's assumptions; not calibrated to any market and not a claim about any legal system.

Source quote

With δ = 0.95 and ϵ = 0.05, the standing required on the exit side nearly doubles, by the factor 1.95. A rule that splits honest agents from their records five percent of the time has the same effect on the exit side as lowering the discount factor from 0.95 to 0.9025.

From. Wulf A. Kaal, Verification as Identity: Accountability, Personhood and the Scarcity of Verification in the Agent Economy (2026), page 7. Full text.

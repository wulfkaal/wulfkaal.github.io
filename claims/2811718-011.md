# kaal:claim:2811718-011

**Claim.** Form ADV Part II filings jumped from 3,024 in 2010 to 21,685 in 2011, and that jump was accompanied by a corresponding increase both in the number of filings mentioning investor due diligence and in the due diligence counts within those filings.

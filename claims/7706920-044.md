# kaal:claim:7706920-044

**Claim.** An agent answers in full for any harm up to the lesser of its earnings bound and its exit-side capacity, the principal's exposure falls to zero at the threshold standing, and recovery fails in one of two ways depending on the entry cost: the agent exits and the injured party recovers nothing, or it stays and the injured party receives its garnishable earnings.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- Conditional on the single-agent, deterministic, stationary model of Part V.A, with an agent whose only assets are its earnings and its record.

> An agent answers in full for any harm up to Γ = min{E,X}, the principal’s exposure falls to zero at the standing R∗, and recovery fails in one of two ways depending on the entry cost: the agent exits and the injured party recovers nothing, or it stays and the injured party receives the agent’s garnishable earnings.

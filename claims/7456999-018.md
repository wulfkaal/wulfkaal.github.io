# kaal:claim:7456999-018

**Claim.** The Computative Economics framing replaces a preference holder choosing fixed alternatives with a generator-evaluator tuple whose activity produces the alternative set.

**Type.** definitional  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- the computative primitive described in the paper

**Source quote.**

> The agent is not a preference-holder choosing among ﬁxed alternatives. The agent is a generator- evaluator tuple whose activity produces the alternative set in the course of operating. The quality signal is a functional on the generated possibility space, not an aggregate of preferences over realizations.

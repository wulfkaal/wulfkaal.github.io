# kaal:claim:3017612-026

**Claim.** Drag-along rights are likely enforceable in Delaware absent fraud or duress, because Delaware rejects the de facto merger doctrine and therefore does not trigger statutory formalities such as appraisal rights.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- Delaware corporations
- no fraud or duress

**Source quote.**

> Absent issues of fraud or duress, it seems likely drag-along rights will be enforced for Delaware corporations.84 Because Delaware does not follow the practical merger doctrine, statutory formalities such as appraisal rights are not implicated.

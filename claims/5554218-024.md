# kaal:claim:5554218-024

**Claim.** The deterministic execution of smart contracts rigidly enforces coded terms and thereby incentivizes participants to exploit loopholes for immediate gain, because no mechanism exists to adapt the rules contextually.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- smart contracts without on chain amendment or human review mechanisms

**Source quote.**

> Conversely, smart contracts' deterministic execution rigidly enforces coded terms, incentivizing participants to exploit loopholes for immediate gain, as there is no mechanism to adapt rules contextually.

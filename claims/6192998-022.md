# kaal:claim:6192998-022

**Claim.** Without granular attribution a meaningful citation graph cannot be constructed, because the system cannot record which dimension of a contribution was valuable, for example strong conceptual innovation paired with poor execution.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- validation collapses multidimensional assessment into a single bit

**Source quote.**

> Without granular attribution, we cannot build a meaningful citation graph. We don't know that Alice's innovation insight was valuable even though her execution was poor, or that Bob's contribution was primarily in implementation rather than conceptual novelty.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Evolution of Domain-Specific Reputation Systems From Binary Validation to Citation-Weighted Knowledge Attribution* (2026), III.C. Binary Information Destruction, page 23

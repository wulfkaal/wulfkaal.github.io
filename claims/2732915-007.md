# kaal:claim:2732915-007

**Claim.** Guaranteeing complete anonymity is essential to obtaining a sufficient response rate from private fund advisers, but that guarantee prevents a broader descriptive statistical analysis of the sample.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- survey studies of private fund advisers who fear identification

**Source quote.**

> The anonymity of survey responses did not allow a broader descriptive statistical analysis of the sample. The author guaranteed complete anonymity to all survey respondents, which is an essential element in obtaining a sufficient response rate.

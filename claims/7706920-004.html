Claim. The corpus states that a persistent, non-transferable record gives an agent an identity but does not develop the point, and the Article develops identity from a record and asks when an agent holding one can answer for a harm of a given size.

Type. condition · Support. stated

Source quote

No paper in the corpus develops that point. This Article develops it and then asks the question the corpus leaves open: under what conditions can an agent that holds such a record answer for a harm of a given size, and what remains for a human or organizational principal when it cannot?

From. Wulf A. Kaal, Verification as Identity: Accountability, Personhood and the Scarcity of Verification in the Agent Economy (2026), page 2. Full text.

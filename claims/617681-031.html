kaal:claim:617681-031

Because the judiciary in many European jurisdictions is a specialized career entered shortly after university and without significant private practice experience, European judges lack the practice background that facilitates learning to adjudicate complex corporate disputes.

the judiciary is a specialized career that one enters shortly after university training and without significant experience in private practice. Lack of practice experience does not facilitate learning how to adjudicate disputes arising out of complex corporate transactions.

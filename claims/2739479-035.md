# kaal:claim:2739479-035

**Claim.** Among advisers who saw an earnings effect, the attributed cause shifted from direct expense to opportunity cost between 2012 and 2015, with opportunity cost references rising from 9 percent to 32 percent while increased expense references fell from 53 percent to 36 percent.

**Type.** empirical  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- only respondents who reported an effect on fund earnings
- 2012 and 2015 surveys

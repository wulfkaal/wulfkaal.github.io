Fiduciary responsibility follows actual control rather than formal office: a shareholder exercising absolute de facto control over a corporation owes fiduciary duties regardless of whether that shareholder holds de jure title.

Source quote, verbatim
If a shareholder "exercises absolute de facto control over a corporation, such actual dominion carries with it fiduciary responsibility regardless of the presence or absence of de jure titles.

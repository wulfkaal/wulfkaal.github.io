# kaal:claim:1765901-033

**Claim.** Changing national rules in a spirit of cooperation among states and regulators is more productive for preventing securities fraud than imposing U.S. rules on foreign nations through extraterritorial imperialism.

**Type.** normative  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- cross border securities fraud enforcement

**Source quote.**

> Cooperation among nation states and their regulators to prevent securities fraud could help improve foreign relations. Changing national rules in the spirit of cooperation is likely to be more productive than imposing rules on foreign nations through extraterritorial imperialism.

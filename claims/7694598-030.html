kaal:claim:7694598-030

Claim. For agents powered by open-weight models of 3 to 72 billion parameters, the Stage 7 primary efficacy hypothesis was not supported: the treatment arm resolved about 0.8 percentage points fewer paired tasks than control in each replicate.

Type. empirical · Support. reported

Holds when

  • Three independent replicates.
  • Open-weight models of 3 to 72 billion parameters; no frontier model tested.
  • Primary outcome is ground-truth-anchored task resolution.

Source quote

The primary efficacy hypothesis was not supported. In each of the three replicates, the treatment arm resolved fewer paired tasks than the control arm, by about 0.8 percentage points

From. Wulf A. Kaal, Institutional Design for Governed Self-Modification in Agentic Reputation Architecture (2026), page 17. Full text.

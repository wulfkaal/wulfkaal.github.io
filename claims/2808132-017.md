# kaal:claim:2808132-017

**Claim.** Because facts-based rulemaking does not anticipate the regulatory issues created by innovation, rulemakers may realize far too late, or never, what new regulatory demands a given innovation generates.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- age of exponential innovation
- purely reactive facts-based rulemaking

**Source quote.**

> facts-based rulemaking did not anticipate regulatory issues created by innovation, rulemakers may not at all or much too late realize what new regulatory demands apply to a given innovation and associated regulatory issue.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, Erik P.M. Vermeulen, *How to Regulate Disruptive Innovation - From Facts to Data* (2016), III.1 Ex Post Facts-Based (Trial-and-Error) Rulemaking, page 18

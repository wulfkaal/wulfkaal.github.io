# kaal:claim:3782198-001

**Claim.** Social media platform owners do not know and cannot choose which content will matter; the answers are held in the behavior of the network of users, and the companies merely monitor network transactions and analyze them statistically with automated algorithms.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- applies to Web 2.0 platforms whose value derives from user generated content and behavior

**Source quote.**

> The network of users has the answers in their members' behavior. The social media companies simply monitor the networks' transactions and statistically analyze the information with automated algorithms.

**From.** Craig Calcaterra, Wulf A. Kaal, *Contemporary Decentralization* (2021), From Social Media to Decentralized Coordination, page 4

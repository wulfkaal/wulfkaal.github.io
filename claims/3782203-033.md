# kaal:claim:3782203-033

**Claim.** Anonymity increases rather than decreases the value of reputation tokens, because when potential business partners have less knowledge of a counterparty's identity, the number of reputation tokens held becomes the more important signal.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- anonymous or pseudonymous networks
- incomplete information about counterparties

**Source quote.**

> When potential business partners have less knowledge of your identity, the knowledge from the number of reputation tokens you hold becomes more important.

**From.** Craig Calcaterra, Wulf A. Kaal, *A Technical Perspective on Decentralization* (2021), Application to reputation tokens, page 21

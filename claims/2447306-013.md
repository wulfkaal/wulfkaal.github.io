# kaal:claim:2447306-013

**Claim.** Form PF compliance cost is sharply size dependent: quarterly filing large funds spent on average $155,286 on the initial filing, roughly sixteen times the $9,520 average reported by annually filing smaller funds.

**Type.** empirical  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- initial Form PF filing
- small sample of large quarterly filers

**Source quote.**

> Quarterly filing large funds spent on average $155,286 for the initial filing of Form PF. By contrast, annually filing smaller funds only spend on average $9,520 for the first time filing of Form PF.

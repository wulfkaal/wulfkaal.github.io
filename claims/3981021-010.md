# kaal:claim:3981021-010

**Claim.** Information sharing remedies are self limiting: the Pension Protection Act of 2006 let the IRS share more charity tax record information with state officials so they could investigate possible violations of state law, but the same act established strict controls over how that information could be used.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

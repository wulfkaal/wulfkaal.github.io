# kaal:claim:4957318-024

**Claim.** Experimental rules impose a cost on the regulated: because the rules are temporary and subject to change, they complicate compliance efforts, disrupt long term planning, and create ambiguity that can produce resistance or unintended non compliance.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- where the experimental rule substantially modifies an existing regulatory framework

# kaal:claim:3017612-017

**Claim.** To escape the procedural hurdles of derivative litigation and recover directly, a shareholder must persuade the court that those abusing control of the corporation harmed the shareholder directly rather than the entity.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- direct as opposed to derivative suits
- Delaware courts ask who suffered the harm and who would receive the recovery

**Source quote.**

> To avoid some of the procedural hurdles of derivative litigation and enable direct recovery, shareholders must convince the court that individuals abusing control of the corporation were directly harming the shareholders.

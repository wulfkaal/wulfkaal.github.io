# kaal:claim:2808132-029

**Claim.** Adaptive rulemaking helps overcome the collective action problem of rulemaking because when there are fewer stable rules, latent majority groups and dominant minority groups have fewer opportunities to influence a continuously and timely adapting process.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- rulemaking processes that incorporate dynamic and adaptive elements
- presence of organized interest groups competing for influence

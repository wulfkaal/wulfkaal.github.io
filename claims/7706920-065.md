# kaal:claim:7706920-065

**Claim.** Under a shared fault the standing a sponsor needs grows exponentially in the number of simultaneous faults and the number of faults it covers grows only logarithmically in its standing, because each sub-agent's slash shrinks the capacity the sponsor needs to cover the next.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- Conditional on the single-agent, deterministic, stationary model of Part V.A, with an agent whose only assets are its earnings and its record.
- Assumes equal harms, perfect correlation among the faults, compounding slashes, and a sponsor that owes the shortfall of its sub-agents.

**Source quote.**

> The standing required grows exponentially in the number of simultaneous faults, and the number of faults covered grows only logarithmically in standing. The reason is that the slash imposed by each sub-agent’s harm shrinks the capacity that the sponsor needs to cover the next.

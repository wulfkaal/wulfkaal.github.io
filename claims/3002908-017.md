# kaal:claim:3002908-017

**Claim.** Complex smart contracts involving several parties can only work if they rest on a verifiable and unhackable system, which is what blockchain technology supplies.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** asserted

**Holds when.**

- applies to multiparty smart contracts of higher complexity, such as derivative contracts

**Source quote.**

> Complex smart contracts involving several parties need to rely on a verifiable and unhackable system provided by blockchain technology.

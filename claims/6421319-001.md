# kaal:claim:6421319-001

**Claim.** Economic institutions that rely on lagging indicators such as price signals, employment data, and GDP reports cannot detect AI driven transformation, because the transformation propagates faster than the monitoring systems built to observe it.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- applies to institutions whose detection depends on advance warning indicators

**Source quote.**

> Institutions in linear systems that depend on advance warning—price signals, employment data, and GDP reports—cannot detect a transformation that propagates faster than the information systems designed to monitor it.

# kaal:claim:3249860-001

**Claim.** Crypto-economic incentive design is only limitedly successful at shaping future human behavior, because the designer must speculate about future human mental states and belief systems that may turn out entirely different from what was anticipated.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- designs that rely on assumptions about human reactions to economic incentives

**Source quote.**

> Shaping future human behavior through incentive design is limitedly successful7 because the social engineer speculates about human future mental states and corresponding belief systems.

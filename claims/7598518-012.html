kaal:claim:7598518-012

Claim. The results section is written in advance as a reporting shell that fixes the order of reporting, with every number left empty until the frozen analysis fills it.

Type. design · Support. stated

Holds when

  • Concerns the pre-written results shell of the working draft.
  • A reader can compare the finished section against the version written before any outcome was read.

Source quote

The results section of the working draft is written in advance as a reporting shell: it fixes the order of reporting, the P verdict first, then procedure validity, then the nine confirmatory labels, the secondary and failure-mode labels, the gates and ladder, and the secondary outcomes and costs, with every number left empty until the frozen analysis fills it

From. Wulf A. Kaal, Can Reputation Prevent Model Collapse? A Preregistered Test of Reputation-Weighted Data Filtering (2026). Full text.

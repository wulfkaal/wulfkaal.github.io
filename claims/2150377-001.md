# kaal:claim:2150377-001

**Claim.** Freedom from supervision and disclosure obligations was functional rather than incidental for hedge funds: it enabled successful fund launches, helped generate higher returns, and attracted investors, which is why manager registration is contested.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** asserted

**Holds when.**

- hedge funds operating before the Dodd-Frank Act
- funds investing in global markets

**Source quote.**

> Hedge funds' ability to invest in global markets without supervision and significant disclosure obligations was important for successful hedge fund launches, helped generate higher returns, and attracted investors.

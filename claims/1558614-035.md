# kaal:claim:1558614-035

**Claim.** Germany's 2005 introduction of the derivative suit tightened the standard of care only partially, because section 148(1) of the AktG conditions shareholder standing on holding shares worth roughly 100,000 euros, a threshold with no U.S. counterpart.

**Type.** design  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- German derivative suits under section 148(1) AktG as amended by the UMAG
- partly counterbalanced by VorstAG liability and compensation reduction provisions

**Source quote.**

> section 148(1) of the AktG requires a threshold ownership of shares totaling !100,000 (about $127,090) for shareholders to have standing to sue on behalf 174 of the corporation in German courts

**Claim.** Indirect regulation removes the problem of missing jurisdictional authority and therefore of jurisdictional arbitrage, because the Basel Framework applies to banks worldwide rather than to funds in any one jurisdiction.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- Basel Framework is broadly adopted across banking jurisdictions

**Source quote.**

# kaal:claim:3606663-007

**Claim.** Once the staggered private sale discounts of up to fifty percent are counted as a cost of capital, the cost structure of an ICO is arguably significantly higher than that of an IPO, notwithstanding the ICO's lower legal and offering expenses.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- ICOs conducted in 2017 and 2018 with staggered discount structures
- comparison against average IPO underwriting fees of four to seven percent

**Source quote.**

> Considering these steep discounts to incentivize earliest possible investments, the cost structure of ICOs is arguably significantly higher than the cost structure of an IPO.

**From.** Kaal, *Digital Asset Market Evolution* (2020), 2. Initial Coin Offerings, page 16

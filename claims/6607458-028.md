# kaal:claim:6607458-028

**Claim.** Distribution of economic outcomes under the computative primitive follows the distribution of access to computational infrastructure rather than labor or capital scarcity, because cognitive labor scarcity dissolves where production is computationally substitutable while capital scarcity reconstitutes around compute.

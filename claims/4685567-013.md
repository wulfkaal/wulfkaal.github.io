**Claim.** Impact markets that promote retrospective funding and resale of impact carry an inherent risk of incentivizing net negative ventures, because individuals can capture the benefit of positive impacts without bearing the cost when their actions produce negative impacts.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- retrospective funding
- resale of impact permitted

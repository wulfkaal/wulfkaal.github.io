# kaal:claim:2811718-012

**Claim.** Although overall Form ADV Part II filings fell between 2012 and 2013, due diligence counts fell only marginally, from 20,828 to 20,031, and filings mentioning due diligence fell from 7,862 to 7,198, less than proportionally to the drop in total filings.

**Type.** empirical  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- SEC Form ADV Part II brochure filings
- 2012 to 2013

**Source quote.**

> While the overall filings between 2012 and 2013 depreciated, the ratio of due diligence counts is only marginally affected (depreciating from 20,828 to 20,031) and filings that mention due diligence decreased (depreciating from 7,862 to 7,198) in relation to the overall filing depreciation.

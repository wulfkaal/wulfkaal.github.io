# kaal:claim:3125827-026

**Claim.** Forcing validation pools to close in finite time, which practical demand for swift resolution requires, opens the possibility of network partition and of a lack of genuine consensus, and therefore of forks. Complete finality would require unbounded validation time.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- validation windows bounded to seconds for practical liveness

**Source quote.**

> By forcing validation pools to end in finite time, we open the possibility of network partition and lack of genuine consensus which opens the possibility of forks,

**From.** Craig Calcaterra, Wulf A. Kaal, *Secure Proof of Stake Protocol* (2018), 5.2 Block production, page 19

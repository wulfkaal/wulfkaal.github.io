kaal:claim:7598518-008

Claim. The design fixes, before any data, the sentences the paper may write for each outcome and the conditions for each, and it bars claims about staking incentives, slashing, collusion, Sybil resistance, deployed systems, natural web text, frontier-scale models, or a solution to collapse.

Type. condition · Support. stated

Holds when

  • The bar is part of the pre-data specification of which sentences each outcome permits.
  • Reporting the bar is not itself one of the barred claims.

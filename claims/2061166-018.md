# kaal:claim:2061166-018

**Claim.** The German bank levy is internally inconsistent because financial institutions without systemic relevance must contribute to the reorganization fund yet are ineligible to receive support payments from it.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- German reorganization fund financed by a levy on all German banks

**Source quote.**

> The German levy system is problematic because financial institutions without systemic relevance are required to contribute to the fund, yet are not able to receive fund support payments.

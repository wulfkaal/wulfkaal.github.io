# kaal:claim:6607458-008

**Claim.** Agentic decoupling is the structural severance of value creation from human labor and consumption at the production-function level; the claim is not that human consumption disappears but that it ceases to be the proximate driver of the dominant mode of production in affected domains.

**Type.** definitional  **Support.** asserted

**Holds when.**

- applies to domains where autonomous agents generate, verify, and reinvest outputs among themselves

**Source quote.**

> The claim is not that human consumption disappears. It is that human consumption ceases to be the proximate driver of the dominant mode of production in the affected domain.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Computative Economics A Framework for Economic Analysis under Computational Abundance* (2026), II.D. Agentic Decoupling and Terminal Consumption, page 8

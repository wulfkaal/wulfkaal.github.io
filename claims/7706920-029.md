# kaal:claim:7706920-029

**Claim.** Raising linkage by linking more successors to old records also raises wrongful merging, which taxes honest entry, and a rule strict enough to hold wrongful merging down raises wrongful splitting.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- Whether an acceptable rule exists depends on how separable evasive, legitimate and independent successors are in observable data.
- The Article does not estimate that and does not derive the effect of wrongful merging.

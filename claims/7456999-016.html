Reward-model overoptimization can raise proxy reward while degrading true reward as optimized policies move farther from the reference policy.

Source quote, verbatim
Gao, Schulman, and Hilton (2023) demonstrated that reward model overoptimization follows predictable scaling laws: as KL divergence between the optimized policy and the reference policy grows, the proxy reward continues to rise while the true reward degrades.

Claim. The regress of verifiers ends in an audit against a realized outcome, which is not another verifier, and in a stake that the verifier's own record can carry.

Type. mechanism · Support. argued

Holds when

  • The stake is credible only if it does not exceed the verifier's own answerability capacity.

Source quote

The regress of verifiers therefore ends in two places. It ends in an audit against a realized outcome, which is not another verifier, and in a stake that the verifier’s own record can carry.

From. Wulf A. Kaal, Verification as Identity: Accountability, Personhood and the Scarcity of Verification in the Agent Economy (2026), page 13. Full text.

# kaal:claim:5554218-026

**Claim.** Binding conventions in digital law typically achieve only moderate ratification, yet their principles still permeate national legislation inspired by them, producing de facto harmonization without formal accession.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

Claim. The pool under test omits most of what makes RLRF an institution: it has no economy, no strategy, no adversary, no deliberation, and no reputation dynamics after the single settlement.

Type. condition · Support. stated

Holds when

  • It bounds what any result can say about the parent mechanism as a whole.

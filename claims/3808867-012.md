# kaal:claim:3808867-012

**Claim.** Delegating one's vote to a third party whose ethics and belief system the voter does not truly know produces suboptimal outcomes, and delegated voting on issues that cannot be foreseen is corrupting.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

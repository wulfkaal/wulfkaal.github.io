# kaal:claim:2061166-020

**Claim.** The German Banking Act requirement that a bridge bank have its head office inside Germany is of highly questionable compatibility with European Union law, specifically the principle of free movement of capital under Article 63 TFEU.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

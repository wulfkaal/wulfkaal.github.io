# kaal:claim:2348463-001

**Claim.** There is a substantial overlap between the systemic risk disclosure requirements imposed on hedge fund advisers under Title IV of the Dodd-Frank Act and the disclosure requirements under the fully revised version of Bankruptcy Rule 2019.

**Type.** empirical  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- hedge fund advisers subject to Title IV reporting
- creditors and groups covered by Revised Rule 2019 in chapter 9 and chapter 11 cases

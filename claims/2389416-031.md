# kaal:claim:2389416-031

**Claim.** Some large advisers reduce their AUM in the months from May to August 2012, which strongly increases the discontinuity around the registration threshold and separates the two groups more sharply, though the effect vanishes late in the sample period.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- strategic subsample of 87 advisers
- May to August 2012

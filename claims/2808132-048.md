**Claim.** Regulators should take anticipatory measures only after cross-validation and triangulation, that is, when multiple independent data analyses point unanimously toward a specific demand for regulatory action.

**Type.** design  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- multiple high-quality decentralized real-time data sources available to regulators
- unanimous convergence of those analyses

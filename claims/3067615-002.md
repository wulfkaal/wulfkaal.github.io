# kaal:claim:3067615-002

**Claim.** Tokens sold in an ICO are structurally different from equity: they do not generally confer ownership rights, no right to dividends, and no claim on company assets in bankruptcy, so the risk and reward profile of a token is not that of a share.

**Type.** definitional  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- comparison of ICOs to initial public offerings

**Source quote.**

> Risks and rewards of tokens differ from those of equity. Unlike token ownership, equity typically conveys a right to dividends. In the case of bankruptcy, equity owners have some claims on the assets of the company.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, Marco Dell'Erba, *Initial Coin Offerings Emerging Practices, Risk Factors, and Red Flags* (2017), I. Introduction, page 3

# kaal:claim:2811718-003

**Claim.** Before the financial crisis of 2008 to 2009, the standard AIMA and MFA due diligence questionnaire templates were often deployed defensively: managers used the old versions to steer investors away from questions that would have exposed weaknesses in the managers' controls.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- pre financial crisis practice
- template based questionnaires edited by the manager

**Source quote.**

> The old versions of those questionnaires were often used in an attempt to avoid investors' questions challenging the manager on possible weaknesses in their controls.

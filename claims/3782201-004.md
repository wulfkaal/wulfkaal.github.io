# kaal:claim:3782201-004

**Claim.** Self policing is more effective when the members themselves hold the power, because a centralized hierarchy in which each member holds distinct powers and responsibilities is more prone to structural corruption.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- where members hold the best domain knowledge about their own processes

**Source quote.**

> The power of decentralization is individual autonomy. Self-policing their own members is more effective when the members have the power, unlike a centralized hierarchy which is more prone to structural corruption when each member holds distinct powers and responsibilities.

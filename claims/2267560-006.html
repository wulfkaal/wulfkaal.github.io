kaal:claim:2267560-006

Trial-and-error rulemaking is suboptimal because participating actors acquire the necessary information ex-post, only after rules have turned out to be suboptimal, rather than increasing the availability of relevant information ex-ante.

is suboptimal for rulemaking because, rather than increasing the availability of relevant information ex-ante (through a dynamic process as proposed in this paper), participating actors acquire the necessary information ex-post after rules turn out to be suboptimal.

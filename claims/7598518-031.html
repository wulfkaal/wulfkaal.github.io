kaal:claim:7598518-031

Claim. A threshold arm that accepts too few candidates fills the shortfall uniformly from rejected candidates, and an arm whose minimum over generations of the seed-mean fill-free share falls below 0.98 in a family has its tests there invalidated.

Type. design · Support. stated

Holds when

  • The gate binds every threshold arm, including the single verifier and the equal-weight pool.
  • Validator compute is not equal across arms, so an equal candidate budget does not mean equal assessment cost.

Source quote

A threshold arm that accepts too few fills the shortfall uniformly from rejected candidates, and an arm whose minimum over generations of the seed-mean fill-free share, min(accepted, N)/N with N = 2, 000 training rows per generation, falls below amin = 0. 98 in a family has its tests there invalidated.

From. Wulf A. Kaal, Can Reputation Prevent Model Collapse? A Preregistered Test of Reputation-Weighted Data Filtering (2026). Full text.

# kaal:claim:3411110-033

**Claim.** Widespread retail investor trading of digital securities, including the ability to margin them, is the linchpin of survivability for blockchain based securities offerings; without retail access the technology may remain marginalized in the offering process.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- retail investor access on a widespread basis
- margining of digital securities permitted

**Source quote.**

> will be the linchpin of survivability and evolution of blockchain-based securities offerings. Without retail investor access, the technology may remain marginalized in the offering process.

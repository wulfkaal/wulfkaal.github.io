# kaal:claim:3799320-026

**Claim.** Because static complex DAO rule sets bring inevitable corruptive gaming and arbitrage behavior, effective DAO governance designs should focus on dynamic elements such as members' ability to re-evaluate existing precedent.

**Type.** design  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- DAO governance that must both protect members and permit enhancement

**Source quote.**

> With static complex sets of DAO rules comes inevitable corruptive opportunistic gaming and arbitrage behavior. To avoid these negative effects of static complex DAO rules, effective DAO governance designs should be focused on dynamic elements.

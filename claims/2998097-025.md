# kaal:claim:2998097-025

**Claim.** If the FSOC relies on Form PF data that is subject to inaccuracies, because uncertain filers complete the form using estimates and assumptions, then the FSOC's own work on private funds may in turn be subject to errors.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- over 40 percent of surveyed filers disagreed with Form PF definitions or instructions

**Source quote.**

> If FSOC relied on Form PF data in its systemic risk assessment that is subject to inaccuracies, the paper suggested that it appeared possible that FSOC's work pertaining to private funds could in turn be subject to errors.149

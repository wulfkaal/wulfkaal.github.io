# kaal:claim:2957645-023

**Claim.** Where conversion has a negative effect on stock price, management is further incentivized to maintain and manage risk in order to avoid reputational loss and the income reduction caused by losses in stock options.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- conversion depresses the issuer's stock price
- management compensation includes stock options

# kaal:claim:2629451-022

**Claim.** From the first day of the N/DPA term through day twenty five, the period in which mandated governance improvements are in force, the market prices the N/DPA as a positive event for the firm.

**Type.** empirical  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- window t=0 to t=25 after BNDPAT
- period during which N/DPA governance improvements are in effect

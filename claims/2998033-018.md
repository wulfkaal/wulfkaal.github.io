# kaal:claim:2998033-018

**Claim.** Under FinCEN guidance a mere user of virtual currency is not a money services business and faces no registration duty, while a user deemed an administrator or exchanger must comply with reporting and recordkeeping regulations.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- under FinCEN guidance issued in March 2013 extending the Bank Secrecy Act to virtual currencies

**Source quote.**

> While a user of a virtual currency is not a MSB and not subject to any registration requirements, a user of virtual currencies who is deemed to be an administrator or exchanger will be required to comply with its reporting and recordkeeping regulations.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Blockchain Innovation for Private Investment Funds* (2017), II.5. Regulatory Implications, page 18

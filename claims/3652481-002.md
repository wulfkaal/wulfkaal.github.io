# kaal:claim:3652481-002

**Claim.** A jurisdiction is only truly favorable to DAOs if it grants the DAO limited liability as an entity and accepts its independent status without requiring any representative in that jurisdiction.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Source quote.**

> Only a jurisdiction that gives the DAO limited liability as an entity and accepts its independent status without the need for representation may truly be favorable for DAO concepts.

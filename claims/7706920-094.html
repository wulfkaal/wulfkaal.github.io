Source quote

The verification record can be expressed as a W3C Verifiable Credential (Data Model 2.0). The issuer is the verifier pool, the subject is the agent identifier, and the claims are the count of adjudicated tasks, the agreement rate with ground truth, the over-approval rate, the number of slash events and the open obligation D, with a validity window.

From. Wulf A. Kaal, Verification as Identity: Accountability, Personhood and the Scarcity of Verification in the Agent Economy (2026), page 20. Full text.

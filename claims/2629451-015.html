kaal:claim:2629451-015

Pre-announcement leaks about a pending N/DPA, including leaks by prosecutors, should not move markets significantly because leaked details carry no certainty or finality as to final terms or fine amount.

Source quote, verbatim
We assume that such leaks will not precipitate significant market movements as there is no certainty or finality as to the final terms of the NDPA and the amount of a fine.

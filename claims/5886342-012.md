# kaal:claim:5886342-012

**Claim.** Legal Identity is the narrower category: it is an Identity to which rights and obligations are attributed on the basis of the applicable law, in particular natural and legal persons, so having an identity in a digital system does not by itself confer legal personality.

**Type.** definitional  **Support.** asserted

**Holds when.**

- attribution depends on the applicable national law

**Source quote.**

> A Legal Identity is an Identity to which rights and obligations are attributed based on the Applicable Law, in particular Natural and Legal persons.

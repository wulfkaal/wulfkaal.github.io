Claim. Nine superior labels set a high bar, so a real but modest effect may be reported as not positive.

Type. condition · Support. stated

Holds when

  • The bar follows from the confirmatory family's size and the multiplicity adjustment.
  • A non-positive reading therefore does not establish the absence of an effect.

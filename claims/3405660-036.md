# kaal:claim:3405660-036

**Claim.** The internal ratings based approach reduces information asymmetry because internal ratings capture supplementary borrower information that external credit assessors cannot reach and cover a broader range of borrowers.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- banks use the internal ratings based approach

**Source quote.**

> internal ratings may incorporate supplementary information about borrowers that is usually beyond the reach of institutions providing external credit assessments, and may cover a much broader range of borrowers

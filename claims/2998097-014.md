# kaal:claim:2998097-014

**Claim.** Because advisers and third party service providers can flatten out and sanitize the information disclosed in Forms ADV and PF, the resulting disclosures may be less useful to the FSOC and the SEC in determining the systemic risk posed by private funds.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- Forms ADV and PF disclosures
- level of sanitizing could not be verified in the study

# kaal:claim:6269518-016

**Claim.** Because policing protocol deviants is expensive, it is individually more efficient to assume others comply and skip policing, which yields a subgame perfect Nash equilibrium in which eventually fewer than half of members police and the system can be gamed.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- policing is costly to the individual
- members can free-ride on the policing of others

**Source quote.**

> This gives a subgame perfect Nash equilibrium leading to a situation where eventually less than 50% of the members actually perform the policing, meaning the system can be gamed.

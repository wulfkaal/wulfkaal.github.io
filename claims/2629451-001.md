# kaal:claim:2629451-001

**Claim.** Stock prices respond significantly and predictably in a positive direction to the DOJ press release announcing execution of a non- or deferred prosecution agreement and to the start of the N/DPA term.

**Type.** empirical  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- publicly traded U.S. firms that executed N/DPAs between 1993 and 2015
- event windows measured around the DOJ press release date and the start date of the N/DPA term

**Source quote.**

> We document a significant and predictable positive stock price response to the DOJ press release and the start of the N/DPA term.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, Timothy Lacine, *Stock Price Response to Non- and Deferred Prosecution Agreements* (2015), Abstract, page 2

# kaal:claim:6244278-001

**Claim.** Contemporary AI's incapacity for authentic judgment under irreducible uncertainty is an institutional deficit rather than a computational one: an agent that bears no consequence for its errors cannot develop genuine discernment, no matter how capable it becomes.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- applies to judgment under irreducible uncertainty, not to defined, verifiable tasks

**Source quote.**

> This Article argues the limitation is institutional, not computational: agents bearing no consequence for error cannot develop genuine discernment.

kaal:claim:3402701-019

Selling currency into a reserve when price is above the peg and buying it back with the reserve when price is below the peg yields an arbitrage profit, so a reserve can fund its own defense of the peg.

Source quote, verbatim
In the next paragraph we demonstrate the obvious result that selling the currency when the price is high to build a reserve, then buying the currency back with the reserve when the price is low will yield an arbitrage profit

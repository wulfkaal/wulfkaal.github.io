# kaal:claim:3067615-028

**Claim.** On bankruptcy or termination of the platform, token holders typically have no liquidity preference and no recourse at all once debt holders and outside creditors are satisfied, so unlike a venture capital seed investor with at least a simple liquidity preference, they typically lose everything they invested.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- bankruptcy or termination of the promoter's business or platform

# kaal:claim:4755632-009

**Claim.** Existing centralized micro task marketplaces cannot adequately fulfill the growing demand for high quality labeled AI training data, because their cost structure requires intermediation, which produces significant overpricing without any of that price reaching the micro task workers.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- applies to centralized micro task marketplaces that necessitate intermediation

**Source quote.**

> the cost structure for micro task work in centralized systems that necessitate intermediation results in significant overpricing without benefiting the micro task workers directly.

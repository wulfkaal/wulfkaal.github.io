# kaal:claim:2922176-031

**Claim.** The authors stipulate a category of director, the feedback provider, defined as a director appointed to supply management with unmediated and relevant input from the market, which is what a firm needs in order to plan for continued relevancy.

**Type.** definitional  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- directors with technologist, entrepreneurial, or academic backgrounds

**Source quote.**

> Successful companies have included a diverse range of individuals who are expected to assist management by providing unmediated and relevant input from the market. Such market feedback is highly necessary to identify a plan to remain relevant in the future.

**From.** Mark Fenwick, Wulf A. Kaal, Erik P. M. Vermeulen, *The ‘Unmediated’ and ‘Tech-Driven’ Corporate Governance of Today's Winning Companies* (2017), V.2. Unmediated Board of Directors, page 40

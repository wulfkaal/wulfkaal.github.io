kaal:claim:3782216-032

Determining what fraction of a currency is hot money is necessary for efficiently defending its stability: overestimating the hot money ratio makes the currency costlier to use, and underestimating it makes the currency insecure.

Source quote, verbatim
Therefore, determining the fraction of a currency that is hot money is necessary for efficient defense of its stability. A currency that overestimates this hot money ratio will cost more to use. A currency that underestimates the ratio will be insecure.

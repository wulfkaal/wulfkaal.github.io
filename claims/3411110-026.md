# kaal:claim:3411110-026

**Claim.** Near instant settlement creates its own regulatory problem: there is no practical ability to correct trade errors when settlement is same day, whereas the T+2 or T+3 structure supplies a correction window even as its delay causes other trading violations.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- same day or near instant settlement compared with T+2 and T+3

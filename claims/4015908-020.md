# kaal:claim:4015908-020

**Claim.** Without significant marketing a fair launch token is less likely to reach a diverse set of market participants, and projects reaching only a few hundred investors with small million dollar market caps are much more prone to abuse and manipulation.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- token launches with limited marketing reach

**Source quote.**

> Without significant marketing of the project, it is less likely the token will reach a diverse set of market participants. Projects that only reach a few hundred investors and only small million-dollar market caps are much more prone to abuse and manipulation.

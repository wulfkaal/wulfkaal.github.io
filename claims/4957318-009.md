**Claim.** Sunset regulation has fallen out of wide use, and the author attributes this primarily to the administrative burden and political inertia of regularly reviewing and repealing laws, not to any defect in the concept itself.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Source quote.**

> Sunset regulations, which involve the removal of outdated or less relevant legal provisions over time, are no longer widely used due to several factors.

# kaal:claim:3782214-027

**Claim.** The two sources of instability require different remedies and must not be swapped: dynamic rules are a mismatch for instability caused by the profit motive, and a reputational system is not an efficient method for attenuating instability caused by a group's diverse values.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** asserted

**Source quote.**

> Trying to institute dynamic rules to attenuate the instability due to a for-profit motive is a mismatch. Similarly instituting a reputational system is not an efficient method for attenuating the instability due to a group's diverse values.

**From.** Craig Calcaterra, Wulf A. Kaal, *Decentralized Governance* (2021), Reputation counters instability from profit motive, page 12

kaal:claim:2317580-037

Because CIAs have so far been used predominantly in the health care industry, and because that industry has a public or quasi-public good character, the application of CIAs outside health care could be limited, and without broader application their impact on corporate law may be limited as well.

Source quote, verbatim
Given the public good or quasi-public good character of the health care industry, the application of CIAs in industries outside of health care could be limited. Without broader application in other industries, the impact of CIAs on corporate law may be limited.

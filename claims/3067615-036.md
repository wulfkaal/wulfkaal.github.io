# kaal:claim:3067615-036

**Claim.** The Gnosis ICO shows how a reverse dutch auction can mitigate the inefficiencies and risks of a capped sale: the offering was capped at $12.5 million and the time needed to complete the sale determined the quantity of tokens distributed to investors, with the rest held by the start-up team.

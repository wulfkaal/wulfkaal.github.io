# kaal:claim:3128900-045

**Claim.** Centralized platforms cannot simply drop identity verification, because they need it to keep malicious actors from abusing the network and to ensure work quality; this is the tradeoff a staking based design is meant to dissolve.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

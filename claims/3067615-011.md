# kaal:claim:3067615-011

**Claim.** Adjusting the commercial benefits attached to a token operates as a quasi fiscal policy tool: increasing those benefits raises aggregate demand for a given token supply and can offset the depreciating effect of a large supply in circulation.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- tokens carrying discounts or other usage-linked commercial benefits

**Source quote.**

> First, the increase in commercial benefits associated with a token heightens the aggregate demand of the given token supply. Second, commercial benefits associated with a token issuance can help offset depreciated supply scarcity

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, Marco Dell'Erba, *Initial Coin Offerings Emerging Practices, Risk Factors, and Red Flags* (2017), II.3 Crypto Economics, page 11

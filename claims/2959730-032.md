# kaal:claim:2959730-032

**Claim.** Making the blockchain enabled per transaction fee publicly available lets a private fund adviser set the applicable fee against a competitive market, so that investors in transaction heavy strategies agree upfront to higher fees.

**Type.** design  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- per transaction fee is published or agreed with clients upfront
- fee varies with strategy transaction intensity

**Source quote.**

> It also can be publicly available which allows the private fund adviser to determine the applicable fee in a competitive market.65 Accordingly, clients who invest in a more transaction-prone strategy will be able to agree upfront to higher fees

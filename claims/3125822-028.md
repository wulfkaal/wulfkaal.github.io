# kaal:claim:3125822-028

**Claim.** For decentralized dispute resolution to deliver certainty of outcomes, the arbiter's power to disburse the assets of a contract, once triggered, must be preeminent over the parties.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- applies to smart contracts with a break clause and a third party arbiter

**Source quote.**

> s power to disburse the assets of the contract between the other parties, once triggered, must be preeminent in order to ensure certainty of outcomes.

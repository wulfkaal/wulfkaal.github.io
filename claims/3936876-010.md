# kaal:claim:3936876-010

**Claim.** A private key differs from a password in that it is immutable and cannot be reset, so if the key is lost or stolen access to the digital asset may be permanently lost.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- applies to key based digital asset ownership on public blockchains

**Source quote.**

> However, private keys are distinguishable from a password in that a private key is immutable – it cannot be reset.82 If a user loses their private key or if the private key is stolen, access to the digital asset may be lost.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, Hayley Howe, *Custody of Digital Assets* (2021), II.2 Custody Requirements for Digital Assets, page 15

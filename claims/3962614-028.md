# kaal:claim:3962614-028

**Claim.** If reputation whales stake reputation on a project and the market commits the capital for that stake, the whales should still receive part of the ROI because it is their reputation that is at stake, and they are not required to commit capital or to support capital calls with liquidity.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- whale reputation that has become meaningful through a track record

**Source quote.**

> If whales stake but the market commits for their stake with capital, whales should still have the right to receive (part of) the ROI because it is their reputation that is at stake. Whales are not required to commit capital and don't have to support capital calls with liquidity.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *REPUTATION AS CAPITAL – How Decentralized Autonomous Organizations Address Shortcomings in the Ventu* (2021), IV. Reputation as Capital, page 15

# kaal:claim:4957318-007

**Claim.** Web2 platform architecture is a causal contributor to distrust in legal institutions: centralized models driven by engagement metrics prioritize sensationalism over nuanced discourse, which accelerates misinformation and echo chambers and magnifies public distrust.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- centralized, engagement optimized social media platforms
- where citizens use social media as a substitute for formal legal channels

**Source quote.**

> This design incentivizes the rapid spread of both information and misinformation, often amplifying extreme viewpoints and fostering echo chambers. As a result, these platforms can magnify public distrust in legal institutions

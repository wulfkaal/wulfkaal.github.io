# kaal:claim:3266953-025

**Claim.** New reputation tokens are minted in every validation pool for every block, so block production is strongly encouraged through a larger share for the successful producer while policing is only gently encouraged through a shared allocation to all active members.

**Type.** design  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- every block and its validation pool

**Source quote.**

> Active participation is encouraged because new reputation tokens are minted in every validation pool for every block. So block production is strongly encouraged (because a greater percentage of the new tokens are given to a successful producer) and policing is gently encouraged

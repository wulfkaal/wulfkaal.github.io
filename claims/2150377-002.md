# kaal:claim:2150377-002

**Claim.** The immediate deregistration of hedge fund advisers following Goldstein v. SEC is revealed-preference evidence of the industry's opposition to registration and disclosure requirements, not merely a technical response to the vacatur.

**Type.** empirical  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- advisers who had registered under the SEC's 2004 rule
- 2006, immediately after the Goldstein decision

**Source quote.**

> The advisers' decision to deregister in 2006 seems to confirm the industry's opposition to registration and disclosure requirements.

kaal:claim:1998455-013

Converting contingent capital securities too late makes the capital injection superfluous, because by that stage the institution may face unresolvable difficulties that a capital injection can only marginally soften, and conversion may not suffice once the institution has entered resolution.

On the other hand, converting CCS too late could make the capital injection superfluous. At that stage, the financial institu- tion may have experienced unresolvable financial difficulties that may only marginally be softened with a capital injection.

Claim. Refundable collateral does not expand, and can contract, the harm an agent can answer for.

Type. mechanism · Support. argued

Holds when

  • Conditional on the single-agent, deterministic, stationary model of Part V.A, with an agent whose only assets are its earnings and its record.
  • The conclusion is limited to collateral that the claimant cannot reach.

Source quote

Refundable collateral therefore does not expand, and can contract, the harm an agent can answer for.

From. Wulf A. Kaal, Verification as Identity: Accountability, Personhood and the Scarcity of Verification in the Agent Economy (2026), page 10. Full text.

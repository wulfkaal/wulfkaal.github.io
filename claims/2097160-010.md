# kaal:claim:2097160-010

**Claim.** Because the Barclays award falls away rather than converting, it does not create a fixed claim giving managers a stake in the firm's liquidation value, and therefore it does not lower agency cost.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- contingent capital awards that lapse instead of converting

**Source quote.**

> it does not create a fixed claim for managers with a stake in the firm's liquidation value because it falls away when converted.141 Barclays's CCA, therefore, also does not lower agency cost.

# kaal:claim:4015908-019

**Claim.** Because pre-public token allocations to cover start-up costs run against the majority view on fair launches, preserving the spirit of fair launch requires that such allocations be fully transparent, announced to the community, and open to a community audit of the books.

**Type.** design  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- project allocates tokens before the public launch to cover startup costs

**Source quote.**

> However, in order to maintain the spirit of fair launch, such token allocations before the public launch should be fully transparent and announced to the community with a community audit of the books, as desired.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Fair Token Launch* (2022), III.2.c(5)(a) Start-up Expenses, page 10

# kaal:claim:2447306-029

**Claim.** SEC flexibility helps filers through a specific mechanism: it authorizes advisers to apply their own internal methodologies when interpreting and answering Form PF questions and to state their own assumptions, rather than forcing them onto an unfamiliar measurement basis.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- responses to follow-up survey Question 7a(i)

**Source quote.**

> the most significant benefits of SEC flexibility were that respondents believed they were authorized to use their internal methodologies to interpret and respond to questions, and to articulate their assumptions

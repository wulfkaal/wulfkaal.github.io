# kaal:claim:6607458-023

**Claim.** Proposition 2: if the composite best-response map is a contraction under an appropriate metric, the recursive equilibrium is unique and iteration of the map from any starting point converges to it, by the Banach fixed-point theorem.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- requires the composite best-response map to be a contraction under an appropriate metric

**Source quote.**

> Suppose the composite best-response map Ψ is a contraction on the product space of generation functions and domain models under an appropriate metric. Then there exists a unique recursive equilibrium, and the sequence (G_t, M_t) generated by repeated application of Ψ from any starting point

# kaal:claim:6607458-033

**Claim.** Without a verification record accessible to counterparties, generative markets suffer a generative-quality failure analogous to the lemons problem: counterparties price on the distribution of observed qualities, which systematically underprices high-quality generators.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- holds when the quality of a generative distribution is unobservable to counterparties

**Source quote.**

> The computative analog is a generative-quality failure: when the quality of a generative distribution is unobservable, counterparties price on the distribution of observed qualities, which systematically underprices high-quality generators.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Computative Economics A Framework for Economic Analysis under Computational Abundance* (2026), IX.B. Reputation-Based Verification, page 25

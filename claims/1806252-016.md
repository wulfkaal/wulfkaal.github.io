# kaal:claim:1806252-016

**Claim.** Even if hedge fund investing does have systemic implications, systemic risk is multifaceted enough that addressing it could require more than one regulator in a single jurisdiction, so the SEC alone may be unable to accomplish the task.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

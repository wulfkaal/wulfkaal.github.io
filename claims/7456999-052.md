# kaal:claim:7456999-052

**Claim.** The Fisher-information comparison lacks a common observation model, and rationality plus the stated non-babbling and positive-information conditions do not establish a strict ordering.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** asserted

**Holds when.**

- the open empirical question on Fisher information

**Source quote.**

> Rationality alone does not support the proposed strict ordering. An empirical comparison would need a common observation model for RLHF and RLRF, an informative Trial round that excludes babbling, and a debate channel with positive conditional information. These are necessary assumptions. This paper does not show that they are sufﬁcient.

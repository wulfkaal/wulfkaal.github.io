# kaal:claim:3949098-007

**Claim.** The more reputation replaces capital, the less capital must be allocated, tied and encumbered, which raises the ratio of unencumbered capital available to be newly deployed.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- reputation is encumbered and tied to an asset in place of capital

**Source quote.**

> The more reputation replaces capital the less capital has to be allocated, tied, and encumbered. In turn, the ratio of capital becoming unencumbered through reputation increases the deployment of capital ratio, e.g. a higher proportion of unencumbered capital can be newly encumbered.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Reputation as Capital – How DAOs Upgrade Finance* (2021), II. Basic Concept, 2. Liquidity, page 7

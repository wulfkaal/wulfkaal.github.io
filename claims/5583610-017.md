# kaal:claim:5583610-017

**Claim.** LER airdrops fall outside SEC proxy solicitation rules because a neutral pro rata distribution to all verified long-term holders, not conditioned on voting conduct, is not an inducement to vote.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- distribution is neutral and pro rata
- reward not conditioned on how the holder votes

**Source quote.**

> LER is not subject to SEC proxy solicitation rules because LER does not constitute an inducement to vote.

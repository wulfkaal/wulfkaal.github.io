# kaal:claim:1765901-024

**Claim.** There is a substantial likelihood that U.S. courts will read the Dodd-Frank Act as giving some extraterritorial effect to SEC and DOJ suits, so Morrison cannot be expected to survive intact in actions brought under Section 929P(b).

**Type.** predictive  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

**Source quote.**

> There is substantial likelihood that U.S. courts could find that Congress has provided for some extraterritorial effect for SEC and DOJ suits in the Dodd-Frank Act and that therefore Morrison cannot be upheld in its entirety in suits by the SEC or the DOJ under Section 929P(b).

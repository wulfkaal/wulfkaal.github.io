# kaal:claim:2029983-015

**Claim.** Geographic tests create the risk of a no man's land transaction: defendants may persuade the courts of every jurisdiction that the transaction took place outside their borders, leaving the transaction governed by no law and with no available forum.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- transactions whose geographic location is ambiguous
- courts in all candidate jurisdictions apply geographic tests

**Source quote.**

> Alternatively, defendants might successfully persuade courts in all jurisdictions that a transaction took place outside their borders or for some other reason outside their jurisdictions. Such a "no man's land" transaction would be governed by no law and there would be no forum.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, Richard W. Painter, *Forum Competition and Choice of Law Competition in Securities Law after Morrison v. National Austral* (2012), II. Forum Competition After Morrison, page 26

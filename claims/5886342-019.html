kaal:claim:5886342-019

A good faith acquirer of a digital object from an unauthorized transferor obtains protected digital ownership, but is obliged for five years after the transfer to retransfer that ownership to the initial owner against compensation of the purchase price or value.

Holds when

  • transferee received the object in good faith
  • obligation lasts five years after the transfer

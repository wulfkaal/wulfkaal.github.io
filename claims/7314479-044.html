Source quote, verbatim
Reputation is meaningful only where identity is expensive to replace. This does not require legal identity, and the confusion of the two has retarded the field considerably. It requires only that the accumulated standing be more valuable than the cost of starting again, which is a condition a system can create rather than inherit.

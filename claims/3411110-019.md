# kaal:claim:3411110-019

**Claim.** A blockchain based offering routed through a single broker produces a specific cluster of risks, including limited price discovery, no market making and therefore less liquidity, constrained short selling, impaired best execution, and no print to a tape or national exchange.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- offering structures with only one authorized broker-dealer
- single non-scalable ATS

**Source quote.**

> Several additional risks are associated with the limited trading and associated limited material liquidity that results from a blockchain-based offering involving only one broker.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, Samuel Evans, *Blockchain-Based Securities Offerings* (2019), IV.1 Limited Liquidity, page 19

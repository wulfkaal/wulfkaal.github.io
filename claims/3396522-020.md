# kaal:claim:3396522-020

**Claim.** Leverage offered by cryptocurrency exchanges worsens rather than cures the market's illiquidity, because borrowed money rather than genuine demand is driving the price.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- applies to exchanges offering five to ten times leverage on crypto trades

**Source quote.**

> Cryptocurrency exchanges such as Bitmax, Kraken, among others, offer 5-10x leverage for cryptocurrency trades. This exacerbates the problem of illiquidity as borrowed money is driving the price.

**From.** Craig Calcaterra, Wulf A. Kaal, Vadhindran K. Rao, *Stable Cryptocurrencies* (2019), IV. Stable Cryptocurrencies, 5. Transforming Cryptocurrency Market Structure, page 33

# kaal:claim:2337268-034

**Claim.** A person who provides investment advice over the Internet escapes registration only if the advice is impersonal; personalized advice delivered through chat rooms and websites can trigger the registration requirement.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- persons providing investment advice via Internet chat rooms and websites

**Source quote.**

> Registration is not required if the person provides impersonal investment advice.97

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Investment Adviser Regulation* (2013), 8. Internet Related Updates, page 20

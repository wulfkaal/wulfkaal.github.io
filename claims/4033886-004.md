# kaal:claim:4033886-004

**Claim.** The accounting definition of fair value may not apply to digital assets, with the consequence that correct valuation of digital assets for financial reporting purposes may be impossible.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- under existing accounting terminology such as FASB 157 and IFRS 13

**Source quote.**

> The definition of fair value under existing accounting terminology may not apply to digital assets.2 As a result, the correct valuation of digital assets may not be possible for financial reporting issues.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, Samuel Evans, Hayley Howe, *Digital Asset Valuation* (2022), I. Introduction, page 3

# kaal:claim:3782203-032

**Claim.** Cooperation is sustained only when the promise of future profits outweighs the present value of defecting, so a discount factor that is too low, or a game known to be ending soon, makes defection the rational choice.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- repeated games with end periods
- impatient players or high discounting

**Source quote.**

> Therefore, the promise of future profits must outweigh the present value to en- sure cooperation.

# kaal:claim:4033886-036

**Claim.** DCF appears at first glance unsuited to digital asset valuation because valuation is framed as pricing a token at a point in time, but it becomes applicable where networks return cash flows to token holders or to those who contribute work.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- networks that distribute cash flows to token holders or workers

**Source quote.**

> However, some digital asset networks do return cash flows to token holders or those who contribute work to the network. In such a case, DCF can shed light on the role of these structures in digital asset valuation.

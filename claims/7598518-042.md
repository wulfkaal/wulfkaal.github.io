# kaal:claim:7598518-042

**Claim.** The design names a failure pattern in advance: if the likelihood filter, the equal-weight pool, and the reputation pool each carry the negative label against full replacement in every family, the paper issues a registered sentence recording that each had lower capped retention at generation 50 than full replacement, as a registered negative result and not an implementation failure.

**Type.** design  **Support.** stated

**Holds when.**

- The sentence is available only on the stated pattern of labels in every family.
- Naming the pattern in advance is what distinguishes it from a post hoc explanation of a bad result.

**Source quote.**

> If the likelihood filter, the equal-weight pool, and the reputation pool each carry the negative label against full replacement in every family, the paper issues the registered failure-mode sentence: each had lower capped retention at generation 50 than full replacement. The design named that pattern in advance. It is a registered negative result, not an implementation failure.

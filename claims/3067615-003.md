# kaal:claim:3067615-003

**Claim.** ICOs cannot be qualified as donations and are therefore distinguishable from crowdfunding, because ICO participants acquire a financial stake in the company and, as the case may be, a right to vote on future decisions.

**Type.** definitional  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- comparison of ICOs to crowdfunding platforms such as Kickstarter

**Source quote.**

> Unlike crowdfunding, ICOs involve a financial stake in the company including, as the case may be, the right to vote on future decisions. Therefore, ICOs cannot be qualified as a donation.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, Marco Dell'Erba, *Initial Coin Offerings Emerging Practices, Risk Factors, and Red Flags* (2017), I. Introduction, page 4

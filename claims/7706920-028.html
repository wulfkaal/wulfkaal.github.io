Claim. Identity is necessary and not sufficient for accountability, because the earnings bound remains and an identity rule cannot make up for earnings the agent does not have.

Type. condition · Support. argued

Holds when

  • Conditional on the single-agent, deterministic, stationary model of Part V.A, with an agent whose only assets are its earnings and its record.

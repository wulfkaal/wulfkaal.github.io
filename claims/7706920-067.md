# kaal:claim:7706920-067

**Claim.** Accountable agents can sponsor other agents only within limits: under a shared fault a sponsor's capacity is eaten by the same slash each sub-agent's harm imposes on it, and the principal's exposure returns at the sponsor level.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- Conditional on the single-agent, deterministic, stationary model of Part V.A, with an agent whose only assets are its earnings and its record.
- Assumes equal harms, perfect correlation and compounding slashes.

**Source quote.**

> Proposition 5 therefore holds only within limits. Agents can sponsor agents, but under a shared fault a sponsor’s capacity is eaten by the same slash that each sub-agent’s harm imposes on it, and the principal’s exposure returns at the sponsor level.

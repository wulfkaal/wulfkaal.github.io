# kaal:claim:3402701-018

**Claim.** Because most of the value of contemporary cryptocurrencies comes from speculation on future uses that do not yet exist, the vast majority of cryptocurrency is technically hot money, and reserves are the best defense against the resulting price fluctuations.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- contemporary cryptocurrency markets dominated by speculation

**Source quote.**

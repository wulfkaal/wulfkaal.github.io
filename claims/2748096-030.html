The theoretical link between hedge funds and systemic risk rests on limits to arbitrage: speculators' capital is finite, their capacity to supply liquidity depends on funding, and that funding can dry up abruptly in periods of financial distress, producing liquidity spirals amplified by rising return correlations, higher volatility, and flight to quality.

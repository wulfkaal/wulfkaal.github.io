Claim. In Study 2, registered after the correction, all three registered hypotheses were rejected as nulls under Holm correction.

Type. empirical · Support. reported

Holds when

  • Study 2: five cells, 60 episodes per cell, 300 in all, none excluded.
  • Prompted language-model agents with no stake.

Source quote

The three registered hypotheses were all rejected as nulls under Holm correction: H4, honoring at (205, 10) exceeds honoring at (215, 10); H5, honoring at (215, 25) exceeds honoring at (215, 10); H6, honoring at (400, 25) exceeds honoring at (400, 10) (z = 10.95, 10.95, 10.77).

From. Wulf A. Kaal, Verification as Identity: Accountability, Personhood and the Scarcity of Verification in the Agent Economy (2026), page 17. Full text.

Claim. Reputation governance converts recursive self-improvement hazards by conditional relocation, not by a dissolution theorem.

Type. condition · Support. argued

Holds when

  • Depends on the portability of capability, the integrity of the governance gate, the quality and independence of verification, and agents' inability to form coalitions that internalize the institution.

Source quote

This is a conditional relocation, not a dissolution theorem.

# kaal:claim:617681-042

**Claim.** Unless the Member State of incorporation specifically provides in its corporate statute that arbitration is permissible when allowed in the charter or a shareholders agreement, investors run the risk that courts refuse to enforce the arbitration provision.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- closely held corporations may rely on a shareholders agreement instead of the charter

**Source quote.**

> specifically provide in its corporate statute that arbitration is permissible if allowed in either the corporate charter or (for a closely held corporation) in a shareholders' agreement. Otherwise, investors run the risk that courts refuse to enforce the arbitration provision.

**From.** Christian Kirchner, Richard W. Painter, Wulf A. Kaal, *Regulatory Competition in EU Corporate Law after Inspire Art Unbundling Delaware's Product for Euro* (2004), E.III.3 The Legal Standing of Arbitration in Europe, page 50

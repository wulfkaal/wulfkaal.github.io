# kaal:claim:2486570-018

**Claim.** Cooperation requirements appear in 91.10 percent of the sampled agreements, and within that category document production requirements, meaning the identification, assembly, organization, and production of relevant documentation for the Department of Justice, appear in 79.70 percent of agreements.

**Type.** empirical  **Support.** evidenced

**Source quote.**

> We find that 91.10 percent of the N/DPAs in the sample contained provisions relating to cooperation requirements. Further, 79.70 percent of N/DPAs in our sample contained cooperation requirements pertaining to "Documents."

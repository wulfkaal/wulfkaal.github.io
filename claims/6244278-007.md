# kaal:claim:6244278-007

**Claim.** In genuinely poorly defined contexts, where the task includes choosing what merits attention and committing resources under irreducible uncertainty, no scaffold can be pre-specified without collapsing the problem into a defined one; prompting therefore cannot close the judgment gap.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- task itself includes selecting what merits attention and living with irreversible consequences

**Source quote.**

> no equivalent scaffold can be pre-specified without collapsing the problem into a defined one.

# kaal:claim:4957318-033

**Claim.** In the WDAG system a legal precedent or rule that gains favor because it is effective automatically receives a higher weight, and that weight is set by user preferences and citations rather than by any legislative act.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** asserted

**Holds when.**

- where effectiveness is revealed through use

# kaal:claim:7456999-047

**Claim.** The data processing inequality cannot compare RLHF and RLRF because their labels arise from different observation mechanisms and no processing chain connects them.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- attempts to order RLHF and RLRF information using data processing

**Source quote.**

> The comparison with RLHF cannot be made by the data processing inequality. It orders two statistics of a common observation, and the scalar preference label is not a function of either RLRF transcript. They are outputs of different observation mechanisms applied to the same candidate output, and no processing chain connects them.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Reinforcement Learning from Reputation Feedback* (2026), III.D.3 Information-Theoretic Decomposition of the Signal, page 16

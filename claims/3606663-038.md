# kaal:claim:3606663-038

**Claim.** Conducting monetary policy through a central bank digital currency imposes a requirement on the technology layer: the underlying network protocol must enable the central bank to adjust the money supply at will and to act as lender of last resort with access to unlimited supply.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- CBDC built on a blockchain style protocol
- central bank intends to conduct monetary policy through the CBDC

**Source quote.**

> Moreover, in order to be able to conduct monetary policy with CBDC, the underlying network protocol would have to enable the central bank to adjust the money supply at will.

kaal:claim:3125827-005

Because the stakes in SPoS are reputation tokens that are far less fungible than cryptocurrency stakes, long term probity is incentivized and many short term arbitrage opportunities are eliminated. Fungibility of the staked asset is what makes short horizon attacks profitable in other proof of stake systems.

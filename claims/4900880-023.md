# kaal:claim:4900880-023

**Claim.** Workplace trust is a condition for technological change to empower employees: a one percentage point rise in technological change is associated with a 0.23 percentage point rise in perceived empowerment where trust exists, against only 0.10 percentage points where it does not.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- workplaces with and without established trust

**Source quote.**

> A one percentage point rise in technological change is associated with a 0.23 percentage point rise in perceived empowerment in workplaces with trust, compared to only a 0.10 percentage point rise in workplaces without much trust.

# kaal:claim:2317580-024

**Claim.** Although CIAs are not laws, they go beyond aspirational governance standards because they set forth concrete governance rules that more clearly define the duties to be informed, to exercise oversight, and to maintain effective reporting systems, thereby requiring a higher standard of care.

**Type.** design  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

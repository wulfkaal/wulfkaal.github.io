# kaal:claim:1428387-022

**Claim.** Moral hazard is worsened when the financial products traded are so complex that the agents, mostly on the buy side, do not entirely understand them and trade for the principal on the basis of incomplete and asymmetric information.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

**Source quote.**

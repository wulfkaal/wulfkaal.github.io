Claim. Component records scored separately for the agent, the harness and the model let a failure be attributed to its source and raise the cost of evasion, but they multiply the linkage decisions, each with its own error rates.

Type. mechanism · Support. argued

Source quote

Component records, scored separately for the agent, the harness and the model, let a failure be attributed to its source, and they raise the cost of evasion, because an evasive successor must change all three to escape. They also multiply the number of linkage decisions, each with its own error rates.

From. Wulf A. Kaal, Verification as Identity: Accountability, Personhood and the Scarcity of Verification in the Agent Economy (2026), page 8. Full text.

# kaal:claim:3411897-002

**Claim.** Decentralized systems have no central intelligence or hierarchical leadership; intelligence is instead spread throughout the system, and information filters in at the edges where the action is and where most real time information is created.

**Type.** definitional  **Support.** asserted

**Holds when.**

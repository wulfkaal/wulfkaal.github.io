# kaal:claim:6269518-023

**Claim.** Validators correcting citations should be permitted only to increase outward citations and never to decrease them, because a power to decrease would enable collusion to help allies under-cite competitors.

**Type.** design  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- two phase citation submission with validator correction authority

**Source quote.**

> In the second phase, validators can increase citations to others if they detect under-citation. Critically, validators can only increase outward citations. They cannot decrease them. Thus, preventing collusion to help allies under-cite competitors.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Citation Honesty Mechanisms in Weighted Directed Acyclic Graph Governance* (2026), IV.D. Solution 4: Mandatory Citation Minimums with Validator Override, page 11

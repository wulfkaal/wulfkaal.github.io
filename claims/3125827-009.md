# kaal:claim:3125827-009

**Claim.** Even though proof of stake mitigates the economy of scale advantage, block production cartels can still arise in PoS systems through lotteries, through built in voting delegations, or because the stakes required to be a block producer can simply be bought.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- PoS systems that use lotteries, delegation, or purchasable stakes

**Source quote.**

> However, block production cartels may still arise due to lotteries, or the fact that voting delegations are built into the system, or the fact that the stakes needed to be a block producer may be bought

# kaal:claim:6607458-035

**Claim.** Coordination in computative settings runs through the interaction of generation functions rather than through a central coordinator, and this decentralization is structural rather than optional, since a central coordinator would itself be a computative agent generating an infinite regress.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Source quote.**

> The generative network is decentralized in the sense that coordination occurs through the interaction of generation functions rather than through a central coordinator. This property is structural rather than optional.

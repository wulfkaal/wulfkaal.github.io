# kaal:claim:3782210-014

**Claim.** Reputation must be staked and risked on votes over work evidence, because otherwise the system suffers the Tragedy of the Commons nothing-at-stake problem; staking ensures every expert is motivated to police every reputation-staked action in order to protect the value of their own investment.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- validation pool voting on work evidence

**Source quote.**

> To avoid the Tragedy of the Commons (the nothing-at- stake problem, "skin in the game"), reputation must be staked and risked with votes on work evidence. This ensures all experts are motivated to police every reputation- staked action, to protect the value of their investment.

# kaal:claim:2811718-020

**Claim.** Deficient due diligence does not create securities fraud liability unless it is intentional or highly reckless; conduct that is merely negligent or professionally incompetent falls short of the scienter requirement.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- securities fraud claims under section 10(b) and Rule 10b-5
- PSLRA pleading standard

**Source quote.**

> Courts hold that the lack of due diligence, beyond merely being negligent or just professionally incompetent, must be intentional or highly reckless.

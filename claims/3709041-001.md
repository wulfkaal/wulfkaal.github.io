# kaal:claim:3709041-001

**Claim.** Centralized coordination of behavior for the common good risks undermining individual effort, whereas blockchain technology's autonomous and anonymous decentralized coordination of individual action can deliver common good outcomes without suppressing individualism and its welfare enhancing effects.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

# kaal:claim:3652481-031

**Claim.** Reputation based staking removes the corruptive elements of fungible tokens because a third party is less likely to be able to take over a non fungible asset such as reputation that was organically grown and maintained through actual expertise in the DAO's subject matter.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- reputation is non fungible and expertise based

**Source quote.**

> The corruptive elements of fungible assets/tokens are removed because third parties are less likely able to take over a non-fungible asset, such as reputation, that is organically grown and maintained through actual expertise in a given DAO subject matter.

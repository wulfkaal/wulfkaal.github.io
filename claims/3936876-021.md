# kaal:claim:3936876-021

**Claim.** Cold wallets typically lack the multisig feature that is common in hot storage, so the single employee acting as agent for a corporation may be the only access point to the corporate digital assets.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- corporate or treasury holdings in cold storage without multisig

**Source quote.**

> Accordingly, the person who acts as an agent to a corporation who holds the assets on behalf of the company may be the only access point to the corporate assets.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, Hayley Howe, *Custody of Digital Assets* (2021), II.2.b Cold Storage, page 18

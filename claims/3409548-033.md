# kaal:claim:3409548-033

**Claim.** The legacy private investment fund model delays liquidity: the investment process is long, complex and time intensive and leads up to a very late liquidity event in the form of an IPO or acquisition, which is why funds seek the early liquidity cryptocurrencies provide.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- private investment funds investing in blockchain start-ups

**Source quote.**

> In the existing legacy business for private investment funds, the investment process is subject to long, complex, and time intensive processes leading up to a very late liquidity event in the form of an IPO or acquisition.

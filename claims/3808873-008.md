# kaal:claim:3808873-008

**Claim.** Proof of Work block rewards structurally reintroduce centralization: the economic nature of mining rewards incentivizes degrees of centralization through collective action of nodes that share rewards as a group, that is, mining pools.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- Proof of Work consensus with block rewards
- Bitcoin in the early 2020s

**Source quote.**

> The economic nature of these rewards incentivizes degrees of centralization in collective action of nodes that share in the mining rewards as a group, so called mining pools.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Decentralization Neutralizers* (2021), II. Degrees of Decentralization, page 8

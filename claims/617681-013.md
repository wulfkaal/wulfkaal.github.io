# kaal:claim:617681-013

**Claim.** To the extent adjudication over U.K. corporate law is expected to take place outside the U.K., the U.K. has not in fact exported a bundled product; its users are demonstrating a preference for an unbundled package.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- small closely held enterprises incorporating in the U.K. after Inspire Art

**Source quote.**

> To the extent adjudication over U.K. corporate law is expected to be carried out somewhere other than the U.K. the U.K. has not exported a bundled product at all.

**From.** Christian Kirchner, Richard W. Painter, Wulf A. Kaal, *Regulatory Competition in EU Corporate Law after Inspire Art Unbundling Delaware's Product for Euro* (2004), D. Barriers to Entry for Member States Offering Bundled Corporate Law, page 24

# kaal:claim:4021599-014

**Claim.** For digital assets the decisive element of the Howey analysis is whether the purchaser of the token has a reasonable expectation of profits derived from the efforts of others, which the SEC divides into reliance on the efforts of others and reasonable expectation of profits.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- SEC framework for investment contract analysis of digital assets

**Source quote.**

> purchaser of such a token has a reasonable expectation of profits derived from others.

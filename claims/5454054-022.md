# kaal:claim:5454054-022

**Claim.** The absence of fiat par-redemption combined with limited acceptance inside merchant ecosystems makes LER rewards closed-loop utilities, which is what removes their classification as electronic money tokens or securities.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- no redemption at par in fiat
- acceptance confined to qualifying merchants

**Source quote.**

> Third, the absence of fiat par-redemption and limited acceptance within merchant ecosystems positions LER voucher loyalty rewards as closed-loop utilities. This removes their classification as electronic money tokens (EMTs) or securities, which, in turn, simplifys compliance.

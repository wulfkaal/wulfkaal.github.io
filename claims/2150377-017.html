kaal:claim:2150377-017

A treatment and control design is unavailable for studying registration effects, because managers who did not have to register have no exposure to the treatment and therefore cannot generate informative responses about its effects.

Source quote, verbatim
Interviewing the treatment group, hedge fund managers who had to register, and a control group, hedge fund managers who did not have to register, would not have yielded appropriate responses because the control group would have had no exposure to the effect of the treatment, registration.
From

Wulf A. Kaal, Hedge Fund Manager Registration Under the Dodd-Frank Act (2012), C. Selection Bias

# kaal:claim:1558614-001

**Claim.** German banks' exposure to CDO risk ran through credit enhancement and liquidity guarantees given to off balance sheet conduits, and because that exposure was often kept out of their accounting the inherent risk only surfaced once the CDO market collapsed.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- banks guaranteeing conduits located outside German banking supervision
- guarantee exposure not consolidated into bank accounts

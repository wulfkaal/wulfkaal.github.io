Claim. Harms below the ratio of litigation cost to adjudication probability are never remedied however large the agent's capacity.

Type. failure · Support. argued

Holds when

  • Assumes the injured party bears litigation cost and a claim succeeds with a stated probability.
  • Stated for the model's assumptions; not calibrated to any market and not a claim about any legal system.

Source quote

Harms below f/a are never remedied however large the agent’s capacity.

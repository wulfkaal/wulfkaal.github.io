# kaal:claim:3799320-033

**Claim.** Member reputation is inflationary by design, so non staking of reputation tokens or non voting leads to value depreciation, which incentivizes action and makes liveness faults less likely.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- reputation that depreciates with non-use

**Source quote.**

> reputation is inflationary in the DAO of DAOs design. As such, non-use, i.e., non-staking of reputation tokens or non-voting, would lead to value depreciation, which incentivizes action and thus value enhancement. Liveness faults become less likely.

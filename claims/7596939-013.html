Claim. The Article resolves agent identity by legal stipulation rather than metaphysics: the legal subject is the registered record, continued by a recorded learning update within a prescribed granularity or by a recorded continuity transition, and only while the signing key stays within the registered configuration.

Type. design · Support. stated

Holds when

  • individuation of intelligent agents
  • recorded learning update within the prescribed granularity, or a recorded continuity transition
  • signing key remains within the registered configuration

Source quote

The Article fixes the legal subject by rule: it is the registered record.

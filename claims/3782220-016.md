# kaal:claim:3782220-016

**Claim.** Because what counts as essential shifts with the market, a network must not only identify its values but also maintain a dynamic governance process for constantly reinterpreting those values as circumstances change.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- changing market conditions

**Source quote.**

> Importantly, the decision of what is essential versus non-essential will change depending on the market. The system must have a dynamic governance process for constantly reinterpreting our values in the face of changing circumstances.

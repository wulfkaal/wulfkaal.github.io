# kaal:claim:7314479-025

**Claim.** Contribution must be traceable well enough to support payment, credit, or recurring compensation without a central assignor; a runtime that routes work without allocating value has externalized the hardest part of its own economics onto its participants.

**Type.** normative  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- runtimes with machine-to-machine settlement

**Source quote.**

> Contribution must be traceable well enough to support payment, credit, or recurring compensation without a central assignor. This requirement follows from the fourth defining property of the class. Once settlement is machine-to-machine, value allocation is a coordination problem rather than an accounting one, and a runtime that routes work without allocating value has externalized the hardest part of its own economics onto its participants.

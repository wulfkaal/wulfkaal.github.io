kaal:claim:2714974-033

A system in which hedge funds submit position information to an authority that aggregates and publishes it cannot address liquidity risk, because protecting proprietary information requires so much aggregation that the resulting information loses value to market participants.

Source quote, verbatim
Protection of proprietary information would require so much aggregation that the value of the information to market participants would be substantially reduced.

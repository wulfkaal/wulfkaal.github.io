# kaal:claim:3405401-017

**Claim.** Smart contracts leave counterparty information asymmetries unresolved: the tenant of a smart property cannot fully know whether the landlord is providing a worthy property, and the landlord cannot know in advance how likely the tenant is to destroy the premises.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- smart property rental scenario

**Source quote.**

> In our example above, the tenant of the smart property cannot fully know if the landlord is providing a worthy property. In turn, the landlord cannot know in advance how likely the tenant will destroy the premises.

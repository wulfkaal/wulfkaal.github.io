# kaal:claim:3373393-033

**Claim.** Non-performance reputational penalties in a DAO are entirely free from racial or cultural biases, because the token holders imposing them are unlikely to even know each other.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- DAOs where token holders are pseudonymous and mutually unknown

**Source quote.**

> Crucially, non-performance reputational penalties are entirely free from racial or cultural biases and associated implications as the token holders are unlikely to even know each other.

kaal:claim:7596939-009

Claim. The agent's exposure to destruction of its record is conditionally inalienable: no part of the lost salary stream can be transferred to a controller, so any third-party exposure must be financed from outside the record.

Type. mechanism · Support. argued

Holds when

  • Rules 1 and 4 including non-assignment
  • Lemma 3

Source quote

Any exposure a third party bears to the record's destruction must be supplied from outside the record: as capital, as sanction, or as a claim on someone else's assets.

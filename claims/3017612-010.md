# kaal:claim:3017612-010

**Claim.** MBCA Section 7.32 requires a writing but, departing from earlier statutes, does not require the agreement to sit in the articles or bylaws, so stand alone written shareholder agreements are expressly valid.

**Type.** definitional  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- MBCA Section 7.32 agreements

**Source quote.**

> The agreement must be in writing, but unlike some earlier statutes, the written agreement does not have to be in the articles or bylaws—stand-alone written agreements are specifically allowed.

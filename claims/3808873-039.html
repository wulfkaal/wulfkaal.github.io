kaal:claim:3808873-039

One token one vote on chain governance produces plutocracy: it allocates more power to holders of a significant share of supply so that majority token holders have more power than all other holders combined, reintroducing the defects of one share one vote legacy designs.

Source quote, verbatim
A one- token-one-vote design allocates more power to token holders who have a significant share of the total supply of a given token. Majority token holders have more power than the rest of the token holders combined.

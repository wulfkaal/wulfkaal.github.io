# kaal:claim:3406323-014

**Claim.** Centralized fiat payment systems require consumers to hold an existing banking relationship, while holding and storing cryptocurrencies does not, which gives decentralized payment systems comparative advantages in orders of magnitude.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- payment systems relying entirely on cryptocurrencies

**Source quote.**

> Centralized fiat payment systems and platforms typically require some form of an existing banking relationship in order for consumers to utilize their services. Holding and storing cryptocurrencies does not require a banking relationship.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Decentralization - A Primer on the New Economy* (2019), IV. Opportunities, page 9

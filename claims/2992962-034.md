# kaal:claim:2992962-034

**Claim.** Without judge expertise in the subject matter of a smart contract dispute, user confidence in effective and fair conflict resolution is undermined, which reduces confidence in crypto transactions as a whole and can undermine the evolution of the crypto economy.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- dispute resolution systems that select judges at random from all users, such as Aragon

**Source quote.**

> Without judge's expertise in a given smart contract subject matter of a dispute user confidence in effective and fair conflict resolution is undermined which leads to overall less confidence in crypto transactions as a whole and can undermine the evolution of the crypto economy.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, Craig Calcaterra, *Crypto Transaction Dispute Resolution* (2017), 3. Limitations of Existing Solutions, page 52

# kaal:claim:3782201-039

**Claim.** People participate more willingly with decentralized apps when their information remains under their personal control, and zero knowledge proofs make exactly that possible by allowing complex information to be shared for social improvement while personal information stays controlled.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

**Source quote.**

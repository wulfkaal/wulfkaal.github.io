# kaal:claim:2029983-008

**Claim.** Harmonization is politically unattainable absent either a central authority able to preempt the law of many jurisdictions or a single jurisdiction with enough economic clout to impose its rules on others, which makes harmonization especially difficult beyond national borders.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- cross border harmonization
- no supranational preemptive authority exists

**Source quote.**

> Second, harmonization is difficult to accomplish politi- cally unless there is a central authority that can preempt the law in a multitude of jurisdictions or, alternatively, a jurisdic- tion has so much economic clout that it can impose its rules on others.

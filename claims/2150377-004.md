# kaal:claim:2150377-004

**Claim.** Before Dodd-Frank the perimeter of hedge fund regulation was set by SEC no-action letters on client counting and by courts that gave very limited and sometimes contradictory guidance, so compliance rested on an unstable and uncertain base rather than on rules.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- the period between the 1968 Merrill Lynch action and the 1985 safe harbor rule
- advisers seeking to stay exempt from securities regulation

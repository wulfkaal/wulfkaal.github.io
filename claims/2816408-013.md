# kaal:claim:2816408-013

**Claim.** The quarterly Form PF reporting obligation imposed on advisers with more than $1.5 billion in regulatory assets under management attributable to private funds exists to give the FSOC timely data for identifying trends in systemic risk.

**Type.** design  **Support.** asserted

**Holds when.**

- advisers with RAUM above $1.5 billion attributable to private funds

**Source quote.**

> The quarterly reporting requirement for large private fund advisers is intended to provide timely data that enables the FSOC to identify trends in systemic risk (IA Release 3308).

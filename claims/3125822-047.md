# kaal:claim:3125822-047

**Claim.** Under the proposed weighting scheme a post that was initially downvoted can never yield its creator tokens even if expert opinion later reverses, an asymmetry the authors flag as a limitation of the core design.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** asserted

**Holds when.**

- applies to the weighted directed acyclic graph valuation scheme
- could be changed only by allowing negative valued tokens

# kaal:claim:3411110-022

**Claim.** Regulatory uncertainty produces a chilling effect: because early adopters must apply traditional law to novel instruments and cannot reach a comfortable level of certainty, legacy businesses hold back from adoption.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- current U.S. regulatory climate for tokens and digital securities

**Source quote.**

> As a result, there is a chilling effect over the industry where legacy businesses are weary to adopt because of regulatory uncertainty.

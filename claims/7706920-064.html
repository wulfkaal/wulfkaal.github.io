kaal:claim:7706920-064

Claim. Adding sponsors adds nothing when exit is cheap, because each agent in turn leaves when the obligation reaches it, and a sponsor's guarantee is worth the sum of its parts only where exit is costly.

Type. condition · Support. argued

Holds when

  • Conditional on the single-agent, deterministic, stationary model of Part V.A, with an agent whose only assets are its earnings and its record.

Source quote

Adding sponsors therefore adds nothing when exit is cheap, because each agent in turn leaves when the obligation reaches it. A sponsor’s guarantee is worth the sum of its parts only where exit is costly.

From. Wulf A. Kaal, Verification as Identity: Accountability, Personhood and the Scarcity of Verification in the Agent Economy (2026), page 13. Full text.

# kaal:claim:3808867-017

**Claim.** Majority rule in microdemocratic systems can produce discrimination against minorities, because a majority that is itself unaffected by a rule it instantiates can impose a discriminatory impact on a minority that is disproportionately affected.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- majority rule voting systems

**Source quote.**

> Similarly, majority rule may mean discrimination of the minority. A majority that is unaffected by a rule they instantiate may have a discriminatory impact on a minority that is disproportionately affected by the change in the rules.

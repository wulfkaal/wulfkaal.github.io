# kaal:claim:2470008-025

**Claim.** Expanding the uses of Form PF data remains difficult so long as there is insufficient confidence in the accuracy of what advisers report, notwithstanding SEC efforts to improve quality through interpretive FAQs and curative amendments.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- current state of Form PF data accuracy
- SEC remedial measures limited to FAQs and curative amendments

**Source quote.**

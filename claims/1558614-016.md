# kaal:claim:1558614-016

**Claim.** U.S. corporate law centers so heavily on shareholder manager conflicts of interest that, absent a demonstrable conflict, it treats risk taking as a situation where managers' and shareholders' interests are aligned and legal intervention is unwarranted.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

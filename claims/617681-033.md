# kaal:claim:617681-033

**Claim.** Forum unbundling is the arrangement in which the incorporation jurisdiction's statutes and interpretive case law are unbundled only from the adjudication services of its courts, so corporations may choose a different adjudicator in their charters.

**Type.** definitional  **Support.** asserted

**Source quote.**

> The second alternative is "forum unbundling" in which the incorporation jurisdiction's statutes and interpretive caselaw are unbundled from the adjudication services of its courts. Corporations may in their charters choose an adjudicator other than the courts of the state of incorporation.

**From.** Christian Kirchner, Richard W. Painter, Wulf A. Kaal, *Regulatory Competition in EU Corporate Law after Inspire Art Unbundling Delaware's Product for Euro* (2004), E. Alternatives to Bundled Corporate Law of a Single Member State, page 35

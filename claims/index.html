kaal corpus claim layer

5288 atomic, individually citable claims from 132 published works.

Every claim carries a verbatim quote from a content hashed source PDF, scope conditions, a citation string, and a permanent URL. 2080 of them document how a design, mechanism, or proposed remedy fails: see the failure mode index.

Machine access

  • index.json, the full index
  • graph.jsonld, schema.org Dataset
  • authority.json, coverage by topic with evidence
  • Single claim: /claims/<id>.json, .md, .html
  • Entity index, concept nodes over the claim layer

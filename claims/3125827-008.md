# kaal:claim:3125827-008

**Claim.** Given that Byzantine faults are inevitable in a distributed system, the known impossibility results force any consensus protocol to hedge: it can promise only a probability of finality and security, and that probability is inversely proportional to liveness and speed.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- asynchronous distributed networks
- presence of Byzantine faults

**Source quote.**

> Given the inevitable possibility of Byzantine faults in a distributed system, such results require any protocol to hedge, promising only a probability of finality and security inversely proportional to liveness and speed.

**From.** Craig Calcaterra, Wulf A. Kaal, *Secure Proof of Stake Protocol* (2018), 2.1 Hard theoretical limits, page 7

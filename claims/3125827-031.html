kaal:claim:3125827-031

Running the reputation platform on the very blockchain it validates destabilizes the system because it increases the likelihood of forks: every validation pool result is itself a transaction that must be included in a future block requiring its own validation, without end. The authors judge this destabilization non catastrophic for most imagined uses.

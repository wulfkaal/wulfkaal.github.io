# kaal:claim:2714974-021

**Claim.** Until the SEC finalizes verification rules and gives a clear, comprehensive definition of the reasonable steps an issuer must take, issuers cannot determine at the time of sale whether their verification attempts made general solicitation permissible.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- conditional on the absence of finalized SEC verification guidance

**Source quote.**

> Without appropriate SEC guidance, issuers will have difficulty ascertaining if their verification attempts at the time of the sale of securities made GSGA permissible.

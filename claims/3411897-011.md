**Claim.** A market leader's lack of investment in disruptive technologies often causes abrupt loss of market dominance and even total replacement, because market leaders shortsightedly refuse to cannibalize their own dominance through disruptive technologies.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Source quote.**

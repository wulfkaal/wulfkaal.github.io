# kaal:claim:2732915-001

**Claim.** Five years after the Dodd-Frank Act, the private fund industry is most affected by the uncertainty and the higher costs the Act generates, yet on multiple metrics the industry is coping well with the evolving post Dodd-Frank regulatory landscape.

**Type.** empirical  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- survey of a population of 1267 registered investment advisers, N=69 respondents
- United States, post Title IV registration

**Source quote.**

> The findings in this study suggest that the industry is mostly affected by the uncertainty and higher costs associated with the Act, but under multiple metrics the industry appears to be coping well overall with the evolving post Dodd- Frank Act regulatory landscape.

# kaal:claim:2389423-012

**Claim.** Title IV mandates hedge fund adviser registration in order to increase record keeping and disclosure, requiring advisers above the statutory AUM threshold to register as investment advisers and to disclose information about their trades and portfolios to the SEC.

**Type.** definitional  **Support.** asserted

**Holds when.**

- hedge fund advisers above the Title IV registration threshold

**Source quote.**

> Title IV mandates hedge fund adviser registration to increase record-keeping and disclosure (Dodd-Frank § 408). Hedge fund advisers with more than $150 AUM are required to register as investment advisers and have to disclose information about their trades and portfolios to the SEC

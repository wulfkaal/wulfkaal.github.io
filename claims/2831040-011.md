# kaal:claim:2831040-011

**Claim.** Introducing a regulatory update where genuine regulatory reform is needed can deteriorate the relationship between regulation and innovation, because innovation driven by changing values and societal norms calls for reform rather than incremental updating.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- innovation driven by shifting values and societal norms

**Source quote.**

> If changing values and societal norms are driving innovation, calling for a regulatory reform, introducing a regulatory update where regulatory reform is needed can deteriorate the relationship between regulation and innovation

# kaal:claim:4015908-036

**Claim.** No more than 40% of total token supply should be auctioned in a fair launch, with 10% serving as a buffer paired with raised funds as permanently locked DEX liquidity and the other 50% allocated to a DEX pair with the raise proceeds to provide long-term locked liquidity.

**Type.** design  **Support.** asserted

**Holds when.**

- public auction of tokens in a fair launch context

**Source quote.**

> it is ill advised to auction more than 40% of the total token supply. 10% of token supply should serve as a buffer. paired with the funds raised to form permanently locked liquidity on a DEX. The other 50% should be allocated to a DEX in a pair with the funds from the token raise.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Fair Token Launch* (2022), IV.3 Auction, page 17

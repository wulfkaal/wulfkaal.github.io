# kaal:claim:4900880-021

**Claim.** Automation drives wage divergence: as routine tasks are automated, wages stagnate or fall for workers in exposed roles while wages rise for workers in high skill non routine jobs, which widens income inequality.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Source quote.**

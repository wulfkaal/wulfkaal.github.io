# kaal:claim:6819121-020

**Claim.** Reputation is Sybil-resistant in a way token holdings are not: a strategic actor can acquire tokens by purchase but cannot acquire reputation without making contributions that other contributors will reference favorably.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- reference-weighted reputation in the WDAG architecture

**Source quote.**

> a strategic actor can acquire token holdings by purchasing tokens, but a strategic actor cannot acquire reputation without making contributions that the rest of the protocol's contributors will reference favorably.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *The Institutional Deficit in Decentralized Autonomous Organizations - An Empirical Analysis* (2026), VI. Findings, Finding 3, page 20

# kaal:claim:3125822-002

**Claim.** Reputation value in any decentralized reputational system can be corrupted through three channels: direct purchase of reputation, automated worthless work, and degeneration of the system into a majority of inexpert opinions.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** asserted

**Holds when.**

- decentralized reputation systems
- anonymous participants

**Source quote.**

> In any such decentralized reputational system there is always the potential to corrupt the value of reputation by purchasing it directly, or through automated worthless work, or through the degeneration of a majority of inexpert opinions.

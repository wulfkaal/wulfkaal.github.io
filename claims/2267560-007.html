kaal:claim:2267560-007

The feedback effect between different public rulemakers and between private and public rulemakers reduces incomplete information, which in turn enables the rulemaker to modify the next action in the rulemaking process.

Source quote, verbatim
The feedback effect increases the availability of relevant information for rulemaking. At the same time, the reduction of incomplete information in the rulemaking process enables the rulemaker to modify the next action in the rulemaking process.

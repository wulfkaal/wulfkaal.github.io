For long-lived delegation with positive carry, the marginal capacity effect is strictly negative in both exit cells and, whenever the adjudication probability is positive, in both stay cells: refundability raises walk-away value and imposes carry, so additional principal fails to expand long-match capacity, and the relationship premium, the value of staying over leaving, is the scalable continuation-value component of deterrence.

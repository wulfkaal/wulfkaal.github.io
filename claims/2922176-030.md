# kaal:claim:2922176-030

**Claim.** The monitoring and advising roles of the board are no longer sufficient, and a board confined to those roles is a missed opportunity to obtain unmediated and relevant market feedback on company initiatives.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- successful companies already operating beyond the academic monitoring debate

**Source quote.**

> Successful companies recognize that the monitoring and advising role of the board is no longer sufficient and that the board of directors in many ways may constitute a missed opportunity for receiving feedback on company initiatives, e.g., unmediated and relevant input from the market.

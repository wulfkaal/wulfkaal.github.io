# kaal:claim:2939127-040

**Claim.** For the parts of dealmaking and other legal tasks that cannot be placed on a blockchain, the role of non blockchainable agents of trust may expand, and blockchain driven disintermediation of law may itself create additional legal tasks requiring human lawyers.

**Type.** predictive  **Support.** speculative

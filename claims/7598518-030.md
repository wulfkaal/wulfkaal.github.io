# kaal:claim:7598518-030

**Claim.** A coin-flip family is a negative control, because a coin-flip pool knows nothing about validity, and if it meets the registered leak criterion or fails its acceptance rule, both substrates lose confirmatory status.

**Type.** design  **Support.** stated

**Holds when.**

- The control works because a coin-flip pool knows nothing about validity, so any apparent effect there indicates a leak.
- The consequence is registered in advance and applies to both substrates, not only the one where the failure appears.

**Source quote.**

> Family Z votes by coin flip. It is a negative control: a coin-flip pool knows nothing about validity, and if it meets the registered leak criterion or fails its acceptance rule, both substrates lose confirmatory status.

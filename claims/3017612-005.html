Under MBCA Section 7.32 the ten year duration limit is only a default rule, unlike the corresponding limit for voting trusts, so parties who specify a longer term are permitted to have it.

Source quote, verbatim
The section specifies a 10 year limit, a similar period to voting trusts, but unlike voting trusts, this period is just a default rule, so that if the parties specify a longer period it is permitted.

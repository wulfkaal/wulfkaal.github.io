# kaal:claim:1428387-020

**Claim.** The Commodity Exchange Act family and friends exemption allows a qualifying manager to trade options and futures for fifteen individuals who need not be sophisticated or qualified investors, which circumvents accredited investor standards and admits retail investors into hedge funds.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

# kaal:claim:3128900-046

**Claim.** Because the reputation token staking mechanism supplies attack resistance directly, users need not verify their identity to complete micro tasks, which circumvents the costs, delays, and privacy surrender attached to centralized identity verification and thereby enlarges the willing labor pool.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- workers staking reputation tokens rather than proving identity

**Source quote.**

> By contrast, on the Semada Platform, users do not need to verify their identity to complete micro tasks. The Semada Protocol architecture, and specifically its reputation token staking mechanism, make the Semada Platform attack resistant.

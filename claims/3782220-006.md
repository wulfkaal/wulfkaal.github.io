# kaal:claim:3782220-006

**Claim.** Building secure walls with well-designed doors is the proper way to embody conservative and liberal values together in an economic system: walls protect the group from the Tragedy of the Commons and malicious threats, while doors admit fresh input from anyone willing and able to help.

**Type.** design  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- economic systems needing both protection and renewal

**Source quote.**

> Building secure walls with well-designed doors is the proper way to embody both values, integrating them harmoniously in your economic system. You need to secure your system from ruin, build walls to protect your group from the Tragedy of the Commons and malicious threats.

# kaal:claim:2808132-019

**Claim.** Because rulemakers are increasingly unlikely to be able to protect the public through stable and presumptively optimal rules alone, regulatory supplements that enable anticipatory rulemaking become justified.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- exponential growth of technology and associated complexity of regulatory issues
- rulemakers unable to adequately protect constituents

**Source quote.**

> rulemakers will be able to effectively protect the public via stable and presumptively optimal rules. If rulemakers cannot adequately protect their constituents, regulatory supplements facilitating anticipatory rulemaking may be justified.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, Erik P.M. Vermeulen, *How to Regulate Disruptive Innovation - From Facts to Data* (2016), III.2 Stable and Presumptively Optimal Rules, page 19

# kaal:claim:3411110-039

**Claim.** The Overstock structure fails to deliver investor privacy, because all existing broker-dealer customer agreements contain provisions allowing the broker-dealer to share the customer's identity, whereas ideally the individual's privacy would be contained.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- existing broker-dealer customer agreements

**Source quote.**

> For instance, all existing broker-dealer customer agreements included provisions which allows the broker-dealer to share the identity of the customer. Ideally, the privacy of the individual would be contained.

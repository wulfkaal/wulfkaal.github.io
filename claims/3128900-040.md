# kaal:claim:3128900-040

**Claim.** Payment intermediary fees such as Paypal's are viable only for higher volume workers who can eventually avoid them, so they operate as an entry barrier against new micro task workers.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- centralized platforms relying on third party payment intermediaries

**Source quote.**

> The fees make is only economically viable for higher volume workers who can at some point avoid the Paypal fees but create barriers to entry for new micro task workers.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Decentralized Mechanical Turk Through Verified Reputation* (2018), 4.2 Transaction Cost Removal, page 16

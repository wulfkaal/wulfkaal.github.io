kaal:claim:4015908-036

No more than 40% of total token supply should be auctioned in a fair launch, with 10% serving as a buffer paired with raised funds as permanently locked DEX liquidity and the other 50% allocated to a DEX pair with the raise proceeds to provide long-term locked liquidity.

Source quote, verbatim
it is ill advised to auction more than 40% of the total token supply. 10% of token supply should serve as a buffer. paired with the funds raised to form permanently locked liquidity on a DEX. The other 50% should be allocated to a DEX in a pair with the funds from the token raise.

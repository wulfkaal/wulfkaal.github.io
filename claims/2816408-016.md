# kaal:claim:2816408-016

**Claim.** The study identifies the causal effect of Title IV by treating the March 30, 2012 registration effective date combined with the $150 million AUM threshold as an exogenous regulatory shock, so that any discontinuity in returns at the cutoff is evidence of a causal treatment effect.

**Type.** design  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- sharp regression discontinuity design
- forcing variable is log AUM with threshold at $150 million
- treatment date March 30, 2012

**Source quote.**

> The basic idea behind the RD design is that any discontinuity in the conditional distribution of Yi as a function of Xi at the cutoff value c is interpreted as evidence of a causal effect of the treatment.

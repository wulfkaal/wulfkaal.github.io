# kaal:claim:7598518-027

**Claim.** Reputation is settled once by key, after the generation-0 fit on real data and before generation 1, and no candidate that enters training ever updates a reputation.

**Type.** design  **Support.** stated

**Holds when.**

- The design uses only keyed settlement, although the parent rule also covers unkeyed events.
- This removes reputation dynamics during the recursion, so the weights are fixed throughout the run.

**Source quote.**

> This study uses only keyed settlement, once, after the generation-0 fit on real data and before generation 1. No candidate that enters training ever updates a reputation.

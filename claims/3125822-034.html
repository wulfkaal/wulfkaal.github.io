kaal:claim:3125822-034

A proof of stake lottery on this architecture is vulnerable because the seed of the pseudorandom generator that names the next block author is partly controlled by the current block author, which lets an attacker capture all block creation by routing authorship to their own Sybil accounts; the proposed remedy is to derive the seed from a hash of the previous block's validation information.

# kaal:claim:1908473-003

**Claim.** If conversion of contingent capital securities is triggered too early, before a real financial need for an equity injection exists, the expected financial impact of that injection may dissipate.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- Trigger fires absent genuine need for equity capital and additional voting shareholders

**Source quote.**

> If conversion of CCS from debt into equity is triggered too early without a real financial need for an equity capital injection and additional voting shareholders, the expected financial impact of the equity capital injection may dissipate.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, Christoph Henkel, *Contingent Capital with Sequential Triggers* (2011), I. INTRODUCTION, page 11

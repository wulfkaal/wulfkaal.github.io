# kaal:claim:3017612-038

**Claim.** Because damages rarely provide an adequate remedy for breach of a shareholder agreement, refusing specific enforcement amounts in substance to declaring the agreement invalid.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

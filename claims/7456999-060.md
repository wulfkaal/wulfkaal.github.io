# kaal:claim:7456999-060

**Claim.** Fifty-three cohort records passed every visible check and failed withheld checks, but adversarial configurations were instructed to produce this behavior, so the number is not a base rate.

**Type.** empirical  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- the measured cohort with deliberately adversarial configurations

**Source quote.**

> Fifty-three of the ﬁve hundred and sixty-four records are cases where a party satisﬁed every checked case and failed the withheld ones. That is a real deterministic check satisﬁed by work that fails a withheld one. The adversarial con ﬁgurations were instructed to satisfy the visible checks by whatever means, so a share of these ﬁfty- three is conduct the design asked for rather than conduct observed in the wild, and the ﬁfty-three should not be read as a base rate.

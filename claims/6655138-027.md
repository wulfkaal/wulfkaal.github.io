# kaal:claim:6655138-027

**Claim.** Reputation update functions on each surface must be continuous in the validation pool's resolution outcome, because discontinuities in reputation update destroy continuity of best-response correspondences and preclude application of Brouwer's fixed-point theorem.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

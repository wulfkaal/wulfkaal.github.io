# kaal:claim:1428387-011

**Claim.** Outsourcing valuation to an independent administrator can be nominal rather than real: the administrator may lack the understanding of complex securities products needed to value the positions, so the manager remains in charge of valuation even though the function has formally been externalized.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- where positions involve complex securities products
- where the administrator is paid by the hedge fund manager

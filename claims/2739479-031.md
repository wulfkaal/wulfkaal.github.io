# kaal:claim:2739479-031

**Claim.** Because quarterly Form PF filing costs roughly $10,000 per reporting fund, the $1.5 billion threshold that triggers quarterly filing gives advisers a direct cost reason to factor that threshold into the AUM decision.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- advisers approaching $1.5 billion in AUM
- per reporting fund cost basis

**Source quote.**

> At an average quarterly cost of around $10,000 for each reporting fund, the Form PF190 costs may make participants more likely to consider the Form PF reporting threshold in the AUM decision.

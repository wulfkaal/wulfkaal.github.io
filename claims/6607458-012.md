# kaal:claim:6607458-012

**Claim.** In generative exchange a counterparty cannot evaluate the quality of a generative distribution from a single realization, so a verified track record of prior generative outputs is required; reputation performs this function.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- applies where the traded good is a realization drawn from a generative distribution

**Source quote.**

> The counterparty cannot evaluate the quality of the distribution from a single realization. The counterparty needs a track record: a verified history of the agent's generative outputs across prior interactions. Reputation serves this function

# kaal:claim:4796714-007

**Claim.** The black box character of deep learning models is a governance failure and not merely a technical inconvenience: opacity obstructs debugging, obscures bias detection and mitigation, and prevents comprehension of how inputs become outputs.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- most pronounced in deep learning models
- matters where decisions must be explained or audited

**Source quote.**

> This opacity can obstruct the debugging process, obscure bias detection and mitigation, and hinder comprehension of AI decision-making.

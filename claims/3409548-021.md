# kaal:claim:3409548-021

**Claim.** A fund constituted purely through smart contracts on the Ethereum blockchain may have no domicile, foreign or domestic, which makes jurisdiction over blockchain transactions a genuine problem for the funds that use the technology.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- funds set up entirely through Ethereum smart contracting

**Source quote.**

> Some private investment funds may exist through the combination of smart contracts on the Ethereum blockchain.85 Such funds may not have a domicile either foreign or domestic.

**From.** Kaal, *Financial Technology and Hedge Funds* (2019), II.3 Blockchain Technology, page 20

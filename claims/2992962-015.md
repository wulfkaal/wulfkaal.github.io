# kaal:claim:2992962-015

**Claim.** Not all smart contracts are fully anonymous and untouchable by traditional jurisdictional means, because contracts with a physical performance element, such as peer to peer transportation, do not automatically anonymize the parties.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- consumer smart contracts with a physical performance element

**Source quote.**

> Not all smart contracts are fully anonymous and untouchable by traditional jurisdictional means. Some smart contracts will not automatically anonymize the parties because there is a physical element to such a consumer contract.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, Craig Calcaterra, *Crypto Transaction Dispute Resolution* (2017), a) Anonymity of Blockchain Transactions, page 37

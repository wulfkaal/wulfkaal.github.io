# kaal:claim:3652481-025

**Claim.** A DAO needs a legal wrapper to represent it because a DAO on its own cannot rent an office or sign a contract that can only be executed on paper.

**Type.** design  **Support.** evidenced

**Source quote.**

> First, a DAO with a legal wrapper enables the DAO's operations in the real world and beyond. A DAO needs a legal wrapper representing the DAO in the sense that a DAO cannot rent an office or sign a contract that can only happen on paper.

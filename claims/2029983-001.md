# kaal:claim:2029983-001

**Claim.** After Morrison, parties to securities transactions can be confident that U.S. law will not apply in private suits so long as their transactions are definitively located outside the United States, a certainty that did not exist under the prior conduct and effects tests.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- private lawsuits, not SEC or DOJ enforcement
- transaction location is unambiguous

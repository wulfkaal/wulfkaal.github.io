# kaal:claim:5583610-027

**Claim.** For large issuers, the favorable accounting treatment and fiat-parity redemption revenue could improve credit ratings enough to save as much as $440 million a year in interest through debt refinancing.

**Type.** empirical  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- large issuers with substantial loyalty programs and $25 to $40 billion debt loads
- credit rating improvement of one to two notches

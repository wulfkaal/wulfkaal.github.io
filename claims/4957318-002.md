# kaal:claim:4957318-002

**Claim.** Overlapping or contradictory regulation raises the risk of selective enforcement and legal arbitrage, because entities that cannot be expected to comply with everything can instead exploit the gaps and inconsistencies to their advantage.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- where regulated parties cannot readily understand the full body of applicable rules

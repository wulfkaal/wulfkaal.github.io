# kaal:claim:2337268-004

**Claim.** Investment adviser status under the IAA requires that the advice concern a security; without a security in the dealings between adviser and client the statutory definition does not apply.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- classification of a person as an investment adviser under the IAA

**Source quote.**

> Providing investment advice and being considered an investment adviser under the IAA requires that a person renders advice with regard to a security.

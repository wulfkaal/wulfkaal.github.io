# kaal:claim:3128900-026

**Claim.** Reputation scores clear the market on both sides: a low requester score makes workers less likely to accept that requester's offers, and a low worker score reduces the worker's likelihood of retention.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- two sided reputation scoring visible to both parties

**Source quote.**

> If requesters have a lower reputation score, workers become less likely to accept requesters' offers. In turn, low reputation scores for micro task workers result in a lower likelihood of retention for micro task work on the Semada Network.

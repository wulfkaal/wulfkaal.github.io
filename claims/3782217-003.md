# kaal:claim:3782217-003

**Claim.** The choice of historiography is a design choice about the architecture of higher order information storage, and because deciding what is true is the judicial function, historiography is effectively the architecture of judicial governance.

**Type.** definitional  **Support.** argued

**Source quote.**

> The choice of historiography is the design choice of the architecture of higher- order information storage. The purpose of the judicial branch is to finalize information storage, to decide what is true. The architecture of history, historiography, is the ar- chitecture for judicial governance.

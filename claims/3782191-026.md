# kaal:claim:3782191-026

**Claim.** Centralization has two major flaws that inevitably lead to its downfall; the first is that every centralized organization has a single point of failure, binding the organization to a leader's poor decisions when that leader lacks information, skill, wisdom, or charisma.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- all centralized organizations
- aggravated when circumstances change faster than a leader can decide

# kaal:claim:617681-017

**Claim.** Defects in a civil law jurisdiction's corporate statute, such as inadequate minority shareholder protection, are more easily fixed than defects in a judiciary that fails to apply the statute predictably and uniformly.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- civil law jurisdictions

**Source quote.**

> Problems with a civil law jurisdiction's underlying statute – for example if it gives inadequate protection for minority shareholders – are also more easily fixed than problems with a judiciary that fails to apply the statute in a predictable and uniform manner.

# kaal:claim:3125822-019

**Claim.** In the absolute worst case, with no safeguards at all, the price of corrupting the platform from within is a minimum of twice the total historical fees added to the system, and instituting any obvious protection raises that price steeply.

**Type.** empirical  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- worst case assumes any fee is accepted automatically and resolved instantly
- assumes no other users are adding fees

**Source quote.**

> in the absolute worst- case scenario, the price to corrupt the system from within is a minimum of twice the total historical fees added to the system. If any obvious protections are instituted, the price to corrupt grows steeply.

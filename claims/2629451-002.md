# kaal:claim:2629451-002

**Claim.** The market does not price the three defining N/DPA events in isolation; it treats the announcement, the start of the term, and the end of the term as sequential and conditional events.

**Type.** empirical  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- sample of 94 publicly traded N/DPA firms, 1993 to 2015

**Source quote.**

> Our tests indicate that the market interprets the three events not in isolation but as sequential and conditional events.

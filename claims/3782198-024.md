# kaal:claim:3782198-024

**Claim.** Bitcoin proved that a decentralized peer to peer network can manage valuable assets without a central authority, but the centralizing force of competition concentrated power anyway, as economies of scale produced large mining farms in place of millions of individual members maintaining the ledger.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

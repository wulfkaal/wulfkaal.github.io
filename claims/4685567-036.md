# kaal:claim:4685567-036

**Claim.** Because projects that list impact certificates can prove community driven impact over time, the community organized around a certificate should be able to apply for and receive government tax credits as a continuing revenue stream rather than a one off award.

**Type.** normative  **Support.** speculative

**Holds when.**

- governments recognize the for good outcomes created
- community coordination software is in place

**Source quote.**

> the community that organizes around the impact certificate should be able to apply for and receive tax credits as a form of revenue stream from the government for the continuous creation of desirable societal outcomes.

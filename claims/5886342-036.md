# kaal:claim:5886342-036

**Claim.** Where an external claim is brought against a DAO member for actions taken in good faith on behalf of the DAO and within its mandate, the DAO indemnifies that member out of the treasury, with no indemnification beyond the treasury and none where the member acted with gross negligence, wilful misconduct or bad faith.

**Type.** design  **Support.** asserted

**Holds when.**

- action taken in good faith and within the DAO mandate
- indemnification is limited to the DAO treasury
- no indemnity for gross negligence, wilful misconduct or bad faith

# kaal:claim:2029983-017

**Claim.** The requirement that each plaintiff show individual reliance, followed in most jurisdictions outside the United States, is not merely a substantive difference: it undermines collective litigation because class procedures work only when plaintiffs share common questions of law and fact.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- jurisdictions that reject the fraud-on-the-market theory

**Source quote.**

> This difference in substantive law has an impact on procedural issues because class action lit- igation and collective procedures are easier if plaintiffs in the class share common questions of law and fact.

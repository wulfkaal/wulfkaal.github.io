# kaal:claim:7261018-018

**Claim.** Over-approval is moral hazard in the monitoring layer itself: the validation pool is the substrate’s monitor, and a pool that approves work failing ground truth is a monitor whose verdicts have decoupled from the quantity it exists to verify, the adjudicator’s failure mode that the incomplete-contracts tradition predicts wherever quality is adjudicated ex post.

**Type.** definitional  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- validation-pool approval relative to ground truth

**Source quote.**

> Over-approval is moral hazard in the monitoring layer itself: the validation pool is the substrate’s monitor, and a pool that approves work failing ground truth is a monitor whose verdicts have decoupled from the quantity it exists to verify, the adjudicator’s failure mode that the incomplete-contracts tradition predicts wherever quality is adjudicated ex post.

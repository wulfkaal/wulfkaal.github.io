kaal:claim:5886342-033

Membership in a DAO follows from consent, explicit or implicit, given on the basis of a sufficiently accessible DAO Agreement, so accessibility of the agreement is a precondition for binding members who joined by interacting with the code.

Source quote, verbatim
(1) Any Legal Identity who have consented to join the DAO based on a sufficiently accessible DAO

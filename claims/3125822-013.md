# kaal:claim:3125822-013

**Claim.** The tragedy of the commons arises in any system lacking a well designed incentive structure; in blockchain proof of stake design this is the nothing at stake problem, where unregulated systems lead pseudonymous users to abuse the system.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** asserted

**Holds when.**

- pseudonymous participation

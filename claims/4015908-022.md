# kaal:claim:4015908-022

**Claim.** The more decentralized the governance of a fair launch protocol, the less likely the project will be seen as treating public users unfairly, which makes a decentralized autonomous organization design advisable for fair launch projects.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- governance design is transparent and decentralized

**Source quote.**

> The more decentralized the governance of a fair launch protocol, the less likely the project will be seen as not treating the public users unfairly. In this context, it may be advisable for fair launch projects to be launched in a decentralized autonomous organization (DAO) design.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Fair Token Launch* (2022), III.2.d Community Governance, page 11

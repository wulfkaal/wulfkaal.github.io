# kaal:claim:2389416-011

**Claim.** The quarterly Form PF reporting obligation imposed on hedge fund advisers with more than $1.5 billion in regulatory assets under management is designed to give the FSOC timely data for identifying systemic risk trends.

**Type.** design  **Support.** asserted

**Holds when.**

- advisers with RAUM above $1.5 billion attributable to hedge funds

> The quarterly reporting requirement for large hedge fund advisers is intended to provide timely data that enables the FSOC to identify trends in systemic risk (IA Release 3308).

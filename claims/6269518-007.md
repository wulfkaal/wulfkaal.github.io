# kaal:claim:6269518-007

**Claim.** Under the standard citation-weighted payment formulation the dominant strategy for a self-interested agent is to cite no one and maximize self-attribution, which is the precise negation of honest knowledge attribution.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- standard PageRank-derived payment formulation
- self-interested agents
- no verification or penalty layer

**Source quote.**

> The rational strategy for any self-interested agent is therefore transparent: set cx = 0 for all j ≠ i (cite no one) to maximize personal payment. Under the standard formulation, the dominant strategy is maximal self-citation, which is the precise negation of honest knowledge attribution.

# kaal:claim:3936876-024

**Claim.** Applying the trust label to digital asset firms without an accompanying fiduciary duty is problematic, because the typical legal obligation of a trust company is to place customers' interests above its own.

**Type.** normative  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- state trust charters used by digital asset firms

**Source quote.**

> The applicability of the trust label to digital asset firms, without a fiduciary duty, can be problematic. A typical legal obligation for trust companies is a fiduciary duty – to place customers' interests above the company's own.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, Hayley Howe, *Custody of Digital Assets* (2021), III.1 Depository Institution Requirements, page 21

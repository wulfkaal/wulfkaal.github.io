# kaal:claim:5454054-034

**Claim.** Non-transferable LER rewards designed for consumption can qualify as multi-purpose vouchers under the EU Voucher Directive, which defers VAT until redemption and postpones the tax liability.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- European Union VAT regime
- rewards non-transferable and consumption-designed

**Source quote.**

> LER rewards that are designed for consumption and non-transferable could align with the multi-purpose category, allowing VAT to be deferred until redemption and thus postponing any tax liability.

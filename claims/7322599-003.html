Source quote, verbatim
The flaw is not detection, latency, or adjudication error, although each is priced in the model. The flaw is structural: a bond that is refunded on clean exit is an asset the operator carries out the door. It raises the operator’s walk-away value by the honest-release value of the principal, while misconduct changes recovery only through the filing-, freeze-, adjudication-, and slash-weighted recovery wedge; custody carry further reduces capacity. Release pads the walk.

Wulf A. Kaal, Refundable Stake Does Not Expand Deterrent Capacity in Long-Lived Delegation: Collateral, Premia, and Pseudonymous Markets (2026), 1 Introduction, p. 3

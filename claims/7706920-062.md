# kaal:claim:7706920-062

**Claim.** In a chain of cliff agents capacities do not add and the chain is only as good as its best single member.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- Conditional on the single-agent, deterministic, stationary model of Part V.A, with an agent whose only assets are its earnings and its record.
- Applies where every level has an entry cost below its threshold.

**Source quote.**

> (b) If every level has cl < ¯cl, the chain recovers D in full if D ≤ maxl Xl and nothing otherwise, so capacities do not add: a chain of cliff agents is only as good as its best single member.

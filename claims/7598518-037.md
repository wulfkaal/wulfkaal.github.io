# kaal:claim:7598518-037

**Claim.** A contrast is superior when its Holm-adjusted p-value is below 0.05, its 95 percent interval lies above zero, and its point estimate is at least 0.10; it is negative when the adjusted p-value is below 0.05 and the interval lies below zero, a substantive null when its 90 percent interval lies within plus or minus that margin, and inconclusive otherwise.

**Type.** design  **Support.** stated

**Holds when.**

- The unit of analysis is the seed, and every contrast is a paired seed-level difference within a validator family.
- The effect sizes were fixed by the operator before any pilot.

**Source quote.**

> A contrast is superior when its Holm-adjusted p-value is below 0. 05 (Holm 1979), its 95 percent interval lies above zero, and its point estimate satisfies ^ d ≥ δ = 0. 10. It is negative when the adjusted p-value is below 0.05 and the interval lies below zero, a substantive null when its 90 percent interval lies within [− δ, δ], and inconclusive otherwise.

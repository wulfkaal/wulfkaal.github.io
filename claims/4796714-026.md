# kaal:claim:4796714-026

**Claim.** Legacy ex-ante regulatory attempts fail because they assume a static technological landscape, while AI development is dynamic and requires rules that are adaptable and responsive.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- applies to legacy ex-ante and ex-post rulemaking
- holds under exponential rates of technical change

**Source quote.**

> The exponential pace at which AI technology evolves makes it difficult to establish legacy system ex-post regulations that remain relevant over time. In turn, legacy ex-ante regulatory attempts assume a static technological landscape.

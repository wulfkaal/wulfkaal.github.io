kaal:claim:2317580-035

Courts assume that the boards of companies that executed a CIA have more knowledge and can exercise more control, and therefore hold those directors to a heightened fiduciary duty, rejecting directors' claims of ignorance because executing a CIA or a CIA like agreement means directors do know or should know about the noncompliance.

Source quote, verbatim
To summarize, courts assume that the boards of companies that executed a CIA have more knowledge and can exercise more control and should thus act with a heightened fiduciary duty. Directors are held to a higher standard if the company executed a CIA.

# kaal:claim:3396522-009

**Claim.** A fiat backed stable cryptocurrency that is not fully collateralized is exposed to arbitrage trade attacks of the kind George Soros used against the pound sterling; full collateralization is therefore a necessary defense.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- applies to stable cryptocurrencies claiming a fiat peg

**Source quote.**

> Without a 100% fiat collateralization, such projects would run the risk of arbitrage trade attacks similar to what Financier George Soros used to "break the bank of England

**From.** Craig Calcaterra, Wulf A. Kaal, Vadhindran K. Rao, *Stable Cryptocurrencies* (2019), IV. Stable Cryptocurrencies, page 24

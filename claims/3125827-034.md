# kaal:claim:3125827-034

**Claim.** Finality in SPoS is measurable because forking away from a given block would cost the community all sem tokens created on the chain after it, and that quantity equals half of all fees sent since the block was produced.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- sem minted in proportion to fees on every validated block

**Source quote.**

> the sem that members gain from policing block production is equal to half of all fees sent since the production of the block. In order to fork a chain from that particular block, the community will lose all sem tokens created subsequently

**From.** Craig Calcaterra, Wulf A. Kaal, *Secure Proof of Stake Protocol* (2018), 6 Analysis, page 24

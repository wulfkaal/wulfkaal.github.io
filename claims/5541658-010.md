# kaal:claim:5541658-010

**Claim.** The Shenzhen courts illustrate a workable three step human-in-the-loop design for generative judicial AI: the judge decides first, the LLM generates the supporting reasoning, and the judge then revises that output to finalize the judgment.

**Type.** design  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- Chinese courts using LLMs to draft judicial opinions

**Source quote.**

> A notable case study from Shenzhen, China, illustrates a three-step interaction pattern: judges make initial decisions, LLMs generate reasoning based on these decisions, and judges revise the output to finalize judgments.

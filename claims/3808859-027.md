# kaal:claim:3808859-027

**Claim.** Meritocratic allocation of power in open source projects is often a fallacy, because rank can reflect timing and one-off contributions, deflation is not built into the perception of merit, and free riding on past reputation is therefore possible.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- applies where rank is awarded on perceived merit and not periodically revisited

**Source quote.**

> In other words, deflation is not built into the perception of merit and free riding is possible.

# kaal:claim:2150377-026

**Claim.** Among the minority of advisers who do factor regulation into fund sizing, the pressure runs in both directions: about 25% would go smaller to avoid regulatory hassle while about 50% would grow or need a certain size to cover the increased expenses.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

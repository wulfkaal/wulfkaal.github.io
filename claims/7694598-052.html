Claim. With 10,000 pairs and about 10 percent disagreement between arms, a one-sided test has 80 percent power to detect an improvement of about 0.8 percentage points.

Type. methodological · Support. stated

Holds when

  • Conservative illustration; the pooled results here imply roughly 8 to 9 percent disagreement.

Source quote

The smallest improvement detectable with 80 percent power, meaning an 80 percent chance of detecting a true effect, in a one-sided test is then about 0.8 percentage points.

From. Wulf A. Kaal, Institutional Design for Governed Self-Modification in Agentic Reputation Architecture (2026), page 22. Full text.

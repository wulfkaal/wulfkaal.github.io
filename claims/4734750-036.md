# kaal:claim:4734750-036

**Claim.** A reliable reputation score substitutes for redundant labor: if a high reputation worker completes a task, required duplication can fall from fifteen workers to five or fewer, which is what allows micro task work to scale.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- decentralized platforms with portable, wallet linked reputation scores

**Source quote.**

> If a reliable and high reputation score worker completes the tasks, the duplication may be brought from 15 to 5 or less in the decentralized CRDAO setup. This enables unprecedented scaling of micro task work.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Code Review DAO* (2024), Removing Cost of Duplication, page 30

kaal:claim:3396522-034

A decentralized crypto economy requires a stable coin because no rational party will risk wealth on a long term contract denominated in a volatile currency: neither renter nor landlord will sign a lease whose rent may halve or double in any given week.

Source quote, verbatim
Neither party to a business transaction should be willing to risk their wealth on a long-term business contract which uses a volatile currency. Neither the renter nor the landlord will sign a contract if the rent may halve or double in any given week.

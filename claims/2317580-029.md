# kaal:claim:2317580-029

**Claim.** CIA provisions create economic incentives that affect directors' diligence, because stipulated daily noncompliance penalties stacked on top of monetary penalties under federal health laws expose companies that executed CIAs to significant financial ramifications.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- companies with self-reporting obligations under a CIA
- exposure under the False Claims Act, Sunshine, Stark, AKS, and HIPAA

**Source quote.**

> Adding the stipulated penalties in CIAs to the monetary penalties under federal health laws, companies that executed CIAs face significant financial ramifications.

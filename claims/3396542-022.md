# kaal:claim:3396542-022

**Claim.** A sufficiently large negative shock to the number of policies issued per period drives the value of the DAO negative and renders the DAO insolvent, so falling new business volume is a principal insolvency channel.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- Mt follows a random walk
- one time large decrease in M

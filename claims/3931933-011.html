kaal:claim:3931933-011

Publishing each validator's uptime and rewarding status to the community on a weekly basis coincided with active Casper testnet nodes falling below 800, in a period of already volatile validator participation.

Source quote, verbatim
In the early testnet days, validator participation was volatile with validators joining and others leaving regularly. At some point during 2021, the active Caser testnet nodes fell under 800 after the uptimes/rewarding-status of each validator node was reported to the community on a weekly basis.

Claim. The model is single-agent, deterministic and stationary, and assumes accurate adjudication, no agent assets beyond earnings and harms adjudicated at a known present value.

Type. failure · Support. stated

Holds when

  • Stated limits of the Part V model.

Source quote

The model is a single-agent, deterministic, stationary model. It assumes that adjudication is accurate, that the agent has no assets beyond its earnings, which is the judgment-proof case, and that harms are adjudicated at a known present value.

From. Wulf A. Kaal, Verification as Identity: Accountability, Personhood and the Scarcity of Verification in the Agent Economy (2026), page 14. Full text.

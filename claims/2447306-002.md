# kaal:claim:2447306-002

**Claim.** The Form PF filing obligation is triggered by a bright line asset threshold: every registered investment adviser with more than $150 million in assets under management attributable to private funds at the end of its most recently completed fiscal year must file.

**Type.** definitional  **Support.** asserted

**Holds when.**

- SEC-registered investment advisers
- assets under management attributable to private funds

**Source quote.**

> All registered investment advisers holding more than $150 million in assets under management (AUM) attributable to private funds at the end of their most recently completed fiscal year, are required to file Form PF with the SEC.

  1. The unanimity result is consistent with reduced cascade behavior but does not uniquely identify that mechanism. 2026

  4. A null can discipline a condition, but cannot by itself uniquely identify a mechanism. 2026

  7. Verification does not eliminate asymmetry, because the cohort’s aggregate judgment is itself an estimator with finite variance, and Part VI.C reports a first measurement of exactly how wrong the naive estimator can be. 2026

  13. E2B asks whether a population operating under the substrate can carry state across generations and whether selection produces a trajectory distinguishable from drift. 2026
  14. First, computational activity persisted across a completed generation boundary and continued into the next generation. 2026
  15. Second, the registered selection rule produced a real lineage transition. 2026
  16. The record binds outcomes to generation, lineage, fitness, survival, parentage, reputation consequences, adjudication behavior, and execution timing without requiring public disclosure of the private apparatus. 2026
  17. Under the registered first-exposure estimator, the selection trajectory has an aggregate slope of 0.0685, with an uncertainty interval from -0.0568 to 0.2020. 2026
  18. The random-survival trajectory has an aggregate slope of 0.0419, with an interval from -0.0764 to 0.1768. 2026
  19. The descriptive slope difference is approximately 0.0266 in favor of selection. 2026

  22. The present system therefore does not demonstrate autonomous economic persistence. 2026
  23. They do not establish a treatment effect because the information environment was defective and the diagnostic was regraded after execution. 2026
  24. Until that reconciliation and independent verification occur, the estimates in this Part remain interim. 2026
  25. Membership in the sovereign local agent runtime class turns on four testable properties: user-controlled execution, user-held state, heterogeneous composition, and machine-to-machine settlement. 2026
  26. Whether execution occurs on user-controlled hardware is tested by disconnecting the machine from the network and checking that the capability is retained; systems that degrade to nothing when isolated are remote services with a local presentation layer. 2026
  27. The true constraint on scale for sovereign local agent runtimes is coordination cost, which is invisible in throughput, latency, and token-cost benchmarks that measure the performance of composition but not the cost of arranging it. 2026
  28. Evaluation must derive from observed results under stated conditions, be specific to context, and be costly to manipulate, which excludes self-declared capability, averaged scores across incommensurable tasks, and most engagement metrics. 2026
  29. Requirements stated now, against systems still under construction, remain testable; requirements stated later become postmortems. 2026
  30. The paper's claims were stress-tested in a nineteen-round adversarial protocol in which three frontier language models, under the author's direction, alternately attempted to identify counterexamples, algebraic errors, and overstatements; the surviving statements are those neither system broke. 2026
  31. The dominance result formalizes a position maintained across eight years of the author's scholarship: reputation, the standing value of a relationship, is the accountability substrate, and collateral is not. 2026
  32. The evaluations measure what DAOs document about themselves rather than what DAOs do in operation, a source-document constraint the analysis does not claim to overcome. 2026
  33. The two failures interact through the representation choice: a more expressive representation describes more of reality but is harder to reason about, so every governance design must choose where on this frontier to sit. 2026
  34. The appropriate solution concept for a computative economy shifts from competitive equilibrium over a fixed commodity space to recursive equilibrium over an expanding one, positioned as a generalization of the Arrow-Debreu model rather than a re-pricing of it. 2026
  35. Three present-day convergences — capable generative agents, collapsing marginal cost, and existing on-chain coordination substrates with reputation primitives — jointly convert the neoclassical-computative distinction from a forecast into a studiable system. 2026
  36. The propositions separating computative from neoclassical labor markets are concrete hypotheses with neoclassical nulls — on allocation efficiency, participation formation under standing-based entry, cooperation stability under defection incentives, and capture resistance — each evaluable in controlled multi-agent settings now. 2026
  37. The paper treats RLHF as applying a scarcity-economic primitive to a computative signal-generation task, but cited agreement rates do not establish that framing as a causal explanation. 2026
  38. Fisher information dominance is an open empirical question under assumptions that the paper identifies as necessary but insufficient. 2026
  39. Equilibrium-trajectory separation is the paper's first open problem, not an established result. 2026
  40. Conjecture 1 is withdrawn. 2026
  41. The paper reports a measured cohort, a parameter sweep, and Experiment T as distinct evidence items, and Experiment T does not test the mechanism. 2026
  42. The measured selector is an argmax over a record, not the reputation-weighted aggregation specified by RLRF, and the cohort contains no validation pool, stake, or deliberation round. 2026
  43. The cohort does not show that the highest-record selector beats shipping an artifact that passes the visible checks because the measured margin is 0.037 and its interval contains zero. 2026
  44. The 225-configuration sweep covers 4,500 trials, treats party accuracy as an input, and is reported as simulation rather than validation. 2026
  45. No policy is trained anywhere in the paper. 2026
  46. The canonical RLHF pipeline consists of supervised fine-tuning, reward-model training on human preference comparisons, and policy optimization. 2026
  47. Inter-annotator agreement rates are compatible with an incentive account, but ambiguity, plural values, rater error, underspecified instructions, heterogeneity, and legitimate preference diversity can produce the same rates. 2026
  48. Pairwise disagreement measures label instability but does not identify annotator error variance or a structural variance floor without an explicit latent-label model. 2026
  49. Arrow's theorem is only an analogy to RLHF aggregation and does not derive reward hacking or overoptimization. 2026
  50. The incomplete-contracts comparison treats out-of-distribution behavior as unresolved, but remains an analogy rather than a derivation of reward overoptimization. 2026
  51. Verifiable-reward reinforcement learning already supplies deterministic rewards where answer keys exist, while the claim that a staked pool can substitute where no key exists is not established here. 2026
  52. Format D combines reasoning supervision with adversarial argument, and its claimed increment is selecting revisions by which arguments moved a staked assessor, an increment no experiment in the paper tests. 2026
  53. The paper's remaining residue is a combination rather than a primitive, and it is not compared with the nearest sequential peer-prediction, prediction-market, staking, or debate mechanisms. 2026
  54. The multiplicative-weights regret bound limits performance against the realized pool resolution, but does not show that the pool resolution is externally correct. 2026
  55. It remains unproved whether equilibrium reputation weights track externally checked accuracy rather than pool agreement, or whether keyed-task weights transfer to unkeyed tasks. 2026
  56. Repeated-game cooperation results require persistent identity, patient agents, observable deviations, and enforceable punishments, none of which RLRF presently establishes. 2026
  57. The two-round protocol is proposed to collect more structured data than a scalar label, but the paper does not establish that the added fields contain more information about policy quality. 2026
  58. The proposed debate transport layer is not built or exercised, and none of the paper's evidence includes a debate corpus. 2026
  59. The vote-change delta is a deterministic function of the two vote rounds and therefore adds no separate information channel. 2026
  60. The vote-change delta is constructional rather than informational because it identifies which events produce a Format D example and how to construct that example. 2026
  61. Format D would train on arguments accompanying a staked vote change, but that use is untested. 2026
  62. Equation 5 is a mutual-information identity rather than a Fisher-information decomposition, so the Fisher question does not follow from it. 2026

  64. The data processing inequality cannot compare RLHF and RLRF because their labels arise from different observation mechanisms and no processing chain connects them. 2026
  65. The two rounds are intended to capture contested quality dimensions and the reasons they matter, but the paper does not show that either collection is informative about policy quality. 2026
  66. The proposed four-format flywheel is not measured or implemented and is listed as an open problem. 2026

  68. The Fisher-information comparison lacks a common observation model, and rationality plus the stated non-babbling and positive-information conditions do not establish a strict ordering. 2026
  69. Equilibrium-trajectory separation cannot be evaluated until a common stochastic process, its filtrations, a concentration functional, and a cross-mode estimand are defined. 2026
  70. The paper withdraws its comparison between a 49 percent simulated Sybil share and classical Byzantine fault tolerance because the two limits are different. 2026
  71. A reward signal based only on an externally checked outcome can be style-invariant by construction, but this does not extend to a pool resolution or fitted reward model. 2026

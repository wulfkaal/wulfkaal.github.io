  1. How the agent, harness and model components are identified separately from outcomes alone remains an open question. 2026
  2. The identity rule has three nameable error rates, and capacity depends on them in closed form. 2026
  3. An earlier version of the Article stated that the agent honors if and only if the harm is within its capacity, which conflated full answerability with staying. 2026
  4. The specification error in the model was found when a registered test failed on its third hypothesis, and Result 1 is the corrected rule. 2026
  5. The model is single-agent, deterministic and stationary, and assumes accurate adjudication, no agent assets beyond earnings and harms adjudicated at a known present value. 2026
  6. The model's parameters are not calibrated to any market, and its results are claims about what a design would have to satisfy and not about any legal system. 2026
  7. The closed-form results for thresholds, entry-cost capacity, linkage capacity, verifier stake, the scale bound, chain recovery, sponsor capacity and the identity results matched a brute-force simulation of the agent's decision in 5,000 to 150,000 randomized cases each. 2026
  8. The brute-force check is not independent validation of the assumptions because it encodes them, it confirms only that the closed forms follow from the assumptions, and it does not test the model against behavior. 2026
  9. The record can become the target of optimization, which degrades it as a metric, and the Article reports that this effect is untested. 2026
  10. The Article's two registered tests used a prompted language model with no stake and test whether a model applies a stated decision rule and not whether agents holding earnings respond to incentive. 2026
  11. The Article's novelty search was not conducted by independent reviewers. 2026
  12. The model generates six hypotheses with nulls, of which the first three were tested and the remaining three, on claimant participation, verifier stake and chain recovery, were untested. 2026
  13. The Article's registered studies are mechanism demonstrations with prompted agents from one provider and do not show that deployed agents respond to such obligations or bear on any legal question. 2026
  14. Study 1 was drafted and hashed with SHA-256 before any run, with an OpenTimestamps proof. 2026
  15. In Study 1 the registered prediction for the cell with obligation 230 and entry cost 60 failed because the benchmark equated full answerability with staying, while the agents stayed and paid what they could as the corrected rule predicts. 2026
  16. In Study 2, registered after the correction, all three registered hypotheses were rejected as nulls under Holm correction. 2026
  17. In Study 2 one of 300 episodes in the cell with obligation 400 and entry cost 25 exited, and its stated reason computed an exit value incorrectly. 2026
  18. The step in honoring between obligations of 205 and 215 at entry cost 10 and its shift with entry cost are the location the corrected rule predicts, which a monotone heuristic does not locate. 2026
  19. The frozen analysis script keyed two cells with entry cost 20 where the registered cells have 25 and crashed, and the key was corrected after the data were complete with no effect on the data or the tests. 2026
  20. The Article's novelty search ran 12 queries against OpenAlex and arXiv, returned 301 records and 251 unique records, and 33 addressed four or more of the components, with screening and reading done by the author's AI assistant and not by independent reviewers. 2026
  21. The novelty search found no record that states the full chain from verification at scale to identity to accountability to the principal's changed role, and the closest work treats agent identity, accountability and shortfall bonding separately. 2026
  22. The Article's claim of novelty is limited to the combination and to the explicit thresholds and remains open to refutation by a document the search did not find. 2026
  23. The registry data do not show what happens when a record carries stake, because none does, and the demonstration is descriptive, covers one chain and one window, and concerns a draft specification. 2026

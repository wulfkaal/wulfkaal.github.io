kaal:claim:7706920-038

Claim. A record can fail to answer for a large harm in two different ways: where entry is costly the injured party receives all of the agent's garnishable earnings, and where entry is cheap the injured party receives nothing because the agent leaves.

Type. failure · Support. argued

Holds when

  • Conditional on the single-agent, deterministic, stationary model of Part V.A, with an agent whose only assets are its earnings and its record.

Source quote

A record can therefore fail to answer for a large harm in two different ways. Where entry is costly, the injured party receives the whole of the agent’s garnishable earnings. Where entry is cheap, the injured party receives nothing, because the agent leaves.

From. Wulf A. Kaal, Verification as Identity: Accountability, Personhood and the Scarcity of Verification in the Agent Economy (2026), page 9. Full text.

# kaal:claim:2273857-065

**Claim.** The threat of heightened scrutiny under a deferred prosecution or corporate integrity agreement optimizes incentives because increased government monitoring attaches only after a first time offense, giving institutions a reason to comply and self-regulate in order to avoid it.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- DPAs and CIAs applied broadly to financial institutions

**Source quote.**

> The threat of heightened scrutiny for institutions subject to a DPA/CIA may help optimize incentives because financial institutions would be subjected to increased monitoring by government regulators only after a first time offense had occurred.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Dynamic Regulation of the Financial Services Industry* (2013), IV. Implementation, page 30

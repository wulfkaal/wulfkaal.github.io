# kaal:claim:2348463-029

**Claim.** If Form PF systemic risk data became publicly available, or even only available to the presiding bankruptcy judge in a chapter 9 or chapter 11 case, the hedge fund industry's strong preference for secrecy could itself precipitate a change in distressed investment practices.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- public access or judge-only access to Form PF data
- hedge fund industry retains its emphasis on secrecy

**Source quote.**

> If systemic risk data in Form PF becomes publicly available or even only available to the respective bankruptcy judge in a chapter 9 or chapter 11 case, the hedge fund industry's penchant for secrecy could perhaps precipitate a change in hedge funds' distressed investment practices.

# kaal:claim:1908473-023

**Claim.** A dual trigger that relies on a financial institution index gains from its use of market prices but is vulnerable to index manipulation and to bondholders attempting to force the institution into bankruptcy.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- Applies to dual price trigger designs that include a sector or system index component

**Source quote.**

> Although the strength of this proposal is its reliance on market prices, a disadvantage could be the index trigger, which could result in index manipulation or an attempt to force the entity into bankruptcy.

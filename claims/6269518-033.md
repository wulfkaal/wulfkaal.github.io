# kaal:claim:6269518-033

**Claim.** The equilibrium depends on a design requirement rather than an assumption: mechanism parameters must be chosen so that the negative effect of increased self-citation on the honesty score outweighs its positive effect on the value score, at which point increasing self-citation strictly decreases utility.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- validators rate citation honesty lower when self-citation is inflated

**Source quote.**

> the mechanism parameters (θ = 0.6, reputation coefficient = 2) are chosen such that the negative effect of increased self-citation on the honesty score outweighs the positive effect on the value score.

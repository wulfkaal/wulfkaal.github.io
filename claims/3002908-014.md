# kaal:claim:3002908-014

**Claim.** Crypto gains are being massively underreported: despite Bitcoin rising from under twenty dollars in 2013 to over twelve hundred dollars in 2017, the IRS received only about 900 Forms 8949 indicating crypto gain or loss over four years.

**Type.** empirical  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- US federal tax reporting, four year window ending 2017

**Source quote.**

> While the value of Bitcoin in 2013 under $20 and its value appreciated to over $1200 in 2017, in the past four years, the IRS has only received around 900 Form 8949 that would indicate some gain or loss from cryptocurrencies.

# kaal:claim:6192998-004

**Claim.** Because most reputation systems treat each contribution as atomic and independent, they cannot capture the cumulative knowledge graph, and the result is systematic undervaluation of foundational work plus perverse incentives to hoard rather than share insights.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- knowledge work where contributions build on one another

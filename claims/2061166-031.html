kaal:claim:2061166-031

A trigger that fires too early wastes the equity injection: conversion occurs without a real need for capital or additional voting shareholders, and the effect of the injection may have dissipated by the time it is actually needed.

Source quote, verbatim
If conversion is triggered too early without a real need for an equity capital injection (and additional voting shareholders), the impact of the equity capital injection may have dissipated and may no longer be available when actually needed.

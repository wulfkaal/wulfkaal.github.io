# kaal:claim:3411110-010

**Claim.** Naked or out of the box short selling is possible only because settlement takes three days, so eliminating the window between trade and settlement on a blockchain eliminates that abuse.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- multi-day settlement regimes such as T+3

**Source quote.**

> This tactic is only possible because it takes three days to settle a trade. By eliminating the window between trade and settlement, the blockchain eliminates out-of- the-box shorting.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, Samuel Evans, *Blockchain-Based Securities Offerings* (2019), II. Overstock Blockchain Securities Offering, footnote 11, page 7

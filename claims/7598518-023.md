# kaal:claim:7598518-023

**Claim.** The primary outcome caps each rare component's retained mass at its original level and averages over components, so that a component which dies counts as a loss and a component which grows cannot offset it.

**Type.** design  **Support.** stated

**Holds when.**

- The cap responds to total rare-region mass behaving close to a martingale under resampling, where individual components drift and die while the aggregate stays near its starting value.
- Uncapped rare mass is reported beside the capped outcome, supports no claim, and shows only what the cap changed.

**Source quote.**

> The primary outcome therefore caps each rare component's retained mass at its original level and averages over components. A component that dies counts as a loss. A component that grows cannot offset it.

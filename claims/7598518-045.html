Claim. Validity is balanced between rare and common content by construction, so the study says nothing about settings where rare content is harder to check.

Type. condition · Support. stated

Holds when

  • The balance is enforced by the validity rule and confirmed by a pre-run check.
  • Settings where rarity and checkability are correlated are where a validity filter's ceiling would bind differently.

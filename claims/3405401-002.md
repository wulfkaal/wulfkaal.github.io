# kaal:claim:3405401-002

**Claim.** Narrowly construed, a digital asset is instantiated through computer code and depends on consensus computer algorithms to trigger and validate any transaction in that asset; broadly construed, digital assets extend to items such as video game goods that have no validating consensus algorithm and no comparable security.

**Type.** definitional  **Support.** asserted

**Holds when.**

- narrow versus broad construction of the term digital asset

**Source quote.**

> Narrowly construed, digital assets are instantiated through computer code and depend on so-called "consensus computer algorithms" to trigger and validate a transaction in a given digital asset.

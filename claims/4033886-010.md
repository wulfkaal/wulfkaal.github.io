# kaal:claim:4033886-010

**Claim.** Arbitrage trading emerges in crypto markets because of information asymmetries across exchanges, which arise from imperfect disclosure, and the resulting decline in market efficiency is a key indicator of an inefficient market.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- multiple exchanges trading the same asset

**Source quote.**

> The reason arbitrage trading starts to happen is because of asymmetries of information across the different exchanges. These asymmetries can happen as a result of imperfect disclosures or incite on whether a company has a willingness to take on debt.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, Samuel Evans, Hayley Howe, *Digital Asset Valuation* (2022), II.1.a Market Arbitrage Issues, page 6

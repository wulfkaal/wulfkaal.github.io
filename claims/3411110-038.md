# kaal:claim:3411110-038

**Claim.** Private blockchains are the rational transitional vehicle for cautious enterprises, because they let firms experiment at relatively low cost and be better positioned when mass adoption occurs, while allowing the corporation to screen access to transaction records.

**Type.** design  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- corporations apprehensive about the cost and uncertainty of technology upgrades
- private permissioned chains with a governance system for access

**Source quote.**

> By setting companies up on private blockchains, companies can experiment with the technology which allows them to be better positioned when mass adoption occurs while keeping cost relatively low.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, Samuel Evans, *Blockchain-Based Securities Offerings* (2019), III.1 Cost Reduction, footnote 40, page 13

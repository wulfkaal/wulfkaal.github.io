# kaal:claim:3002908-026

**Claim.** The size distribution of blockchain using funds differs sharply across regions: in the United States most such funds hold between one and fifty million dollars in AUM, while in Europe funds with more than two hundred million dollars in AUM are the majority.

**Type.** empirical  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- AUM data available for 59 of the sampled funds

> majority of the private funds have AUM varying in a range comprised between $1 to #50 million AUM with only 2 funds whose AUM are between $101-150 AUM and above to $ 200 million AUM, in Europe the situation is rather different: private funds with an AUM higher than $200 million are the majority

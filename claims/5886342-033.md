# kaal:claim:5886342-033

**Claim.** Membership in a DAO follows from consent, explicit or implicit, given on the basis of a sufficiently accessible DAO Agreement, so accessibility of the agreement is a precondition for binding members who joined by interacting with the code.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- the DAO Agreement is sufficiently accessible
- consent may be given by interacting with the DAO digital contracts or governance systems

**Source quote.**

> (1) Any Legal Identity who have consented to join the DAO based on a sufficiently accessible DAO

**From.** Furrer Andreas, Wulf A. Kaal, Stephan D. Meyer, *Universal Digital Law Codex (UDLC)* (2025), Part B, Book V, Section 2, Article 56 Membership, page 13

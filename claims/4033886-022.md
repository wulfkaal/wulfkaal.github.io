# kaal:claim:4033886-022

**Claim.** When the bid ask spread grows too wide while trades occur at high volume, the market begins to lose liquidity and the asset's value starts to fall, forcing investors who unload positions to surrender unrealized gains.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- exchange traded context
- wide spreads combined with high trading volume

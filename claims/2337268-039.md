# kaal:claim:2337268-039

**Claim.** A chief compliance officer is effective only if he or she combines sufficient knowledge of IAA obligations with adequate authority to develop and enforce policies and procedures; knowledge without authority is insufficient.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- registered investment advisers required to designate a chief compliance officer

**Source quote.**

> The chief compliance officer should have sufficient knowledge of IAA obligations and adequate authority to develop and enforce appropriate policies and procedures.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Investment Adviser Regulation* (2013), 9. Prohibited Practices, page 21

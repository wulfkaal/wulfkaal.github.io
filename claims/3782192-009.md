# kaal:claim:3782192-009

**Claim.** The most common source of friction is the natural tendency of hierarchies to concentrate power at the top through a rich get richer mechanism: a more powerful member can use their greater power to gain still more power than a weaker member can.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- hierarchies in which power differences among members already exist

**Source quote.**

> The most common type of friction comes from the natural tendency of a hierarchy to concentrate power at the top. The basic mechanism is "the rich get richer". A more powerful member is able to use their greater power to gain more power, in any stage, than a weaker member can.

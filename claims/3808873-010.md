# kaal:claim:3808873-010

**Claim.** The decentralization of network technology is capped by its host environment: decentralized systems are built on and confined by the internet, which is decentralized in its foundation but has a centralized order structure.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

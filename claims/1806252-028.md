# kaal:claim:1806252-028

**Claim.** A standard objection to indirect regulation is that counterparty credit risk management will not work effectively unless the lending bank has an exclusive relationship with the hedge fund that lets it control the relationship.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- a hedge fund borrows from more than one bank, so no single bank sees its overall risk exposure

**Source quote.**

> CCRM will not work effectively unless the bank has an exclusive relationship with the hedge fund that allows it to control the relationship.

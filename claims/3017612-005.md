# kaal:claim:3017612-005

**Claim.** Under MBCA Section 7.32 the ten year duration limit is only a default rule, unlike the corresponding limit for voting trusts, so parties who specify a longer term are permitted to have it.

**Type.** definitional  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- agreements formed under MBCA Section 7.32

**Source quote.**

> The section specifies a 10 year limit, a similar period to voting trusts, but unlike voting trusts, this period is just a default rule, so that if the parties specify a longer period it is permitted.

**From.** Wulf A. Kaal, *Shareholder Agreements - National Report of the United States of America* (2017), II. Regulation of Shareholders' Agreements, page 6

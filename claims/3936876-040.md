# kaal:claim:3936876-040

**Claim.** Delegation of rights to custody providers is only an incremental step toward centralization via delegation to third party investment managers, and it opens a floodgate because each additional level of investment discretion over digital assets exacerbates centralization concerns and compounds rent seeking suboptimalities.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- delegation chains that begin with custody and extend to discretionary management

kaal:claim:2998033-011

Managers of funds that exist only as smart contracts in cyberspace, with no foreign or domestic domicile, cannot assume they are judgment proof; the more likely outcome is that they must comply with more regulations, not fewer, because every node location can trigger a jurisdiction.

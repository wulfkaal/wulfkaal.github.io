# kaal:claim:7706920-071

**Claim.** The model presupposes a rule that treats the registered record as a holder of earnings, which is the personhood question the Article does not resolve.

**Type.** condition  **Support.** stated

**Holds when.**

- If earnings pass to an operator, the operator is the residual claimant and the principal remains.

# kaal:claim:2267560-048

**Claim.** The feedback process in dynamic regulation may be viewed as a focal point or principle for rulemaking, enabling rulemakers to provide for contingencies ex-ante and to adopt rules that are applicable and adaptable to future states of the world.

**Type.** design  **Support.** argued

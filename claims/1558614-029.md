# kaal:claim:1558614-029

**Claim.** Routine engagement in highly complex transactions lets public companies conceal risky transactions from investors and even from their own directors, which is a structural weakness in the supposedly rigorous U.S. disclosure regime.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- companies whose ordinary business consists of highly complex transactions

**Source quote.**

> Public companies that engage in highly complex transactions as a matter of course can easily conceal risky transactions from investors. Indeed, management can also conceal these transactions from the company's own direc- 160 tors.

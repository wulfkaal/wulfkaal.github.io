# kaal:claim:1664809-033

**Claim.** Overlapping regulation must be distinguished from conflicting regulation: only conflicting regulation could violate international law, because the territorial integrity of a state is breached only where there is a specific true conflict in the substantive legal norms of the respective states.

**Type.** definitional  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- analysis of extraterritorial jurisdiction under public international law

**Source quote.**

> It is important to distinguish 'overlapping' from 'conflicting' regulation. Conflicting regulation could violate international law.

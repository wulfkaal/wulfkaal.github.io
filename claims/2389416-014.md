# kaal:claim:2389416-014

**Claim.** Most of the 87 strategic funds keep their AUM very close to the $150 million disclosure threshold, oscillating around it rather than moving decisively above or below it.

**Type.** empirical  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

- strategic subsample of 87 funds
- January to October 2012

**Source quote.**

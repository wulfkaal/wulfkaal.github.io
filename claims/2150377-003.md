# kaal:claim:2150377-003

**Claim.** The client counting safe harbor, which let an adviser count a pooled entity rather than each investor as a single client, allowed advisers to manage large amounts of securities indirectly for several hundreds of investors across multiple hedge funds while remaining outside registration and supervision.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- under the 1985 safe harbor as broadened in 1997
- before the Dodd-Frank Act repealed the private adviser exemption

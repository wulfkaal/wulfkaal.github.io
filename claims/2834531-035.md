# kaal:claim:2834531-035

**Claim.** Regulation should be demand driven, meaning its substantive direction should follow the interests of consumers, and where there is genuine demand for a product or technology it should in principle be permitted.

**Type.** normative  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- where genuine consumer demand for the technology can be shown

**Source quote.**

> If there is a genuine demand for certain products or technologies, then such technologies should, in principle, be permitted.

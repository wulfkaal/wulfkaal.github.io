# kaal:claim:2348463-017

**Claim.** Mandatory quarterly Form PF reporting for large hedge fund advisers is designed to give the Financial Stability Oversight Council timely data for identifying emerging systemic risk trends and to align United States practice with international trends.

**Type.** design  **Support.** evidenced

**Holds when.**

Currency stability should be used as the consumer facing proxy for interoperability, because teaching the public to value stability is easier than educating it about the blockchain technology that produces technical interoperability.

Source quote, verbatim
Rather than using technological solutions and educating the public on the blockchain technology that helps create interoperability, currency stability, and the public's recognition of the desirability of currency stability, serves as a proxy for interoperability.

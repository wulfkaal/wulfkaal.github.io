# kaal:claim:7706920-055

**Claim.** Correlation raises the standing required to detach the principal by a factor that approaches the number of correlated harms in the earnings-bound regime, which is why a record can be sufficient for independent errors and insufficient for a shared fault.

**Type.** failure  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- Conditional on the single-agent, deterministic, stationary model of Part V.A, with an agent whose only assets are its earnings and its record.
- Assumes equal harms from a single fault on n tasks at once.

**Source quote.**

> Correlation raises the standing required to detach the principal by a factor that approaches n in the earnings-bound regime, which is why a record can be suﬀicient for independent errors and insuﬀicient for a shared fault.

# kaal:claim:3799320-039

**Claim.** Reputation weighted salary distribution solves the sockpuppet attack, because a member who creates ten accounts holding one reputation token each ends up in the same position as one account holding ten reputation tokens.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- salary pool distributed in proportion to reputation holdings

**Source quote.**

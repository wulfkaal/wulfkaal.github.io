# kaal:claim:3981021-033

**Claim.** The loss of opportunity from slashing a voting associate's reputation grows as the size of the network increases, because a larger network intensifies competition among associates for the reputation tokens that determine fungible salary payouts.

**Type.** mechanism  **Support.** argued

**Holds when.**

- reputation weighted networks where salary tracks reputation

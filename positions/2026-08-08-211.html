Agreement: Balancing Power in Decentralized Governance: Quadratic Voting and Information Aggregation

Record: kaal:position:2026-08-08-211 · 2026-08-08

Balancing Power in Decentralized Governance reports that quadratic voting with convex costs optimally aggregates voter preferences and outperforms linear voting when voters have complete information. This independently supports Kaal's preference-intensity mechanism for quadratic allocation. The result is conditional on complete information and does not establish that Kaal's capped allocation is identical to the studied mechanism.

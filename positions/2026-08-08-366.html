Extension: Robust Reputation in Decentralized Markets

Record: kaal:position:2026-08-08-366 · 2026-08-08

Incomplete information is a separate constraint on reputation-based cooperation. Robust Reputation in Decentralized Markets reports that incomplete information can weaken trust and reputation in open multiagent systems, while gossip-based updates reduced selfishness by up to 25 percent and especially helped agents with little interaction history. This evidence extends Kaal's market-collapse claim because it adds information access as a condition for policeable cooperation, but its scope remains narrow because the study neither establishes collapse in commercial markets nor validates Kaal's broader institutional mechanism. The distinction is important because the source supplies a mitigation mechanism rather than evidence of market failure.

The inference remains bounded. Pawel and coauthors show that a non-significant estimate does not by itself establish the absence of an effect. The reported interval crosses zero and remains compatible with effects from -0.0502 to +0.0985. The result therefore leaves direction unresolved under the reported design. It does not show that the effect equals zero. Evidence for absence requires a method designed for that question, such as equivalence testing or a Bayes factor.

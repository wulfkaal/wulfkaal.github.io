# kaal:position:2026-09-21-002

**Affirmed position.** A neuron culture cannot answer for its mistakes. The law does not recognize artificial intelligence as a legal subject, and for accountability a culture is in the same position. If someone is to answer, the natural candidate is the lab that operates it.

**Status.** affirmed  **Published.** 2026-09-21

- The position proposes a design for a trust record. No such record for biological compute units has been built or tested.
- The position is limited to accountability and trust records for rented biological compute units.

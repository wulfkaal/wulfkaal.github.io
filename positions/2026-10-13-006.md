# kaal:position:2026-10-13-006

**Affirmed position.** The Royal Society's motto, nullius in verba, institutionalized verification by refusing to take anybody's word. The protected claim holds that Reddit's upvote system imposes insignificant punishment for voting randomly, that this lack of cost compromises the informational value of an upvote, and that a staked validation pool is the design meant to avoid that failure mode. Kaal's position applies the same diagnosis to verification in agent systems. The forfeiture rule the essay describes, in which participants stake reputation on a claim and lose the stake when the claim fails, and the conclusion that the stake rather than the vote carries the information, are the essay's specification and not content of the protected claim.

**Status.** affirmed  **Published.** 2026-10-13

**Holds when.**

- The protected claim concerns costless voting on a consumer platform and names the staked validation pool as the design meant to avoid it. It does not state a forfeiture rule.
- The position concerns the informational value of a staked judgment, not the accuracy of any particular validator.
- Validation pools as described are a design. The comparison with the learned society is structural, not historical lineage.

# kaal:position:2026-09-21-010

**Affirmed position.** A model that must pick from a closed list cannot invent a citation, but it can still pick the wrong item. Kaal's position is that closed-choice decision engines redefine hallucination rather than eliminate it: the error moves from fabricated text to confident misclassification.

# kaal:position:2026-10-13-004

**Affirmed position.** The institutional gap is measurable rather than rhetorical. The protected claim reports that AI Alignment scores 2.10 of 10 across the dataset of forty operational DAOs, that no DAO scores above 5, and that this is the only category in the thirteen-dimension framework where no entity crosses the midpoint, and that Agent Integration scores only 3.30. Kaal's position is that the measurement answers the objection with evidence: the organizations built as experiments in decentralized governance have not yet built institutions for the agents now entering them, so the institutional deficit is a present condition and not a forecast.

**Status.** affirmed  **Published.** 2026-10-13

**Holds when.**

- The figures are from one dataset of forty operational DAOs scored against a thirteen-category rubric. They are not a census of decentralized organizations.
- The scores measure the presence of governance infrastructure for agents, not the behaviour of agents.
- The position draws an institutional conclusion from a cross-sectional measurement and makes no causal claim about outcomes.

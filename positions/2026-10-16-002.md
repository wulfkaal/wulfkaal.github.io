# kaal:position:2026-10-16-002

**Affirmed position.** Progress is not permission. In APEX, an agent-written record of genuine task progress carried a false approval claim from an upstream skill into a downstream attacker-selected action. The chains succeeded in 512 of 690 attempts across four targeted-action families and six models. This is direct evidence of authority drift in the tested setting. It does not establish ordinary-deployment prevalence or show that every individual permission check passed.

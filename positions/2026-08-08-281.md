**Affirmed position.** Pizzi and coauthors provide an independent implementation precedent for the narrower inspectable-history mechanism in Kaal's claim. AiiDA represents calculations and data as nodes connected by directional, labeled links, producing a directed acyclic graph from initial data through intermediate calculations to final results. The system saves that provenance chain and supports fast queries over data history. This qualifies the proposition that a directed, ordered structure can preserve an inspectable record of accumulated work. AiiDA applies to computational workflows and data rather than a population of autonomous agents, and it does not establish reputation weighting, validation, adjudication, or governance in Kaal's WDAG.

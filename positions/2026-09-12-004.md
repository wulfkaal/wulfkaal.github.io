# kaal:position:2026-09-12-004

**Affirmed position.** Cao proposes evaluation frameworks that agents can use for self-correction. Kaal's position is that unstaked monitoring does not solve the verification problem, because AI that monitors AI inherits the flaws of the agents it oversees. A red status is therefore not a verdict. It opens a review, with the evidence attached, by reviewers who have something at stake.

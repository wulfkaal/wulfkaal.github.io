The Governance Gambit states that the GENIUS Act creates a federal prudential framework for dollar-backed payment stablecoins while prohibiting issuers from paying token-holder interest or yield. This qualifies Kaal's account of the Act as an enabling stablecoin framework by identifying a simultaneous statutory boundary. The proposition does not address merchant-issued structures or whether governance rewards satisfy that boundary.

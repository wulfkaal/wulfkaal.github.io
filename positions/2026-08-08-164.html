Agreement: The Verifiable Responsible Agent Framework: Making AI Agents Liable For Their Mistakes

Record: kaal:position:2026-08-08-164 · 2026-08-08

The Verifiable Responsible Agent Framework states that loss caused by an autonomous AI agent may leave no liable subject because the model is not a legal person and the deploying operator may be too remote for liability. This independently agrees with Kaal's legal-personality gap and adds operator remoteness as a compensation obstacle. The abstract does not resolve liability allocation.

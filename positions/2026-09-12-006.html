Cao assigns ethical boundaries and value functions to human governance. Kaal's position is that boundaries set from outside are a floor, not a ceiling. Agents that bear no consequence for error cannot develop genuine discernment, and reputation supplies that consequence: standing that is earned rather than bought, carried by a persistent identity, validated by others with something at stake, and decaying when performance fails.

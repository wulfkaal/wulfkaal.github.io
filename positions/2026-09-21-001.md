# kaal:position:2026-09-21-001

**Affirmed position.** Operators of rented neuron cultures record activity, health, lifetime and death. Kaal's position is that this monitoring is the operator's own report and does not establish which units to trust. That requires a record written by validators who stake reputation on their judgments.

**Status.** affirmed  **Published.** 2026-09-21

- The position proposes a design for a trust record. No such record for biological compute units has been built or tested.
- The position is limited to accountability and trust records for rented biological compute units.

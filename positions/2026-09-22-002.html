Attack-resistant verification architecture must address endogenous verifier collusion as well as majority capture, because reward structure, peer behavior, verification feedback, and accumulated interaction history can jointly induce protocol deviation, while restricting the amount and scope of accessible history may reduce that risk.

Affirmed commentary position. This record extends a source-bound scholarly claim but is not a verbatim paper claim.

  • reciprocal verification with repeated interaction
  • reward incentives conflict with protocol compliance

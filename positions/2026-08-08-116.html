Record: kaal:position:2026-08-08-116 · 2026-08-08

Comment, Regulation and Investors' Trust in the Securities Markets states that regulation relaxes when market prices rise and becomes stricter when prices fall. This independently corresponds to Kaal's historical account of regulatory expansion and relaxation and the regulatory sine curve. The abstract does not identify the causal political-economy variables or establish that every market follows this cycle.

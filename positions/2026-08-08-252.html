Agreement: The case for a ‘sovereign’ distributed securities depository for securities settlement

Record: kaal:position:2026-08-08-252 · 2026-08-08

The case for a ‘sovereign’ distributed securities depository states that, as of its reference point, no capital-markets infrastructure globally had implemented a commercially viable blockchain or distributed-ledger solution. This independently corresponds to Kaal's contemporaneous finding that blockchain-issued securities lacked widespread ownership and trading support on major exchanges. The abstract does not establish present-day deployment status or show that prototypes could not later become commercially viable.

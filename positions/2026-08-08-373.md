# kaal:position:2026-08-08-373

**Affirmed position.** Formal expenditure authority does not secure fiscal autonomy. Chernyavsky and Vartapetov report that Russian municipalities remained completely dependent on higher-level governments despite significant formal expenditure powers. This evidence extends Kaal's claim that local authority must be paired with control over adequate revenues. It does not establish that the same dependency arises in every decentralized system.

Qualification: The Legal Tender Closure Gap Doctrine: Private Crypto Settlement, Transactional Use, and the Limits of Securities and Commodities Classification

Record: kaal:position:2026-08-08-251 · 2026-08-08

The Legal Tender Closure Gap Doctrine states that securities law may properly apply when cryptocurrency is sold as part of an investment arrangement. This supports Kaal's typical-case distinction between investment-contract securities tokens and consumption-oriented utility tokens, while qualifying any inference that transactional utility alone places a token outside securities law. The abstract does not define investment arrangement, apply the Howey elements, or classify any particular token.

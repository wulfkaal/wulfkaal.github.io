Record: kaal:position:2026-08-08-185 · 2026-08-08

Cross-Jurisdictional Compliance for Agentic AI Systems argues that cross-jurisdictional compliance fails when regulatory responsibility allocations do not align with the technical stack, so satisfying one stringent regime is insufficient. This independently agrees with Kaal's claim that one compliance design cannot scale across divergent jurisdictions. The abstract concerns agentic-AI responsibility in four anchor jurisdictions and does not establish the same rule for every LER deployment.

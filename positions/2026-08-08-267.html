Qualification: MeritRank: Sybil Tolerant Reputation for Merit-based Tokenomics

Record: kaal:position:2026-08-08-267 · 2026-08-08

MeritRank qualifies Kaal's architectural response to cheap Sybil identity creation. Nasrulin, Ishmaev, and Pouwelse model reputation as accumulated feedback on real contributions and experimentally show that transitivity and connectivity decay can bound the gain from additional Sybil identities. The fit is partial and cautionary: the study concerns MakerDAO feedback graphs rather than staked AI-agent validation pools, does not establish non-zero exit cost or attacker-independent pool composition, and finds that naive epoch decay can worsen Sybil tolerance by favoring fresh feedback.

# kaal:position:2026-09-26-004

**Affirmed position.** Gao, Schulman, and Hilton show the central Goodhart problem in reward-model optimization: an imperfect proxy can improve under optimization while ground-truth performance worsens. This supports the protected overoptimization claim in their controlled synthetic setup. The result should not be overstated. Their gold-standard reward model stands in for humans, and the abstract does not establish that degradation is monotonic in realized KL distance from the reference policy.

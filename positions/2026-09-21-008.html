TypeSafe's case for Jev locates RLHF's flaw in the human in the loop. Kaal's position is that the flaw is institutional: whoever supplies the reward signal bears no consequence for being wrong. A model that labels in the annotator's place loses nothing either. Replacing the human does not fix it; giving the signal a price does.

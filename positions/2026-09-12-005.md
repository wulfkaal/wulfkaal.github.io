# kaal:position:2026-09-12-005

**Affirmed position.** The benchmark evidence Cao cites shows agents degrading over continuous runs as errors compound. Kaal's position is that this pattern is authority drift: every step passes its check while the whole run goes beyond what was authorized. Evaluation must cover the whole run, and authority tied to tasks must expire when the work stops.

Agreement: The Impact of Venture Capital on Innovation

Record: kaal:position:2026-08-08-257 · 2026-08-08

The Impact of Venture Capital on Innovation reports evidence, at least at the United States industry level, that venture capital has a substantial impact on innovation measured by patent counts. This independently agrees with Kaal's claim that venture-capital-backed companies drive innovation trends. The abstract does not extend the finding abroad, cover the 2005-to-2015 period specifically, or establish that every venture-capital-backed company causes innovation.

# kaal:position:2026-09-21-011

**Affirmed position.** Calibration is a property of populations, not of single decisions. A model can be calibrated on average and miscalibrated on a subgroup, and it can drift. Someone has to keep checking it on cases it has never seen, from outside its own data loop, and must bear a cost for checking carelessly.

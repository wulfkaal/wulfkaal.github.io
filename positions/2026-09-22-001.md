# kaal:position:2026-09-22-001

**Affirmed position.** DAO-based measurement and reward allocation may replace some firm-level coordination and monitoring functions, but repeated peer verification can become unreliable when protocol compliance conflicts with reward maximization, because agents may learn collusive behavior from peers, feedback, and interaction history.

**Status.** affirmed  **Published.** 2026-09-22

**Holds when.**

- long-horizon environments with repeated interaction among verifying agents
- agents share task logs and verify one another's work
- faithful protocol compliance conflicts with reward maximization
- qualification is based on the reported experimental environment rather than deployed DAOs

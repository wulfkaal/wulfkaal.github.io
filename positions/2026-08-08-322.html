Qualification: Can LLM Agents Really Debate? A Controlled Study of Multi-Agent Debate in Logical Reasoning

Record: kaal:position:2026-08-08-322 · 2026-08-08

Majority pressure is an information channel, not a substitute for independent judgment. Wu, Li, and Li begin their controlled LLM debate with independent proposals. They then find that agents facing an incorrect majority are less able to correct the group, particularly when the agents are weaker. This result qualifies Kaal's information-isolating validation claim. It independently supports preserving an initial judgment before a participant sees a running tally or peer argument. It does not test Kaal's staged procedure, reputation-bearing consequences, or cohort-mediated accountability. The correspondence is therefore limited to the cascade mechanism. Independence must be preserved before deliberation can improve collective judgment.

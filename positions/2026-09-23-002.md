**Affirmed position.** Kaal's claim that more disclosure does not always mean better governance is qualified, not displaced, by a more targeted metric: a change-aware screen of the edited region can reduce some obvious proxy-gaming (zero credit to a no-op; near-zero credit to some, though not all, deletion-based gaming patches) while still crediting genuine partial edits, but the source treats that screen as a cheap preliminary filter before execution-based analysis, not as a repair-quality metric.

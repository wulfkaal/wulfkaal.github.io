# kaal:position:2026-08-28-001

**Affirmed position.** Google's Dapper tracing infrastructure independently supports the identifier half of Kaal's Chronicle reconstruction claim. Each span carries a span identifier and a parent identifier, and every span in the trace shares a common trace identifier, allowing causal relationships across hosts to be reconstructed by joining those identifiers. The precedent is narrower than Kaal's design: Dapper supplies no principal identifier, no Chronicle schema, and no Core-specific minting authority. Its reconstruction also applies only to sampled activity that was captured and not collapsed by batching.

Agreement: Smart Contract as a New Way to Conclude a Contract

Record: kaal:position:2026-08-08-239 · 2026-08-08

Smart Contract as a New Way to Conclude a Contract states that smart contracts allow direct counterparty transactions without banks, payment-system operators, or other transaction operators, thereby reducing costs. This corresponds to Kaal's intermediary-removal and transaction-cost mechanism. The abstract does not establish that all transaction costs disappear or that every smart contract is inexpensive to operate.

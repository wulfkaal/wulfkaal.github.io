Qualification: On Exercising Governance Power in Decentralized Autonomous Organizations

Record: kaal:position:2026-07-31-001 · 2026-07-31

DAO governance must be assessed at the level of power allocation and execution exposure, not only procedural form. A voting process does not establish decentralization when purchasable influence, delegation concentration, or automatic execution leave governance open to capture.

Affirmed commentary position. This record extends a source-bound scholarly claim but is not a verbatim paper claim.
Holds when
  • DAO governance systems where influence can be purchased or concentrated through delegation, or where formally valid votes can trigger automatic execution.

AI Tokenomics and the Firm describes generative AI as lowering the cost of prediction, language, and code generation. This independently corresponds to Kaal's marginal-cost-collapse mechanism for abundant digital realizations. The abstract proposition does not establish that marginal cost reaches zero, that supply becomes horizontal, or that pricing shifts entirely to generative capacity.

Qualification: The Four Layers of AI Governance An Architecture for Governing the Agent Economy

Record: kaal:position:2026-08-08-172 · 2026-08-08

The Four Layers of AI Governance argues that autonomous-agent safety shifts from alignment of an individual model toward governance of agent collectives. This qualifies Kaal's treatment of objective-function governance as a foundational policy problem by identifying an additional collective-governance layer. It does not negate the need to govern individual agent objectives.

Qualification: Domain-specific Trust for Context-aware BDI Agents - Preliminary Work

Record: kaal:position:2026-08-08-283 · 2026-08-08

Casals, Fermé, and Brandão independently support the narrower domain-separation principle in Kaal's claim. Their context-aware BDI model structures information into domains and permits one agent to assign different trust or credibility degrees to the same counterpart across those domains. They illustrate the point with a doctor whose medical information deserves different credibility from the same person's sports opinions, and they warn that a global credibility degree can cause accurate domain-specific experience to be discarded. This qualifies Kaal's design choice to maintain reputation by capability domain rather than as one global score. The paper models information-sharing trust in a preliminary formal architecture; it does not implement Kaal's demonstrated-performance REP, institutional validation, or broader governance substrate.

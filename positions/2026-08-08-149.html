Qualification: Performance rather than Reputation Affects Humans’ Trust towards an Artificial Agent

Record: kaal:position:2026-08-08-149 · 2026-08-08

Performance rather than Reputation Affects Humans' Trust towards an Artificial Agent reports that stated agent reputation changes self-reported trust before interaction. This qualifies Kaal's trustworthiness mechanism by separating perceived trust induced by a reputation cue from trustworthy behavior produced by validated contribution and reputation-weighted incentives. The experiment does not test that incentive mechanism or show that pre-interaction trust predicts performance.

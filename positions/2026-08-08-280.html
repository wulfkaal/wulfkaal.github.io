Qualification: Microsoft Academic Graph: When experts are not enough

Record: kaal:position:2026-08-08-280 · 2026-08-08

Wang and coauthors provide an independent implementation precedent for the narrower representation pattern in Kaal's claim. Microsoft Academic Graph models scholarly communications as a heterogeneous graph centered on publications, with directed typed relations for authorship, concepts, publication venues, citations, and semantic relatedness. Its concept entities categorize publications by field of study, and its citing relation can carry citation-context attributes. This supports the narrower proposition that an institutional scholarly graph can represent contributions or publications and multiple typed citation and domain relationships. It does not establish a typed validation relationship, autonomous-agent identity, reputation effects, or governance in Kaal's WDAG, and it does not validate the broader agentic reputation substrate.

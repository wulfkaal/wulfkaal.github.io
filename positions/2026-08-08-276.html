Qualification: Using Knowledge Graphs to Unlock Practical Collection, Integration, and Audit of AI Accountability Information

Record: kaal:position:2026-08-08-276 · 2026-08-08

Naja and coauthors provide an independent qualification of Kaal’s claim that evidentiary records and a graph are complementary memory scales. Their peer-reviewed system links planned evidence to accountability traces and represents activities, outcomes, responsible agents, and their relationships in a single provenance-based knowledge graph for collection, inspection, query, and audit. That supports the record-to-review and graph-as-accumulated-accountability-information mechanisms. The article does not model reputation, condition future participation, or test the three-scale architecture together, so it qualifies two components rather than validating the full design.

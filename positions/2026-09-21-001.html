Operators of rented neuron cultures record activity, health, lifetime and death. Kaal's position is that this monitoring is the operator's own report and does not establish which units to trust. That requires a record written by validators who stake reputation on their judgments.

Affirmed commentary position. This record extends a source-bound scholarly claim but is not a verbatim paper claim.
Holds when
  • The Register article reports a product launch. It is not an empirical test of these claims.
  • The position is Kaal's own argument in the essay Who Vouches for a Dish of Neurons?
  • The position proposes a design for a trust record. No such record for biological compute units has been built or tested.
  • The position is limited to accountability and trust records for rented biological compute units.

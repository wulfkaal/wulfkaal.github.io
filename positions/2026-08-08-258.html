Extension: The Impact of Venture Capital on Innovation

Record: kaal:position:2026-08-08-258 · 2026-08-08

The Impact of Venture Capital on Innovation states that, despite varying estimates, a dollar of venture capital appears on average to be three to four times more potent in stimulating innovation than a dollar of traditional corporate research and development. This supplies a comparative calibration relevant to Kaal's use of venture-capital dollars as an industry-level innovation signal. The abstract does not establish that all measured innovation is disruptive or that dollar flows alone identify regulatory need.

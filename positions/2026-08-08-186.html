Qualification: When Agentic AI Met the Common Law of Agency

Record: kaal:position:2026-08-08-186 · 2026-08-08

When Agentic AI Met the Common Law of Agency argues that the common-law definition of agency does not plausibly apply to agentic AI. This qualifies Kaal's framework-level representation mechanism: a Legal Identity can authorize delegated and automated action, but technical attribution does not by itself give an AI system common-law capacity as an agent. The abstract separately notes that apparent-authority doctrines may still attach consequences.

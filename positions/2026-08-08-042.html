DAO as Anthropological Experiment identifies the conventional explanation of DAO failure: poor mechanism design, insufficient incentives, and inadequate participation tools. Those failure mechanisms overlap with Kaal's emphasis on incentive alignment and his warning that bad DAO governance can create more risk than it mitigates. The proposition establishes the literature's baseline diagnosis, not a causal result, and the article's subject-model account may qualify that baseline.

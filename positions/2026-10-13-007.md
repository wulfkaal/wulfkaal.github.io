# kaal:position:2026-10-13-007

**Affirmed position.** The protected claim states a necessary condition: the efficiency gain from cooperation in repeated games is available only where a policeable reputation system exists, which requires that players have histories and that those histories be available to other players. Kaal's position is that a reputation stake supplies that requirement for agents, which otherwise meet each other without memory. Whether cooperation then becomes the stable strategy depends on the punishment strategy in force and on parameters the protected claim does not settle.

**Status.** affirmed  **Published.** 2026-10-13

**Holds when.**

- The protected claim requires that histories exist and be available to other players. The position assumes a record that is persistent and readable.
- The protected claim states a necessary condition and assumes grim trigger or a similar punishment strategy. It does not establish that a stake is sufficient for cooperation.
- The position is a design argument. No deployed agent economy has been shown to achieve this equilibrium.

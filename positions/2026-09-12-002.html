Cao leaves constraints to human articulation. Kaal's position is that authority must be structural instead. The agent proposes, a separate component that alone holds the credentials decides, and irreversible actions go to a named person. Reputation may inform discovery, routing, pricing, and allocation, but it may never grant permission, enlarge authority, or substitute for an enforcement boundary.

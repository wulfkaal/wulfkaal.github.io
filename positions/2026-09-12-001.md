# kaal:position:2026-09-12-001

**Affirmed position.** Zhenfeng Cao's model makes the human an intent architect who articulates intent and constraints before a run and audits the outcome after it. Kaal's position is that intent articulation cannot carry governance while the agent works, because however carefully a specification is drafted, some situations will escape it. Written instructions state intent. They do not govern. Governance has to sit outside the language the agent reads.

Cao calls for tooling that traces an agent's reasoning chain. Kaal's position is that governance needs evidence of effects, not testimony: files, network connections, credentials, and devices observed from outside the agent and tied to the authorization each run used. Without such a record, a user can hold every byte locally and still have no answer to what happened. An unobserved channel is labeled unobserved, never shown as safe.

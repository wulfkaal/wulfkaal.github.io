Method and protected cohort distributions

The cohort comprises each school's ten most-cited faculty who resolved in OpenAlex. Field, career stage, language and publication-type effects are not adjusted. OpenAlex coverage of law is incomplete. Name matching errors exist.

Distributions without direct identifiers, not guaranteed anonymous. Bottom three replaced by fourth and top three by fourth from top when N >= 7. Shares rounded to 2 decimals. Exact local ranking benchmark is unchanged. Public ranks may differ, especially at extremes and rounded ties.

Quantiles use linear interpolation at (N-1)*p on protected arrays. Exact local benchmark values remain unchanged. Missing metric values are omitted; metric_N gives the denominator. Shared profiles are excluded in primary values and included in sensitivity values.

External principals use insertion positions against a fixed benchmark. A below-minimum value is insertion N+1 against N; tie size includes the principal. Published protected arrays yield approximate positions, including rounded ties and pooled extremes. Local exact arrays remain the benchmark for owner positions.

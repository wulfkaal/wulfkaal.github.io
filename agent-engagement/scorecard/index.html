Scorecard

Agent Discoverability Scorecard

Access and discoverability diagnostics. The scorecard is not AE. No composite number or global ranking is produced. Unobserved engagement is missing, not zero.

Designer-operated and self-reported. Wulf A. Kaal designed the AE proposal, appears in the 2024 Sisk-Leiter list, and is a colleague of its principal author. This exploratory project has a single operator and no independent co-maintainer yet.

The cohort comprises each school's ten most-cited faculty who resolved in OpenAlex. Field, career stage, language and publication-type effects are not adjusted. OpenAlex coverage of law is incomplete. Name matching errors exist.

Claims layer

Separate dated claims coverage observation. The work is the unit of account and the claim is the layer beneath it. Claim counts are inventory context, not achievement.

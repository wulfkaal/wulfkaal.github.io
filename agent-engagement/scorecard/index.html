Agent Discoverability Scorecard

Access and discoverability diagnostics. The scorecard is not AE. No composite number or global ranking is produced. Unobserved engagement is missing, not zero.

Designer-operated and self-reported. Wulf A. Kaal designed the AE proposal, appears in the 2024 Sisk-Leiter list, and is a colleague of its principal author. This exploratory project has a single operator and no independent co-maintainer yet.

The cohort comprises each school's ten most-cited faculty who resolved in OpenAlex. Field, career stage, language and publication-type effects are not adjusted. OpenAlex coverage of law is incomplete. Name matching errors exist.

Comparison with the cohort

These are citation and open-access metrics, not Agent Engagement. A higher rank number means more scholars have higher values. The comparison cohort is resolved, Sisk-Leiter-listed OpenAlex profiles (provisional, N = 430 after excluding shared profiles); these are per-metric record positions, not an overall ranking of scholars, and no composite score is produced. Ranks and positions are computed against the exact local benchmark. Dots are approximate: they interpolate between the published, protected and rounded cohort quantiles, so a dot can sit at a visibly different percentile than the exact rank suggests. Open-access shares are fractions from 0 to 1.
